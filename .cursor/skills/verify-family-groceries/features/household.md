# Household

A list belongs to a household. A new user goes through onboarding (`features/onboarding.md`): signed out, the guided start ends with the account and creates the household from its answers; signed in without a household, "Willkommen bei Snacklist" offers "Haushalt erstellen" (the same guided start from "Mit wem") and "Einladung annehmen". Under Mehr, "Leute einladen" opens the Einladungen page (make an invite, see the open ones, revoke one), and the household sheet's "Mit Code beitreten" (the chip top left of the list) opens the join screen (titled "Haushalt beitreten" since job 57, route `/join`); its back arrow and "Abbrechen" return to the list. More has no join row since job 206. Since job 169b a user can be in several households (up to 20 created or joined, job 200): joining adds one, the household chip top left of the list switches between them, and More's household row opens the household page (rename, members, the default for Siri, Alexa & KI, leave).

## Sub-features

- `hh-create` creates a household (the guided start's setup, job 168b) with a name (prefilled from "Mit wem", "Unser Haushalt" without an answer, 1-80 characters), a kind (`households.kind`: partner, family, flat, club, solo, other, or null when skipped), the stores picked from eleven common ones (the last is „Baumarkt“, job 151) or typed (nothing preselected; job 58) or none ("Überspringen"), and the items from "Was fehlt gerade?". The 11 categories with "Sonstiges" are always seeded. A retry or an app kill makes one household and adds each item once.
- `hh-invite` makes an invite code (8 characters from A-Z without I and O, and 2-9). A person invite is valid for 7 days and works once: after one person joins with it, it is used up. The guided start's invite step and More's Einladungen show it with its link (`APP_URL/join/<code>`), "gültig bis …", "Link kopieren", "Code kopieren", and "Teilen" (the share sheet in the iOS, Android and macOS apps; Web Share where the browser has it). The Linux fg client shows neither "Teilen" (`invite-share` counts 0) nor the link (no `APP_URL`), only the code.
- `hh-group-link` owners and admins can also make a group link (`invite-group`, `invite-group-create`): valid 30 days, up to 50 people, its uses shown (`invite-group-uses`), copy code or link, share, renew and revoke (`invite-group-revoke`), `invite-group-full` once full. Only owners and admins manage it.
- `hh-invite-step` the guided start's invite step makes several invites (`invite-partner`, then `invite-another`), one card each, and marks a card `invite-joined` ("<Name> ist beigetreten") as soon as someone joins with it, live. Recipe in `features/onboarding.md` (`ob-invite`, `ob-joined`).
- `hh-invites-list` lists the household's open invites under Mehr, Leute einladen (`/more/invites`). Any member revokes a person invite („Widerrufen“); its link and code stop at once. The group link is revoked by an owner or admin only. Used, revoked and expired invites are not listed.
- `hh-join` joins with a code (typed at `/join`, or from a link `/join/<code>`): the screen first shows the household's name ("Einladung in den Haushalt"), then "Haushalt beitreten". Lowercase, spaces and dashes in the code are accepted.
- `hh-join-menu` joins one more household from More or a link while in one (job 169b): no warning, nothing is left; after the join the list shows the joined household and the user keeps the others. Before job 169b this moved the user (warning "Du bist schon in einem Haushalt"); that screen is gone. A code or link of a household the user is in already shows that household (`accept_invite` answers `member` with its id and uses nothing).
- `hh-switcher` the chip `household-chip` top left of the list (the household's name and an expand icon) opens a sheet (`household-sheet`): one row per household (`household-row-<id>`: name, "N Mitglieder · M offen", `household-current` check on the shown one), `household-new` "Neuer Haushalt" (`/households/new`: the create step, then Step 3 for the new household), `household-join` "Mit Code beitreten" (`/join`), and `household-default-row` "Siri, Alexa & KI nutzen: <name>", which opens the picker `household-default-picker`. A row switches the list at once, also offline; the device opens the household shown last on the next start. Changes queued offline in a household are still sent after switching away and after a restart (job 169b, `test/list/hidden_queue_test.dart`).
- `hh-page` More → the household's name (`more-household`) opens `/more/household`: `household-rename` (dialog, 1-80 characters), the members (`household-members`, "<name> (du)" for oneself), "Als Standard nutzen" (`household-default`) or the check "Siri, Alexa und KI-Assistenten nutzen diesen Haushalt.", then (job 207, flag `default-list-voice-target`, two lists or more) `household-default-list` "Liste für Siri, Alexa & KI" with the member's default list (`default_list`), a tap opens a menu of the lists (`default-list-<id>`) that calls `set_default_list`; the sheet's footer has the same for the default household (`household-default-list-row`, "Liste: <list>"), and `household-leave` "Haushalt verlassen" with a confirm dialog (others stay: they keep the list; last member: deleted, or with Lifetime archived when `HOUSEHOLD_ARCHIVE` is on; a renewing yearly plan asks first). Leaving the shown household shows the default one; leaving the last one goes to onboarding. The leave dialog reads the members when it opens.
- `hh-page-more` the household page also holds the members with their roles and an actions menu (`member-actions-<user id>`; removing asks in `member-remove-dialog`, `member-remove-confirm`), the lists (`household-lists`, „Neue Liste“ `list-new`), join approval (`join-approval-switch`, owners and admins), „Einladen“ (`household-invite`) and, only with the prices flag (off), the currency (`household-currency`).
- `hh-invalid-code` answers made-up, expired, revoked and used codes with the same "Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen." The field keeps the code.
- `hh-rate-limit`: after 10 failed codes in an hour, every code (a good one too) gets "Zu viele Versuche. Bitte später noch einmal." until the hour has passed.
- `hh-isolation` keeps one household's items invisible to members of another.
- `hh-members` loads the members' display names with the list, so the title can name who added what is new; a new member's name loads with their first item (recipe in `features/live-sync.md`, `sync-new`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Start the app signed out and tap „Los geht's“: the guided start creates the household after the account.
- Or sign up or sign in as a user without a household. After the analytics question (builds with a key), "Willkommen bei Snacklist" offers "Haushalt erstellen" and "Einladung annehmen".
- Tap "Mehr" in the tab bar, then "Leute einladen" (the second group; More has no section labels since job 85). To join, tap the household chip top left of the list, then "Mit Code beitreten".
- Open an invite link (`/join/<code>`). The web build opens it, also before sign-in (the code is kept on the device through sign-in). The release phone apps claim `/join/*` and the other app links of `docs/DEEP_LINKS.md` (`/more/invites` included; universal links, App Links) on `app.snacklist.app` and `app.hummlist.com`. Both hosts serve the `release` branch's `.well-known` files, which list Snacklist from the first release upload after #345. Snacklist builds share invites as `https://app.snacklist.app/join/<code>` (`APP_URL`). The web side of every link: verify-snacklist `features/app-links.md`. The fg debug build sets no `APP_URL`, so its invites show the code only.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions:

- Baseline from `features/README.md`. Peter is signed in on the list.
- The lea client hosts a fresh user without a household: run `fg logout --client lea`, then `fg type --key login-email --input hh-$(date +%s)@fg.test --client lea`, `fg type --key login-password --input test-password-123 --client lea`, `fg tap --text Registrieren --client lea` and `fg wait --key welcome --client lea` ("Willkommen"; the guided start's `onboarding-who` shows only after `onboarding-create`). If lea's client shows `start-screen` instead of the sign-in, `fg tap --key start-log-in --client lea` before typing.

- **Invite.** Run `fg tap --key tab-more`, `fg wait --key more-invite`, `fg tap --key more-invite`, `fg wait --key invite-new`, `fg tap --key invite-new` and `fg wait --key invite-copy-code`. Read the code with `fg find --type Text | jq -r '[.elements[].text | select(test("^[A-HJ-NP-Z2-9]{8}$"))][0]'`. `fg tap --key invite-copy-code` shows the snackbar "Code kopiert". `fg back`, then `fg tap --key tab-list`.
- **Join from Willkommen.** Run `fg tap --key onboarding-join --client lea`, `fg type --key invite-code --input <code> --client lea` (`abcd-efgh` for `ABCDEFGH` works too), `fg tap --key invite-check --client lea`, `fg wait --text "Familie Test" --client lea` (the preview) and `fg tap --key join-confirm --client lea`. `fg wait --key add-item-field --client lea` succeeds; check membership in the database: `fg db query "select u.email, m.role from household_members m join auth.users u on u.id = m.user_id join households h on h.id = m.household_id where h.name = 'Familie Test'"` lists the new user as `member`. The invite is used up: `fg db query "select used_at is not null as used from invites where code = '<code>'"`.
- **Invalid code.** On the join screen, run `fg type --key invite-code --input ZZZZ2345 --client lea` and `fg tap --key invite-check --client lea`. "Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen." shows under the field, which `fg find` and `fg wait` don't see: prove it with `fg screenshot --name hh-invalid-code --client lea`. The same code used a second time gives the same answer.
- **Create.** On "Willkommen" (another fresh user), run `fg tap --key onboarding-create --client lea`, `fg wait --key onboarding-who --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-skip --client lea`, `fg wait --key household-name --client lea`, `fg type --key household-name --input "WG Test" --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea`, `fg wait --key onboarding-stores --client lea`, optionally a store chip (`fg tap --key onboarding-store-REWE --client lea`) then `onboarding-next` (or `onboarding-skip` for no stores), `fg wait --key onboarding-need --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea` and `fg wait --key invite-later --client lea` (the invite step). `fg tap --key invite-later --client lea`, then `fg wait --key add-item-field --client lea`. `fg db query "select count(*) from categories c join households h on h.id = c.household_id where h.name = 'WG Test'"` returns 11; the stores query returns the picked stores (none if nothing was picked or after Skip).
- **Revoke.** Under Mehr, Leute einladen, tap `invite-revoke` on a card. The card goes (`fg wait --key invite-revoke --gone` with one open invite) and no snackbar shows (`fg find --contains "Link und Code"` counts 0). Typed on the join screen of a user outside the household, the code then shows "Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen." (screenshot, as in **Invalid code**). A member of the inviting household gets no error: the membership check comes first, and the app goes back to the list.
- **Join from the household sheet.** As a member of another household, run `fg tap --key household-chip --client lea`, `fg wait --key household-join --client lea`, `fg tap --key household-join --client lea` (route `/join`; More has no join row since job 206), `fg wait --key invite-code --client lea`, `fg type --key invite-code --input <code> --client lea` and `fg tap --key invite-check --client lea`. Since job 169b there is no warning. `fg tap --key join-confirm --client lea`, then `fg wait --key add-item-field --client lea`. The membership query above lists the user in Familie Test, and the user is still in their own household too (not driven yet on the job 169b branch: written from the widget tests).
- **Switch.** `fg tap --key household-chip`, `fg wait --key household-sheet`, `fg tap --key household-row-<id>`; the chip shows the other name and the list is that household's (not driven yet, job 169b).
- **Isolation.** With the fresh user in its own household (after **Create**), add an item there: `fg type --key add-item-field --input Xylophon-WG --submit --client lea` and `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Xylophon-WG" --client lea`. Then `fg db query "select name from items where name = 'Xylophon-WG'" --as peter` returns no rows, while the same query without `--as` returns one. The pgTAP suite (`supabase/tests/rls.test.sql`) covers writes.
- **Proof.** Screenshot the invite card, the join screen with the household name and the list after joining. Add the `household_members` query output.

## Gotchas

- `hh-join-menu` crashed on main until job 04's fix (2026-10-23): a red error screen after "Beitreten" in the old join dialog (gone since job 22). If it comes back, `fg logs --client lea --grep EXCEPTION` names the cause.
- The release web build (the e2e tests, Vercel) strips Flutter's debug asserts, so a controller disposed too early ("TextEditingController was used after being disposed", `_dependents.isEmpty`) never shows there. Only the debug app (fg) and the widget tests (`app/test/household/onboarding_test.dart`) catch that class of bug.
- After a join (or creating a household) the list must not show the offline indicator ("Offline: …"). Until job 2b it stayed there until the first write: two overlapping resyncs shared one map and one threw. Check with `fg wait --contains Offline --gone --timeout 5 --client lea` after `fg wait --key add-item-field`.
- `--as peter` and `--as lea` mean the seeded users, not whoever is signed in on the client of that name.
- A failed `fg tap` or `fg find` leaves an "EXCEPTION CAUGHT BY FLUTTER FRAMEWORK … ext.flutter.marionette" block in the app log. Skip those when you read `fg logs --grep EXCEPTION` for an app error.
- These recipes run on fg's flag-off app: the household chip and its sheet, and More's household and invite rows. Users (`list-declutter` full) have the switcher sheet (`switcher-sheet`) instead and no household or invite rows in More (verify-snacklist `features/household-invites.md`).
- Users from these recipes stay members of "Familie Test" until `fg up --fresh`. Recipes that count its members (`journeys/account-deletion.sh`) compare with the count before their own join.
- Dialogs animate open. Tap inside one only after `fg wait` for its text or field, or the tap can miss.
- Joining a household adds it beside the user's others (`hh-join-menu`); nothing is left. Never run a join with the seeded lea or peter unless the recipe restores them (`fg up --fresh`): they stay members of the joined household.
- The invite card lists the code as plain text below the label "Code". Pick it by its pattern, not by position.
- Labels on buttons ("Haushalt beitreten", "Weiter") are invisible to `fg find`, but `fg tap --text` reaches them; the keys above are steadier.
- Person invites work once (job 22). A recipe that joins two users needs two invites, or a group link.
- Ten failed codes in an hour lock that user out of joining for the hour (`invite_attempts`). Reset in a recipe with `fg db query "delete from invite_attempts" --write`.
- Fresh sign-up users accumulate across runs. Use a timestamp in the email to stay unique.
