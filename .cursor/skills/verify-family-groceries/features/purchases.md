# Snacklist Premium (in-app purchase)

Mehr → Snacklist Premium sells Premium to the signed-in user (per account since job 185, in every household they are in) on iOS and Android through RevenueCat, as a yearly plan (`com.snacklist.app.yearly`, auto-renewing) or Lifetime (`com.snacklist.app.lifetime`, one-time; the server also records Hummlist's `com.hummlist.app.*` ids until that app retires, job 174), both the entitlement `premium` (job 49). The page shows each plan with the price the store itself returns (StoreKit or Play Billing, read once when the page opens, never RevenueCat), "inkl. MwSt." only in euros, the yearly plan's disclosures, Restore, the terms and the privacy policy. RevenueCat's SDK starts only on a tap on Buy or Restore (job 47, H-67). The buyer's user id is RevenueCat's App User ID (`Purchases.logIn`, and the fake store sends the user id); RevenueCat calls the `purchases` function's webhook, which binds the purchase to that user (`purchases.bound_user`) and writes `user_entitlements`, which only the buyer reads. The other members of their households see none of it. Older builds and RevenueCat promotional grants on a household customer keep the household path (`bound_household`, `entitlements`, every member). Premium is active at read time (Lifetime or a RevenueCat promotional grant without end; else the yearly plan or promotional grant that ends last, if its end lies ahead). With an active yearly plan only Lifetime is offered (with a warning first while the plan renews); a lapsed plan brings both back. Nothing is gated by it yet. The offer needs the build define `REVENUECAT_PURCHASES` and the server switch `premium_offer` (both off by default: "Snacklist Premium ist bald verfügbar."). The web never sells. Since job 57 the page opens on a sticker card titled "Snacklist Premium" with the feature list, the same card as Zugänge's.

## Sub-features

- `iap-offer` (PW-P4 „Haushalt“, job 87): under the hero (its heading „Snacklist Premium für dich“ only here, with the switch on and a build that sells, Legal Review N-68) two tiles side by side, yearly left and picked (`premium-card-yearly`: `premium-price-yearly` "24,99 €", `premium-period-yearly` "pro Jahr, inkl. MwSt."; `premium-card-lifetime`: "149,99 €", "einmalig, inkl. MwSt."), `premium-household` („Premium gilt dann für dein Konto, in jedem Haushalt, in dem du Mitglied bist.“), one Buy for the picked plan (`premium-buy-yearly`, or `premium-buy-lifetime` after `fg tap --key premium-card-lifetime`), the picked plan's `premium-terms-<plan>`, `lifetime-restore`, `premium-terms`, `premium-privacy`; only for a household known to have no premium, with the switch on and a price read. A storefront without a Lifetime price shows the yearly tile alone, full width.
- `iap-no-sdk-before-tap` (H-67): no RevenueCat SDK call before a tap on Buy or Restore, one store price read per page opening. Proven by `app/test/purchases/no_sdk_before_tap_test.dart` (the real repository over the SDK's mocked channel). On the web, `e2e/tests/purchases.spec.ts` and `premium-yearly.spec.ts` check no request goes to a RevenueCat host.
- `iap-prices`: the store's price exactly as returned (EUR with VAT line; USD, GBP, CHF without); a plan the store doesn't return is hidden (yearly-only storefronts); no price read: `premium-unavailable`, no Buy. Proven in `premium_screen_test.dart` and `store_prices_test.dart` (fakes; the native query compiles in CI's android and ios gates).
- `iap-switch`: `premium_offer` off: nothing under the hero (job 87: no "bald verfügbar"), no card, no price query. Unreadable (offline): `premium-unavailable` ("gerade nicht verfügbar"), no price query (job 67).
- `iap-buy` buys, shows `lifetime-unlocking`, then the status tile `lifetime-unlocked` ("Snacklist Premium aktiv (jährlich, verlängert sich am …)" or "Snacklist Premium Lifetime"). After a minute without the unlock: `lifetime-unlock-delayed`.
- `iap-yearly-status`: renewing, "läuft am … aus" (auto-renew off), Lifetime, a promotional grant: by the grant, not the switch (N-70): ends before 2100 "In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet", no end or to 2100-01-01 "Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)"; always with `premium-beta-body` and `premium-beta-privacy` (Legal Review final texts, job 87); `premium-manage` ("Abo verwalten oder kündigen") while a yearly plan is active; on the web `premium-manage-web` with both store pages.
- `iap-billing-issue`: only the subscriber sees `premium-billing-issue`; other members see only the end date. The `CANCELLATION` (`BILLING_ERROR`) RevenueCat sends next to it keeps the grace end, in either order (job 67).
- `iap-lifetime-while-yearly`: Buy Lifetime while a plan renews opens `premium-lifetime-warning` (`premium-lifetime-warning-manage`, `-cancel`, `-buy`); afterwards the page shows `premium-lifetime-while-yearly`. The SDK starts only on `premium-lifetime-warning-buy`.
- `iap-two-yearly`: `premium-two-yearly` with the manage row.
- `iap-billing-continues` (H-85): before account deletion, a join that leaves the household (plain, delete, archive) and re-enabling an archived household: `premium-billing-continues` for the subscriber (`premium-billing-manage`, `premium-billing-continue`), `premium-billing-other` for other members of a billed household (`premium-billing-cancel`).
- `iap-partner` a household's unlock (an older build's purchase or a promotional grant on the household customer) shows on every member's client live, without a Buy button, and lists the More row `more-lifetime` live (no detail line since job 85). A purchase in a current build is the buyer's only (job 185): the other members keep the offer.
- `iap-checking` shows `lifetime-checking` and no card while the household's rows are unknown.
- `iap-pending` shows `lifetime-pending` for Ask to Buy or a Play pending payment, until the unlock arrives.
- `iap-restore-lapsed`: Restore with only a lapsed plan says nothing to restore, and Buy stays (job 67; `revenuecat_repository_test.dart`).
- `iap-states` after a tap on Buy: `lifetime-canceled`, `lifetime-error`, `lifetime-unavailable` (product missing), `lifetime-offline`.
- `iap-elsewhere`: `lifetime-used-elsewhere` with the yearly hint (the store account pays a plan for another household).
- `iap-twice`: `lifetime-bought-twice` for two Lifetime purchases; `lifetime-refund-help` opens the refund help (`refund-help`, H-76 texts plus the yearly note).
- `iap-refund`: a refund (`CANCELLATION` with `CUSTOMER_SUPPORT`) locks that purchase; a yearly cancellation without refund keeps premium to the end.
- `iap-join`, `iap-archive`: as in job 43/47 (`join-target-unlocked`, `join-archive`, `archived-households`; "unlocked" now means "has premium now").
- `iap-promotional` (testers, reviewers): a grant on a user's own customer (the beta's lifetime thanks, job 371: `scripts/iap/beta-lifetime.ts`, synced by `promotional_premium_sync_user`) lands in `user_entitlements` for that user only; a RevenueCat promotional grant of `premium` for the household (dashboard, or `scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant --household <id> --apply`, until 2100-01-01) is read from RevenueCat's API when its webhook arrives (or by `promotional.ts reconcile --household <id> --apply`) and stored as an `entitlements` row with `premium_source` `promotional`; the household counts as internal in analytics. The custom tester list and `scripts/iap/testers.ts` are gone. Locally: `fg db query --write "select public.promotional_premium_sync('<household>', true, null)"` (or `'2100-01-01'` instead of `null`), then `lifetime-unlocked` shows the beta line (switch `premium_offer` off) or the gift line (on) on every member's client and `more-lifetime` reads "Freigeschaltet".
- `iap-web`: no buy or restore button; `lifetime-web` ("Snacklist Premium kaufst du in der iPhone- oder Android-App. …") only once `premium_offer` is on.

- `iap-integration-lock` (job 50): the lock in Zugänge. Every build since job 328 shows it exactly while the server switch `integration_lock` is on and the user is known to have no premium (neither the household's grant nor their own plan; review 0 of job 328 removed the flag in front of it): `integration-premium` with what is included, `integration-buy` (opens `/more/lifetime?from=integrations`) where the build sells and `premium_offer` is on, else `integration-soon` ("Bald verfügbar.") or on the web `integration-in-the-apps`; tokens and apps show `access-paused`. The user's own purchase removes the card within one sync; another member's does not (per-user Premium, job 185). `paywall_viewed` (`entry_point` `more` or `integrations`) goes out when the page shows a plan with its price. Drive: `fg db query --write "update app_switches set enabled = true where key in ('integration_lock','premium_offer')"`, `fg tap --text Mehr`, `fg tap --key more-access`, `fg wait --key integration-premium`, `fg tap --key integration-buy`, `fg tap --key premium-buy-yearly` (yearly is the picked tile); lea still sees `integration-premium` (her own Premium is what counts). Native screenshots: `SCREENSHOT_DIR=/tmp/shots flutter test --update-goldens test/settings/integration_lock_screenshots_test.dart`.
## How to get to it (user POV)

- Mehr → Snacklist Premium (`more-lifetime`, below Konto) → `/more/lifetime`. Only signed in and in a household (`authRedirect`). The row is listed only with `app_switches.premium_offer` on (and a build that sells: the fake store counts) or for a household that has Premium (job 64); otherwise open `/more/lifetime` directly.
- On a phone RevenueCat's SDK runs the store sheet and the native channel reads prices. Locally there is no store and no RevenueCat: fg builds carry the fake store (`FAKE_STORE`), whose prices are the launch prices in euros and whose purchases go to the local stack's `POST /purchases/test/event` and the real claim route.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions:

- Run `scripts/iap-local-env.sh` before `fg up` (the webhook and `test/event` answer 503 without it; a running stack loads it only after `fg down` and `fg up`).
- Turn the offer switch on: `fg db query --write "update app_switches set enabled = true where key = 'premium_offer'"` (off: the hero only).
- `FG_FAKE_STORE` at build time: `ok` (default), `pending`, `cancel`, `error`, `unavailable`, `offline`. On the Hetzner runner use `FG_SURFACE=linux`.

- **Offer.** On peter: `fg tap --key tab-more`, `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-lifetime`, `fg tap --key more-lifetime`, `fg wait --key premium-card-yearly`; `premium-card-lifetime` counts 1; `fg screenshot --name iap-offer`.
- **Buy yearly.** `fg tap --key premium-buy-yearly`, `fg wait --key lifetime-unlocked`. `fg db query --as peter "select premium_source, expires_at, auto_renew from user_entitlements"` shows `yearly`, a year ahead, `true`. `fg db query "select plan, expires_at, auto_renew, billing_issue from purchases order by verified_at desc limit 1"`.
- **Partner keeps the offer.** Premium is the buyer's (job 185): on lea before and after peter's buy, `premium-buy-yearly` and `premium-card-lifetime` both stay (count 1), and `fg db query --as lea "select count(*) from user_entitlements"` returns 0.
- **Lifecycle.** Post to the local stand-in (ids from `fg db query "select bound_user, transaction_id from purchases"`): `curl -s -X POST "$(fg env | jq -r .apiUrl)/functions/v1/purchases/test/event" -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"type":"CANCELLATION","cancelReason":"UNSUBSCRIBE","productId":"com.snacklist.app.yearly","appUserId":"<bound_user>","transactionId":"<id>"}'` → `updated`, the tile says "läuft am … aus". `"type":"EXPIRATION","expiresInDays":-1` → premium off on the buyer's client live, both cards back. `"type":"RENEWAL"` → back. `"type":"BILLING_ISSUE","graceDays":14` → only the subscriber sees `premium-billing-issue`. Then `"type":"CANCELLATION","cancelReason":"BILLING_ERROR","expiresInDays":-1` (RevenueCat's companion event, with the paid period's past end; without `expiresInDays` the local route sends a year ahead) → premium stays to the grace end (job 67). `eventAgoMs` sends an older event (`stale`).
- **Lifetime while yearly.** With the yearly plan active (only the Lifetime tile, picked): `fg tap --key premium-buy-lifetime` → `premium-lifetime-warning`; `fg tap --key premium-lifetime-warning-buy` → after the unlock `premium-lifetime-while-yearly`.
- **Billing continues.** As the subscriber: Mehr → Konto → Konto löschen → `premium-billing-continues`; as lea: `premium-billing-other`.
- **Restore, pending, states, refund, elsewhere, twice, join, web**: as before (`lifetime-restore`; `FG_FAKE_STORE=…`; `"type":"CANCELLATION"` with the default `CUSTOMER_SUPPORT`; the same `transactionId` for another household → `used_elsewhere`). Promotional grant: see `iap-promotional`.
- **Proof.** `fg screenshot --name iap-<sub-feature>` per state, the `fg db query` rows, `fg record start/stop` across buy and partner. Native screenshots of every state: `SCREENSHOT_DIR=/tmp/shots flutter test --update-goldens test/purchases/screenshots_test.dart`.

## Gotchas

- Members can't `select *` from `entitlements` (H-55): name the columns; `granted_by` is service-only.
- `household_premium()` / `household_has_premium()` are server-only; `fg db query` (service) can call them, `--as` a member cannot.
- `test/event` exists only on the local stack. The real webhook needs `REVENUECAT_WEBHOOK_AUTH` (locally `scripts/iap-local-env.sh`).
- Real store purchases and real store prices need RevenueCat, the stores and a build with `REVENUECAT_PURCHASES=true`; report them as not drivable here.
- A fake-store purchase is environment Sandbox and counts as internal in analytics.
