# Learned order

Behind the PostHog flag `learned-order` (off for users; on only for the PostHog tester group `learned-order`, never by `is_internal`, until the trip-detection privacy notice is live, Legal N-322; job 352), the list sorts each store's items in the order the household walks through that store, learned from its ticks. The app switches the household to the learned order once per session. Each store section is then one run of items with no category dividers and no category chip (users have neither anyway since `hide-categories` went full on 2026-11-22; what the flag adds is the learned order itself and the place chip); „Ohne Laden“ / "No store" keeps its own order, which only manual places change. In the edit sheet the chip „Neben Butter“ / "Next to Butter" (or „Ganz vorne“ / "At the start") places an item. The server finds shopping trips in the app's ticks: one person ticking at least 3 steps with a median of 20 s or more between them (ticks under 6 s apart are one step) at one store is a walk (so is a run of 1 or 2 ticks within 5 minutes that leaves the store with no open item), and a burst of 5 or more fast ticks is a trip that teaches nothing. Each walk moves the walked items halfway towards the order they were ticked in, so the list follows the walk over a few trips. A detected trip shows in Verlauf with „Aus deinen Häkchen erkannt“; a first-trip card says so once. Any member can remove a detected trip with „Kein Einkauf“, and each person can switch detection off in Einstellungen, which removes their detected trips. Ticking at the same store again within 30 minutes of a trip's last tick continues that trip (TD-Q2). „Nein, nur abgehakt“ on the trip question „Kaufst du gerade ein?“ (or dismissing it) keeps the question away for an hour, per person and device (job 357, for everyone); with the flag that tick run is also not detected: it makes no trip and teaches no order (TD-Q5), and the question says so under its buttons. Detection uses tick times only, no location.

## Sub-features

- `lo-list` with the flag, each store section is one run in the learned order: no category headings, the store divider („Lidl, 18 Artikel“) and rows as before. An item added or moved to a store takes that store's learned place for it (the end of its category's part of the store's walk for a new one); a category change alone keeps its place. Without the flag (or before the server switched the household) the rows follow the store's category walk order, with no dividers on the users' build (`hide-categories`) and with them on a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` build (stores-walk-order).
- `lo-place` the edit sheet of an open item shows „Neben <Name>“ / "Next to <name>" (the item before it in its section; screen-reader name „Platz in der Liste: Neben Butter“ / "Place on the list: Next to Butter") or „Ganz vorne“ / "At the start" after the store chip (where a flag-off build has its category chip). It folds out „Ganz vorne“ and the section's other open items in list order, the current neighbour marked; over 15 others a field „Artikel suchen“ / "Search items" filters them. A pick folds back; „Fertig“ saves the place (after the picked item; „Ganz vorne“: before the first) and the row moves at once, for every member. Picking another store in the same sheet drops the chip: the item takes its place in the new store. A manual place holds until three walks in a row tick it elsewhere.
- `lo-detect` the server reads the app's ticks of people who have detection on (from when they switched it on), every 5 minutes for households without a tick in the last 10 minutes. A walk at one store becomes a detected trip with the items ticked; ticks of a pharmacy (Legal GL-2) are left out first: they make no trip, join no other store's trip and teach nothing. Ticking at the same store again within 30 minutes of the trip's last tick continues it, walk or burst, in either order (TD-Q2); bursts never teach. A tick run whose person answered the trip question „Nein“ or dismissed it (`decline_shop`, sent by the app with the flag) is left out: from that tick until that person's first gap of more than 30 minutes between ticks, no trip, no walk, no change in the order (TD-Q5); a later run is detected as usual. A trip-mode trip's walk teaches too. Siri, Alexa, MCP and the API ticks never count (`item_events.source` is `voice` or `agent`).
- `lo-card` the person's first detected trip brings a mint card above the list, only while `grocery-log` and `trip-mode` are on too (its words point to the trip in Verlauf; job 401): „Einkauf bei Lidl erkannt“ / "Shopping at Lidl detected" (a trip without a store: „Einkauf erkannt“ / "Shopping trip detected"), „Snacklist merkt sich, in welcher Reihenfolge du abhakst, und sortiert die Liste beim nächsten Mal so. Der Einkauf steht im Verlauf, für die anderen im Haushalt ohne Uhrzeit. Abschalten kannst du das in den Einstellungen unter „Einkäufe aus meinen Häkchen erkennen“.“, „Ansehen“ / "View" (the trip's page) and „Verstanden“ / "Got it". Either button ends it for good, on every device. Until a person has seen their card, the others do not see that person's detected trips.
- `lo-history` Verlauf lists detected trips like other trips, with a line under the store: „Aus deinen Häkchen erkannt“ / "Detected from your ticks", „Aus den Häkchen von Mia erkannt“ / "Detected from Mia's ticks", „Aus deinen Häkchen und denen von Mia erkannt“, „Aus den Häkchen von Mia und Noah erkannt“. A tap opens the trip's page („Geplant und gekauft“); for a trip that is not the person's alone (another member's, or a shared one) it shows no clock times, and a shared one shows only once everyone else on it saw their card; and a trip with no walk (a burst) has „Abgehakt, ohne Dauer“ / "Ticked, no duration" where the time would be.
- `lo-remove` the trip page of a detected trip has „Kein Einkauf“ / "Not a shopping trip" under its head (below „5 Artikel abgehakt“) instead of the delete icon in the bar, for any member: „Kein Einkauf? Die Häkchen bleiben, aber die Reihenfolge lernt nicht mehr daraus.“ with „Abbrechen“ and „Entfernen“ / "Remove"; then the snackbar „Entfernt“ / "Removed", the page closes, the trip leaves Verlauf for everyone, and the store's order is learned again without it. Its ticks are never detected again. A failure says „Der Einkauf konnte nicht entfernt werden. Versuch es noch mal.“.
- `lo-settings` Mehr › Einstellungen, section „Einkäufe“ / "Shopping trips": the switch „Einkäufe aus meinen Häkchen erkennen“ / "Detect trips from my ticks" with „Nutzt nur, wann du abhakst. Kein Standort.“ / "Uses only when you tick. No location." On by default. Off removes the person's detected trips in every household (their walks stop ordering the list); on again counts only ticks from then on. A switch the server refuses flips back with „Die Änderung konnte nicht gespeichert werden.“ / "The change could not be saved."; when the start could not read the setting, the section appears once Einstellungen has read it again.
- `lo-quiet` with the flag, the trip question („Kaufst du gerade ein?“, trip-mode `tm-ask`) has a line under „Nein, nur abgehakt“: „Nach „Nein“ fragen wir eine Stunde lang nicht mehr, und dieser Einkauf wird nicht erkannt.“ / "If you say No, we won't ask again for an hour, and this shop won't be detected." The snooze itself is trip mode's (`tm-ask`, 60 minutes per person and device, job 357). With the flag a „Nein“ or a dismissal also sends `decline_shop` for the ticked item (queued after the tick and retried like it, also offline), so that run is not detected (`lo-detect`).
- `lo-agents` MCP `list_items` gives a learned household's items by store in walk order with a `position`; `place_item` and `set_store_order` (MCP) and `POST /v1/items/{id}/place`, `PUT /v1/stores/{id}/order`, `GET /v1/items?order=walk` (API) read and set it (ai-assistants, `docs/MCP.md`, `docs/API.md`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- A build with the flag: `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=learned-order,trip-mode,grocery-log" helpers/launch.sh`. On the users' build (flag off) none of this shows.
- Tab „Liste“ / "List": the sections without category dividers.
- Hold or right-click a row → the edit sheet → „Neben …“ / "Next to …" → „Ganz vorne“ or an item → „Fertig“.
- Tick items in a store as you walk; the card „Einkauf bei Lidl erkannt“ shows on the list once the server has detected the trip (within about 15 minutes of the last tick) and the app reloads or comes back to the front.
- Tab „Verlauf“ / "History" → a trip with „Aus deinen Häkchen erkannt“ → its page (route `/history/trip/<id>`) → „Kein Einkauf“.
- Tab „Mehr“ / "More" → „Einstellungen“ / "Settings" → „Einkäufe aus meinen Häkchen erkennen“.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=learned-order,trip-mode,grocery-log" helpers/launch.sh` (on the Hetzner runner also `FLUTTER_DIR=~/flutter-3.50.0`), on `demo-familie`; export the same `VH_DEFINES` for `doctor.sh` and `drive.sh`.
- `drive.sh learned-order` runs everything below and writes the PR screenshots `352-01` … `352-10` (`journeys/learned-order.mjs`, German light 390 × 844, one English dark). It resets first (the household's detected trips, its declines (`shop_declines`), its app tick events, Mia's and Noah's detection settings, every bought item back), adds Lidl, calls `set_order_mode(household, 'learned')` and puts the 15 seeded items plus Joghurt, Käse and Tomaten at Lidl.
- Detection runs every 5 minutes through pg_cron and skips a household with a tick in the last 10 minutes. To prove it at once, spread the ticks into a walk and call it as the database owner: `update public.item_events set client_at = …, created_at = … where …` (one tick every 30 s from now on, never back before the household's switch to the learned order or the person's first start with the flag, which detection ignores), then `select public.detect_trips('<household>')` (it returns how many trips it wrote). `detect_trips` is not granted to members; the service role may call it (`adminClient().rpc('detect_trips', …)` in the e2e spec).
- Put walked items back as the database owner or the service role (`update public.items set status = 'active'`): an untick in the app is an app tick, which the next detection reads.
- Second view: `apiAs(inst, mia)`: `from('items').select('name').order('sort_key')` per store is the learned order; `rpc('my_trip_detection')` is `{ on, card_seen, first_trip }`; `rpc('trip_history_v2', { p_household, p_today })` holds detected trips with `detected_by`. As the owner: `public.trip_sessions` with `source = 'detected'`, `removed_at`, `walks`; `public.item_order_memory` per (store, item key).
- Existing coverage: `e2e/tests/learned-order.spec.ts` (`scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- learned-order.spec.ts`), server `supabase/tests/learned_order.test.sql` and `trip_detection.test.sql`, app `app/test/trip/parts_widget_test.dart`.

- **List.** After sign-in the household is `learned` (`households.order_mode`); `sectionItems(page, 'Lidl')` (e2e `catalog.ts`) equals the API order. `sectionHeadings` is empty on a plain launch too (`hide-categories`), so it proves nothing about this flag: prove it with the place chip (`/^Platz in der Liste: /`, absent without the flag) and a „Ganz vorne“ that holds over a reload.
- **Place.** `openEdit(page, 'Schokolade')` → `editSheet(page).getByRole('button', { name: /^Platz in der Liste: Neben / })` → click → `getByRole('button', { name: 'Ganz vorne', exact: true })` → `saveEdit(page)`; wait for the response of `/rest/v1/rpc/place_item`, then the row is first in its section, also after `page.reload()`. With 18 items the options have `getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Artikel suchen' })`.
- **Quiet question.** Tick an item: the sheet holds „Nach „Nein“ fragen wir eine Stunde lang nicht mehr, und dieser Einkauf wird nicht erkannt.“; „Nein, nur abgehakt“ sends `/rest/v1/rpc/decline_shop` (one `public.shop_declines` row for Mia), then the next ticks raise no sheet for an hour.
- **Walk and card.** Tick 4 or 5 items, answering the question „Nein“, move their events and detect as above: `detect_trips` returns 0 and the order is unchanged (TD-Q5). Tick the same items again while the snooze holds (no question), move those events to start 45 minutes after the first walk's last tick (a run of its own) and detect: 1. Reload: `getByRole('heading', { name: 'Einkauf bei Lidl erkannt', exact: true })`; „Verstanden“ → gone, `my_trip_detection.card_seen` true, still gone after a reload. The walked items stand closer to their walk order in `sort_key`. `journeys/learned-order.mjs` scripts these steps, the declined first walk (no trip, `decline_shop` stored) and the second walk 45 minutes later (detected) included.
- **Verlauf.** `getByRole('tab', { name: 'Verlauf' })` → `getByRole('button', { name: /Aus deinen Häkchen erkannt/ })` → URL `/history/trip/<uuid>`. As Noah (second browser): `/Aus den Häkchen von Mia erkannt/`, and the page's ARIA snapshot has no `HH:MM`.
- **Kein Einkauf.** `getByRole('button', { name: 'Kein Einkauf', exact: true })` → the text „Kein Einkauf? Die Häkchen bleiben …“ → „Entfernen“ → back on Verlauf, „Entfernt“, the row gone; `trip_sessions.removed_at` set, the API order back to before the walk, `detect_trips` returns 0.
- **Switch.** `openMore(page, 'Einstellungen')` → `getByRole('switch', { name: /^Einkäufe aus meinen Häkchen erkennen/ })` checked; click (wait for `/rest/v1/rpc/set_trip_detection`) → unchecked, no `source = 'detected'` session left, `my_trip_detection.on` false, Verlauf „Noch keine Einkäufe“.

## Gotchas

- The flag needs the trip-mode flag for a detected trip's page: with `trip-mode` off a Verlauf trip opens the „Einkauf bearbeiten“ sheet and `/history/trip/<id>` goes to the list. trip-mode and grocery-log are off for users since 2026-11-22 (Peter HP2, job 401), and the first-trip card needs both (`lo-card`), so the e2e spec and the journey turn them on.
- The card and Verlauf read the server when the app starts or comes back to the front: after `detect_trips` reload the page.
- Others see a person's detected trips only after that person saw the card („Ansehen“ or „Verstanden“). A drive as Noah before Mia's „Verstanden“ finds no detected trip.
- One walk moves items only halfway to their walk places. Two items walked in swapped order end up with equal places after a walk or two and then stay in their old order (ties go by when the item was made), so do not assert that the list equals the walk; assert that walked pairs moved towards it.
- Ticks from before the household switched to the learned order, and from before the person's app first started with the flag, are never detected. Move a test walk's tick times forward, not into the past.
- The snooze after „Nein“ is kept on the device per person (job 357): a reload does not ask again within the hour. Every walk the journey and the spec answer „Nein“ is not detected, so the detected walk is the second one, ticked inside the snooze and spread to start more than 30 minutes after the first.
- Detection reads ticks with an app source only: a tick through `set_item_status` by a signed-in member. Ticks the service role or the owner writes have no source and are never read; `detect_trips` also ignores ticks from before the person's switch-on.
- The card's title is a heading of its own; until job 352's fix the whole card was a banner landmark on the web.
- English labels: `labels('en')` (keys `placeNextTo`, `placeFirst`, `chipPlace`, `placeFilter`, `shopAskQuiet`, `detected*`, `tripNoDuration`, `tripNotShopping*`, `tripRemoved`, `detectedCard*`, `settingsTrips`, `detectTrips`, `detectTripsHint`).
