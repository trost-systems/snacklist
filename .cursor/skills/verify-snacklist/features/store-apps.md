# Store apps and chains

Behind the PostHog flag `open-store-app` (full since 2026-11-25, Peter OS-A; Legal N-292 closed 2026-11-20; job 313), a store can be matched to a grocery chain („zugeordnet“, never „verbunden“: Legal N-293) in the household's region or another country (Lidl, ALDI Nord, ALDI SÜD, EDEKA, Netto Marken-Discount, REWE, Kaufland, PENNY, dm, Rossmann in Germany, plus 49 more chains in AT, CH, FR, NL, PL, IT and ES, the DIY chains (`sa-diy`) in Germany and, for OBI, Hornbach and Bauhaus, in some other countries (also CZ, HR, HU, LU, RO, SE, SK), and IKEA in 22 countries, `sa-ikea`). A matched store whose chain has an app gets a key in its list header and an „App“ card on its screen that open the chain's app: on Android the installed app (else its Play page), on iPhone its universal link where the chain has one, then its URL scheme's bare root (job 335; nothing sent with it), else the App Store page, where „Öffnen“ starts an installed app, in the web app the app's store page in a new tab (the App Store's in an iPhone's browser, else Google Play's), never the chain's own site: nothing reaches the chain (Legal N-296). Stores are matched where they are made: chains are offered while the name is typed, and a plain name that is a chain's asks „Ist das Lidl (Deutschland)?“ once. Stores made before are asked once from a card on the list. The store's own name never changes. Chains are plain text names, no logos, no disclaimer line (the store-name note lives only in the Impressum, Legal N-292).

## Sub-features

- `sa-header-key` (flag on) a matched store whose chain has an app: an icon key (open in new) last in its list header, after the count and the plain caret, 48 px, accessible name „<App> öffnen“ / "Open <App>" („Lidl Plus öffnen“). A tap opens the app (see above) and never folds the section; a hold does nothing. Stores without a chain or app show no key. Folded sections keep their key.
- `sa-tip` (flag on) once, under the first header that has a key: „Öffnet die App des Ladens.“ / "Opens the store's app." with an X („Schließen“). Gone for good on this device and household after the X or the first key tap.
- `sa-large-text` from 200 % text the header's count shows as a number only („2“); the accessible name keeps „2 Artikel“. Nothing overflows at 310 %, with or without the key.
- `sa-suggest` (flag on) typing a new store's name (Liste → „Anpassen“ → „Laden hinzufügen“, and „+ Neuer Laden“ in the add sheet's and the edit sheet's store options) shows up to three chains under the field from the second letter: name, country, app. Accessible name „<Chain> (<Land>) wählen“ / "Choose <Chain> (<Country>)". The browser's or phone's region comes first, then every country („Hofer“ on a German phone finds HOFER · Österreich). A tap makes the store matched; its name is the chain's when the typed text only began it („Re“ → „REWE“), else the typed text.
- `sa-ask` (flag on) saving a plain name that names a chain („Lidl“, „Lidl am Markt“) asks once: „Ist das Lidl (Deutschland)?“ / "Is this Lidl (Germany)?" with „Dann kannst du Lidl Plus direkt aus der Liste öffnen.“, „Ja“ and „Nein, so lassen“. A name of two chains („Aldi“) asks „Welches Aldi?“ with one button per chain and „Keins davon“. On the stores page it is a dialog after the name dialog closes; in the add sheet it replaces the name field, the new store already picked for the item. Closing without an answer leaves the store unasked (the list card may ask). A name of no chain („Wochenmarkt“) is saved as before, no question.
- `sa-card` (flag on) stores that are not matched, not answered, and whose name names a chain with an app get a card at the top of the list, one store at a time with „1 / 2“: „Ist dein „Lidl“ die Kette Lidl (Deutschland)?“ with Legal's line „Erkannte Läden können einen Knopf für ihre App zeigen. Die Namen bleiben, wie du sie geschrieben hast.“ and „Ja“ / „Nein“ (two chains: „Welche Kette ist dein „Aldi“?“, a button each, „Keins davon“). An answer is stored for the household and never asked again; „Ja“ is announced „Zugeordnet“ / "Matched". No card offline, behind the open add sheet, or with the flag off.
- `sa-store-screen` (flag on) a store's details: under its name, for a matched store with an app, an „App“ card with the app's name and „Öffnen“ (accessible name „<App> öffnen“). Under it the chain line „Zugeordnet: Lidl · Deutschland“ / "Matched: Lidl · Germany" with „Ändern“ („Kette ändern“) and „Zuordnung aufheben“ („Zuordnung zur Kette aufheben“); a store without a chain shows „Kette zuordnen“ / "Match to a chain", which opens the chain picker: a search „Kette suchen“, the region's chains first, typed text finds every country's chains and aliases. „Ohne Laden“ shows none of it.
- `sa-app-choice` (flag on) a chain with two apps (BILLA and jö Bonus Club, Hofer and Unser HOFER, Leclerc and Leclerc DRIVE, and others) shows „Welche App soll sich öffnen?“ with a radio per app on the store's App card; the first is the chain's loyalty or coupon app. The choice is the store's, shared with the household, and the header key's name follows it („jö Bonus Club öffnen“).
- `sa-region-chips` (flag on; job 321 OB1) the guided start's store step and „Neuer Haushalt“ from the switcher offer the browser's or phone's region's chains as chips, without the hint line („Wähl die Läden, in denen ihr einkauft. In dieser Reihenfolge stehen sie in der Liste.“); a tapped chip makes a matched store, so neither card nor question asks about it later (onboarding `stores`). Flag off: the 11 plain chips with that hint.
- `sa-done-card` (flag on; job 399, SC1) right after the last open item of a matched store with an app is ticked off, its section and key are gone, so a card at the top of the list says „Lidl am Markt ist erledigt“ / "Lidl am Markt is done" with „App öffnen“ / "Open app" below it (always below, at every text size; screen readers hear „Lidl Plus öffnen“) and an X („Schließen“). No icon. Screen readers hear the card as it appears („Lidl ist erledigt. Lidl Plus öffnen.“) and their focus moves to it; only when it appears, not on a rebuild or an app restart (job 474). Only members who ticked an item of that store within the 30 minutes before its last tick see it; one who only added items, or ticked in another store, does not. One card, the store finished last. „App öffnen“ opens the app as the key does. The card shows only in the session of the tick: it is gone when Snacklist goes to the background or is closed and opened again (paused, hidden or detached; a momentary inactive, like the iOS control center, keeps it), so also after „App öffnen“ on a phone, and it is never brought back by a cold start from the items alone. On the web a hidden tab counts as the background (`visibilitychange`): a store page that opens in a new tab and takes focus hides the opener, so the card is gone when the user comes back, as on a phone; closing and reopening the tab starts without it. A window that only loses focus (alt-tab) is `inactive` and keeps it. A headless Playwright page is never hidden, so there the card stays unless the journey fires `visibilitychange` itself. The X hides it on this device until the store is finished again (a new item ticked off); it also goes 60 minutes after the last tick, and when an item is added there. No card for a store without an app, behind the open add sheet, or with the flag off.
- `sa-ikea` (flag on; job 408) IKEA is in the registry as the chain `ikea` in the 22 countries where Inter IKEA's main IKEA app is offered (Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Iceland and the Baltics have their own IKEA apps, not covered). A store named „IKEA“ / „Ikea“ is matched the usual way (a suggestion, „Ist das IKEA (Deutschland)?“, the list card) and then gets the key „IKEA öffnen“ in its header. The region chips never include it (unit test `retailer_registry_test.dart`, pgTAP `retailers.test.sql`).
- `sa-diy` (flag on; job 410) DIY chains: stores named „OBI“, „Hornbach“, „Bauhaus“, „toom“, „hagebau(markt)“, „Globus Baumarkt“/„Globus Bau“, „Sonderpreis Baumarkt“ match their chain and get „<App> öffnen“; HELLWEG and B1 Discount have no app. Plain „Globus“ asks „Welches Globus?“ with the buttons „Globus“ (the Mein Globus app) and „Globus Baumarkt“ and stores the pick (driven in the web app by review 1, 2026-11-23, and again 2026-11-24 on 8290425e: „Globus“ and Enter in „Laden hinzufügen“, „Welches Globus?“ with both buttons, „Globus Baumarkt“ stored as `globus-baumarkt`; an existing store „Globus“ gets „Welche Kette ist dein „Globus“?“ on the list). None is a setup chip (unit test `retailer_registry_test.dart`, pgTAP `retailers.test.sql`).
- `sa-analytics` a key, „Öffnen“ or the done card's „App öffnen“ that opened something sends `store_app_opened` with the chain id, its country, app or store page (`outcome`) and where the tap was (`source`: `header`, `card`, `store`), never the store's name (after the usage-data yes only; privacy §9 names it „Laden-App geöffnet“).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Users have all of it since 2026-11-25 (the flag is full):
- List tab: the key at the end of a matched store's header; the card at the top asks about older stores; after the last item of a matched store is ticked, the „… ist erledigt“ card at the top.
- Liste → „Anpassen“ → „Laden hinzufügen“: type, tap a chain or press Enter.
- Add sheet: type an item, tap the store chip („Laden: …“), „Neuer Laden“ („Neuen Laden anlegen“), type the name.
- Liste → „Anpassen“ → a store: the „App“ card, the app choice and the chain line.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions: a plain `helpers/launch.sh` (the users' build has the flag), then `helpers/drive.sh store-apps` (`--expect on`, the default on that build) (suggest, ask, the App card, the header key and tip; `journeys/store-apps.mjs`); `journeys/onboarding-store-chains.mjs` and `journeys/new-household-store-chains.mjs` for the region chips. On a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=open-store-app` build `drive.sh store-apps --expect off` checks that none of it shows. In the e2e specs: the flag on through the PostHog fake (`fakeCollector(context, { flags: { 'open-store-app': true } })` in `e2e/tests/helpers.ts`); the browser locale de-DE is the region. Route `https://play.google.com/**` and `https://apps.apple.com/**` in the context so a key's new tab stays local. `e2e/tests/store-apps.spec.ts` covers every sub-feature but `sa-large-text`, `sa-analytics`, `sa-ikea`, `sa-diy` and `sa-done-card` (those: unit and pgTAP tests, `journeys/store-done.mjs`, and the Globus question by hand) (widget tests: `app/test/list/store_header_test.dart`, `app/test/list/store_app_test.dart`).

- Suggest: „Laden hinzufügen“, `typeInto(getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Name des Ladens' }), 'Re')` → button „REWE (Deutschland) wählen“; click → row „REWE“ on „Läden“, no dialog.
- Ask: type „Lidl am Markt“, Enter → text „Ist das Lidl (Deutschland)?“ → „Ja“; the store's details then read „Zugeordnet: Lidl · Deutschland“ and show „Lidl Plus“ with the button „Lidl Plus öffnen“.
- Open: `context.waitForEvent('page')` around a click on „Lidl Plus öffnen“ (store screen or list header) → the new tab's URL is `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lidl.eci.lidlplus` (Chromium's user agent is no iPhone).
- App choice: a store answered „Ja“ for „Billa“ (BILLA, Österreich) → details → `getByRole('radio', { name: 'jö Bonus Club' })` (in the radiogroup „Welche App soll sich öffnen?“) → button „jö Bonus Club öffnen“; after a reload the list header's key has that name.
- Card: a household made with the stores Lidl, dm and Baumarkt → text „Ist dein „Lidl“ die Kette Lidl (Deutschland)?“ and „1 / 2“ → „Ja“ → „dm“ asked → „Nein“ → no card, also after a reload.
- IKEA: „Laden hinzufügen“, type „IKEA“, Enter → „Ist das IKEA (Deutschland)?“ → „Ja“ → the details' button „IKEA öffnen“.
- Done card: `node journeys/store-done.mjs <dir>` on the same build (two members, Noah adds and Mia ticks; the card is the group named „Lidl am Markt ist erledigt“, its button „Lidl Plus öffnen“, the X „Schließen“). Widget tests: `app/test/list/store_done_card_test.dart`, the 30-minute rule `app/test/list/store_done_test.dart`, goldens `app/test/design/store_done_card_golden_test.dart`.
- Server read-back: `stores.retailer_chain`, `retailer_country`, `retailer_app`, `retailer_answered` (`apiAs` select), or `rpc('match_retailers', { p_name, p_country })`.

## Gotchas

- Flag off (`VH_FLAGS_OFF=open-store-app`, or a new install before PostHog's first answer): no key, tip, card, suggestions, question, App card or chain line; the header's plain caret is everyone's (stores-walk-order).
- The web app opens the app's store page, not an app; Android and iPhone behaviour is in the code and unit tests only (no device on the box). Unverified on a device: Android's launch without a package query, which screen the 43 universal links open, and whether the iOS apps register the 20 schemes read from the Android apps (job 335; `supabase/retailers/coverage.md` lists each app's way and source). On an iOS Simulator, `launchUrl` of a scheme root answers false without the app and opens an installed one, with nothing in Info.plist (job 335's probe).
- The done card reads who ticked from `items.bought_by` (set by the items trigger). Its dismissals live in the device's list prefs (`store_done_dismissed`, store id to the store's bought item ids then), so another device of the same member still shows it, and a card closed offline stays closed when the server stamps its own `bought_at`.
- Answers are per store: once a store is answered („Nein“ too), neither card nor question asks again; only the store screen's chain line changes it.
- Adding stores needs a connection; offline no chains are offered and typing works as before.
- The registry is `supabase/retailers/retailers.json` (108 entries, 115 apps: 21 entries in Germany, IKEA and the DIY chains included, 87 elsewhere, 17 of them DIY chains in other countries); a chain's or country's id must also be in the app's analytics allow-list (the lint gate checks).
