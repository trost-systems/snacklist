# Snacklist Premium (held: not on sale)

Premium launch is on hold: nothing is sold until the 1 December publish. One thing is locked today: AI assistants (MCP) and the Snacklist API need the account's own Premium, since production turned the server switch `integration_lock` on (2026-11-24; Zugänge then shows the Premium card and „Pausiert“, ai-assistants); Siri and Alexa stay free, and testers keep access through their per-user Lifetime. The two other locks the code still has (older trips in Verlauf behind „Ältere Einkäufe siehst du mit Snacklist Premium“ / "See older trips with Snacklist Premium" (`prem-locks`), and the card „Was war geplant, was kam dazu?“ in a trip's summary) sit behind the flags `grocery-log` and `trip-mode`, off for everyone since 2026-11-22 (Peter HP2, job 401; job 413 keeps them off at launch and re-enables them only if users ask), so no one meets either and no copy may promise them. Siri and Alexa work for every household without Premium and stay free even after the launch (jobs 184 and 72). Mehr does not list it before the launch (job 64): the row shows only once Premium may be offered (server switch `premium_offer`, and in the store builds the purchases define) or for a household that has Premium. Its page (`/more/lifetime`, PW-P4 „Haushalt“ since job 87) opens with four hexes (three members and a plus), the household sentence and the benefit lines (the heading „Snacklist Premium für dich“ only once sales are on, and never on the web, Legal Review N-68), and nothing else before sales start: no „bald verfügbar“, no „in der App kaufen“ note on the web (Legal Review's final texts, job 87). There is no price, no buy button and no restore button anywhere. A household that has been given Premium by the team (testers, reviewers, partners: a RevenueCat promotional grant) sees a honey status card under the hero, „In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet“ (a tester's grant: „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“) with „Du zahlst nichts, und nichts verlängert sich automatisch. Bevor die kostenlose Freischaltung endet, sagen wir dir mindestens 4 Wochen vorher Bescheid.“ and the link „Mehr dazu in der Datenschutzerklärung“, and the Mehr row appears (one line, no hint since job 85).

## Sub-features

- `prem-row` the Mehr row „Snacklist Premium“ (below „Konto“, no hint since job 85): today only for someone who has Premium: the shown household's grant (a free beta or tester unlock) or the account's own Premium (a purchase, or the beta lifetime grant `prem-beta-lifetime`). A household's grant follows the household shown, so switching can show or hide the row; an account's own Premium shows it in every household. Once offered, every household sees it.
- `prem-page-web` on the web (app.snacklist.app, app.hummlist.com, the preview), once sales start (`premium_offer` on), the page adds „Snacklist Premium kaufst du in der iPhone- oder Android-App. Danach ist es auch hier für dein Konto freigeschaltet.“ Not shown today, also not for an unlocked household. Nothing to buy on web, ever.
- `prem-page-soon` in the iOS and Android store builds and on the web today (purchases build define off, `premium_offer` off) the page shows the hero only (no slogan, no refund link): no „bald verfügbar“, no note on where to buy, no cards, no prices, no buy, no restore.
- `prem-refund-link` the link „Doppelt gekauft? So bekommst du dein Geld zurück“ (opens „Kauf zurückfordern“ help with the Apple and Google refund steps) ends the page only for someone who paid: once sales are on and the household or the user has a store purchase (or one is unlocking, or the store says it belongs to another account or an earlier household: „Dieser Kauf gehört zu einem anderen Konto oder zu einem früheren Haushalt und bleibt dort.“; the line about a yearly plan paid on another account (`premiumUsedElsewhereYearly`) follows only after a yearly Buy, not after a Lifetime Buy or a restore, job 422). A promotional grant alone (a beta account's lifetime grant, a tester's or a beta household's) never shows it. Not visible to anyone else today (from code and widget tests, `premium_screen.dart` `paid`, job 371b).
- `prem-granted` household with a free unlock (a RevenueCat promotional grant: testers, beta households, app review): honey status card; the grant decides the title (Legal Review N-70): a grant that ends before 2100 is a beta grant, „In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet“, also after the launch; a grant without end or to 2100-01-01 (`scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant`, testers and app review) reads „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“. - **Granted household (testers).** Grant the household through the webhook helper, then the row appears (`getByRole('button', { name: /^Snacklist Premium/ })` visible, live, without reload), and the page shows `page.getByText(/^Snacklist Premium (Lifetime|aktiv|über)/)` for a purchase. ## Gotchas

- Production builds do not set the purchases define or the fake store (`app/config/auth.json`), the server switch `premium_offer` is off, and `integration_lock` is on (since 2026-11-24; `helpers/launch.sh` turns it on with the other `VH_USER_SWITCHES`). Plans, prices and the buy card are unreachable for a normal user; the locked state on Zugänge is what every member without Premium of their own sees.
- Every build since job 328 follows the server switch `integration_lock`; with it on, Zugänge shows the Premium card, tokens show „Pausiert: braucht Snacklist Premium“ and MCP and the Snacklist API answer that the user needs Premium of their own (job 419: neither the household's grant nor another member's plan counts, so a tester's grant must be a per-user one, `scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant --user <uuid>`); the app follows the user's own Premium, so a user with only a household grant sees the card and paused tiles; Siri and Alexa keep adding, and the Siri credential's and Alexa's tiles are never paused (jobs 184 and 72). The card lists only „KI-Assistenten, die MCP unterstützen“. A Shortcut's access token still shows as paused, though Siri accepts it. That is a test of shipped-but-dormant code, not today's product. The e2e web build still never sells (web is `PurchasesUnsupported`).
- The Mehr row has no hint since job 85; the old placeholder „Siri und KI-Assistenten“ is gone from the ARB, and since job 184 so are `premiumBenefitSiri` and `integrationPremiumSiri`.
- Testers get Premium by a RevenueCat promotional grant (made by the team in the RevenueCat dashboard or with `scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant --user <uuid> --apply`, until 2100-01-01; since job 419 a household grant with `--household` no longer opens MCP or the API, only the user's own does); the old tester list is gone. The page shows the tester line „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“ (job 87, N-70), not Lifetime. The grant takes effect through RevenueCat's webhook or `scripts/iap/promotional.ts reconcile --household <uuid> --apply`; RevenueCat unreachable means a retry later. Who gets this free unlock and what RevenueCat receives is in the privacy policy, section „Snacklist Premium in der Beta“ (snacklist.app/datenschutz#premium-beta, #216): testers and Apple's and Google's app review; it lasts until the team ends it and never turns into a paid purchase. The same section also covers the per-account beta lifetime grant (Art. 6(1)(b), policy dated 23 November 2026).
- Words in this feature for copy: „Snacklist Premium“ is the name; yearly and Lifetime are the planned plans but are not on sale. Do not announce a price.
- `paywall_viewed` (entry points `more`, `integrations`, `history` from the grocery log's locked row, and `trip` from a trip summary's locked card) fires only when a plan with a store price is shown, so never in production today; it is also subject to analytics consent. unverified in a live run (read from code only).
- Real store purchases cannot be driven on the box (no store). unverified (no store sandbox). StoreKit-local Lifetime cases (purchase, restore, refund, Ask to Buy against `Snacklist.storekit`) run in the ios-storekit CI gate on an iOS 26.2 simulator, which the gate downloads on the `xcode-27` image (jobs 127, 304 and 306, `scripts/gates/ios-storekit.sh`); the yearly plan has no StoreKit test.
- The last member deleting the account ends any Premium, a free unlock included: see account-deletion `acct-premium`.
