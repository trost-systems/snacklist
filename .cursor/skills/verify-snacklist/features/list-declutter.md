# Decluttered list

What users see (flag `list-declutter`, rolled out in full): the list screen has one switcher and no second header row, and the household sheet has a page per household. The household controls live there, not in More: More has no row for the household and no „Leute einladen“ / "Invite people", and the invite tips open the sheet's invite page. `/more/household` and `/more/invites` still open the old pages by link. Users have no totals block and no „Währung“ today: both need `prices-and-trip-total`, off for everyone since 2026-11-22 (`ld-total`, `ld-sheet`). The flag-off screen (list tabs, More's household page) is in [lists-and-claims](./lists-and-claims.md) and [household-invites](./household-invites.md).

## Sub-features

- `ld-switcher` top left of the list one raised button shows the household small and grey over the shown list in bold („Familie Brandt“ over „Einkaufsliste“), with an up-down icon. Its accessible name is „<list> · <household>“ („Einkaufsliste · Familie Brandt“), its hint „Liste oder Haushalt wechseln“ / "Switch list or household". It shows with one household and one list too. There is no row of list tabs under it.
- `ld-glass` the status bar area and the bar are one clear layer (job 242, Peter on build 1325): the rows scroll on under it in full, with no blur, tint or edge line; the switcher, Anpassen and the new-adds capsule keep their own faces. While the add sheet is open (`add-feedback-list`, on for all users) the whole bar fades out over the 300 ms glide, takes no taps and is out of the screen reader's view, and the rows take its room up to the status bar; it fades back on close (quick-add `qa-feedback-list`). The summary block of `ld-total` and „Zuletzt verwendet“ are not shown while the sheet is open. Once rows pass under the status bar, it alone gets a scrim (the page's colour fading out downwards) so the clock stays readable. Taps on the layer never reach a row under it.
- `ld-bottom` the add field and the tab pill float over the list with nothing behind them: the rows (and the summary block) stay in full view between and beside them, with no fade. The list ends with room for both, so at its end the last row and „Zuletzt verwendet“ (open from the start since `recently-used-open` went full, tick-and-undo) lie above the add field, with the summary block between them only when prices are on (`ld-total`).
- `ld-total` with prices (`prices-and-trip-total`, off for everyone since 2026-11-22, Peter HP2, job 401; `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=prices-and-trip-total,trip-mode"` for a drive), the summary block (ST3, job 237) sits after the last store section and before „Zuletzt verwendet“, not as a bar over the add field. With two groups or more (stores, „Ohne Laden“ counts as one) it reads „EINKAUFSSUMME“ / "SHOPPING TOTAL", then a line per store in the list's store order with „Ohne Laden“ / "No store" last (name in full, wrapping; under it „<n> ohne Preis“ / "<n> without price" when some have no price; the subtotal right), then „Gesamt“ / "Total" with „<n> mit Preis · <n> ohne Preis“ / "<n> with price · <n> without price" and the sum (Peter's ST-Q6 answer, job 242: a part with 0 is left out, never „offen“ without a trip; during a trip „Gesamt“ always shows both parts, „0,00 € gekauft“ included, while a store line leaves out an empty bought part). One group: only „Einkaufssumme“ with the counts and the sum. A tap on a store line scrolls its section under the header (unfolding it); its hint is „Zum Laden in der Liste“ / "Go to the store in the list". The sections have no „Summe <Laden>“ line at their end then.
  - Without a running trip the sums count only open items: a tick takes the item out.
  - With an item without today's price in a sum, it reads as a floor: „mind. 32,08 €“ / "at least €32.08".
  - A price per kg or l typed per kg or l on an open item counts from the planned weight: its row reads „≈ 1,51 €“, and so does every sum holding it („mind. ≈ …“ with both). The label's total typed as „Gesamt“, a price per piece and a ticked item are exact. A price per kg or l on a quantity without a weight („3“ Gurken at 2,99 €/kg) sums nothing and counts as „ohne Preis“ (job 237 review); its chip reads „+ Preis“. Only a price in another currency stays out of the counts.
  - While a trip runs on the shown list ([trip-mode](./trip-mode.md)), items bought on the trip count too: each line says „1,19 € gekauft · 2,49 € offen“ / "€1.19 bought · €2.49 open" (then „· 1 ohne Preis“), and a store bought in full stays with a check and „alles gekauft“ / "all bought" (not tappable). Gesamt says the same for the list. After the trip ends the ticked items drop out again.
- `ld-total-priced` (job 348, TE1, flag `total-priced-only`, off for everyone, internal installs included, since 2026-11-22: Peter HP2, job 401) the summary block shows only what has a price. No item with a price (a price in another currency counts as none): no block at all, the list ends with „Zuletzt verwendet“. Otherwise a line only for a store or „Ohne Laden“ with a priced item, with no „<n> ohne Preis“ under it; the head and the store lines only with two such lines (one store priced among unpriced ones: „Einkaufssumme“ alone). Under „Gesamt“ only „<n> ohne Preis“ / "<n> without price" (every item without a price on the list, the hidden stores' too), never „mit Preis“; every item priced: no count line. „mind.“, „≈“ and the trip's „gekauft · offen“ split stay; a store bought in full without a price gets no line, one with a price keeps „alles gekauft“. An amount too wide for its share of the line (at 310 % text) goes under the name, whole („mind. ≈“ / „5,70 €“), instead of breaking inside the number. Off: `ld-total` unchanged. Driven by `journeys/total-priced-only.mjs` (`--expect on` with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=total-priced-only,prices-and-trip-total,trip-mode"`, `--expect off` with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=prices-and-trip-total,trip-mode"`; a plain launch has no prices and no trip question since 2026-11-22, so both fail there); widget tests `app/test/list/summary_priced_only_test.dart` run each case with the flag on and off.
- `ld-pill` the tab pill holds „Liste“ and „Mehr“ for users („Verlauf“ between them only with `grocery-log`, off for everyone since 2026-11-22). With three tabs it keeps all labels at 130 % text also on a 358 px phone (its tabs narrow first; rounded under RD1); at larger text the unselected tabs show their icon only. Switching to „Liste“ says „Auf Liste <Liste> im Haushalt <Haushalt>, <n> offen“ („nichts offen“ at 0) / "On list <List> in household <Household>, <n> open items" ("1 open item", "no open items") to screen readers (job 474); the tabs keep their selected state.
- `ld-sheet` a tap on the switcher opens the sheet on the shown household's page: „‹ Haushalte“ / "‹ Households"; the household's name (no „<n> offen“ since job 474; each list keeps its count), a pencil button „Haushalt umbenennen“ / "Rename household" (the same rename dialog as More's household page) and the button „Einladen“ / "Invite" (no icon before the name); the members row „3 Mitglieder · 1 Einladung offen“ / "3 members · 1 open invite" (the invites part only with open person invites); „Listen“ / "Lists" with each list (open count, a check on the shown one, a menu „Aktionen für „<name>““ with „Umbenennen“ and „Löschen“, not on the last list); „Neue Liste“; then „Währung“ / "Currency" („EUR · €“; only with `prices-and-trip-total`, so users do not see it), „Beitrittsanfragen“ / "Join requests" with the switch „Beitritte bestätigen“ / "Confirm new members" (owner and admins only; its subtitle „Neue Leute warten, bis ein Admin sie aufnimmt.“, one screen-reader stop with it), and a red „Haushalt verlassen“ / "Leave household" (the same dialog as before; the sheet closes after leaving). „Als Standard nutzen“ is not on this page: the default is the `ld-voice` row. A tap on a list shows it and closes the sheet; on another household's page it switches to that household and shows that list.
- `ld-households` „‹ Haushalte“ shows today's „Deine Haushalte“ without the invite buttons: each household with a chevron, a tap opens its page (it does not switch). Pending join requests, „Neuer Haushalt“, „Mit Code beitreten“ are as today. Below them one row for Siri, Alexa & AI (`ld-voice`).
- `ld-voice` the households page's last row reads „Siri, Alexa & KI nutzen:“ / "Siri, Alexa & AI add to:", then the default household after a house icon and its list for Siri, Alexa & AI after a checklist icon (also a household's only list), each on its own line and wrapping (no „…“). Screen readers hear it as one: „Siri, Alexa & KI nutzen: Haushalt: <household> Liste: <list>“ / "Siri, Alexa & AI add to: Household: <household> List: <list>" (job 248). A tap opens „Standard für Siri, Alexa & KI“ with „‹ Haushalte“: each household, a check on the default. A tap on one with two lists or more opens „Liste für Siri, Alexa & KI“ with „‹ Siri, Alexa & KI“ / "‹ Siri, Alexa & AI" and „Was du sagst, ohne eine Liste zu nennen, landet in „<household>“ auf dieser Liste.“, each list with a check on the current pick; a tap on a list saves it and the household and goes back to the households, whose row names both. A tap on a household without a list step saves it at once. Back from the list step saves nothing. Both pages wait for the server before moving on (the tapped row shows it is busy, the others take no tap) and show a snackbar when the save fails (`voice_default_pages.dart`), unlike the flag-off picker, which shows the change at once.
- `ld-members` the members row opens „Mitglieder“ / "Members" with „‹ <household>“: each member with their role and, for the owner and admins, the same menu as on More's household page (remove asks „<Name> entfernen?“); „Leute einladen“ / "Invite people" (a page of the same sheet, job 242); „Offene Einladungen“ / "Open invites" with each open person invite's code, „gültig bis …“ and „Widerrufen“ / "Revoke" (no confirm, as on the invite sheet).
- `ld-invite` „Einladen“ on the household's page and „Leute einladen“ on the members page open „Leute einladen“ / "Invite people" as a page of the same sheet, not a second sheet (job 242): „‹ Mitglieder“ / "‹ Members", the title, the household's name and the invite panel as on More's Einladungen (Neue Einladung, the group link with its QR code, Teilen, Link kopieren, Code kopieren, Neuen Link erstellen). It scrolls inside the sheet; back shows the members page with the new invite under „Offene Einladungen“.
- `ld-back` back goes invite → members → household page → households, and list step → household step → households for Siri, Alexa & AI. The system back (Android) goes the same way. A tap beside the sheet, Escape, dragging it down or the ✕ „Schließen“ / "Close" top right closes it from any page. Back and ✕ stay at the top while a long page scrolls under them, and the sheet never grows over the status bar: its drag handle always shows below it (job 242).
- `ld-reorder` press and hold a list or a household, then drag it up or down. The lists' order is the household's (as „Nach oben“ / „Nach unten“ on the old household page); the households' order is each member's own, kept on the server, and also orders them in MCP and in the flag-off household sheet (the public API lists no households). Screen readers in the phone apps get „Nach oben“ / „Nach unten“ actions on each row (not on the web, whose engine has no custom actions). Moving another list first never changes the list on screen. Offline, changing lists is refused with „Listen lassen sich nur mit Verbindung anlegen, umbenennen, sortieren oder löschen.“, also for another household's lists.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- A plain `helpers/launch.sh` build (on the Hetzner runner with `FLUTTER_DIR=~/flutter-3.50.0`).
- Tab „Liste“ → the switcher top left → the shown household's page; „‹ Haushalte“ → all households; the members row → „Mitglieder“. On screen the back row is an arrow and the page's name; its accessible name is „Zurück: <Seite>“ / "Back: <page>" (`getByRole('button', { name: /^Zurück: / })`; „Zurück: Haushalte“, „Zurück: Familie Brandt“, „Zurück: Mitglieder“, „Zurück: Siri, Alexa & KI“, driven 2026-11-15 on c3ea4261). The ✕ is „Schließen“.

## Driving it with Playwright

- `node journeys/list-summary.mjs <dir> [--lang de|en] [--dark] [--scale 1.6] [--compact]` (`ld-total`) on `demo-familie`, launched with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=prices-and-trip-total,trip-mode"` (a plain launch has neither since 2026-11-22: no block, no trip question, so it fails there): ticks off the household's open items a day ago, puts seven priced and unpriced items over Lidl, dm, a long-named market and no store on Mia's list, reads every block line's accessible name, jumps to Lidl from its line, ticks Seife and answers „Ja“ (dm „alles gekauft“, Gesamt split), ends the trip and checks Seife drops out. It deletes its items and trip sessions at the end, by name, so the seeded „Bananen“ goes too: `launch.sh --fresh` before `trip-mode.mjs`, which needs it.
- `node journeys/list-declutter.mjs <dir> [--lang de|en] [--dark] [--scale 1.3]` on `demo-familie` or `demo-family-en` (not through drive.sh: a sub-feature journey). It adds two lists, a second household and an open person invite through the API, shoots the list (top, scrolled, the end; the total only on a build with prices), the three sheet pages and the members page, drags a list and a household by real touch (CDP touch events, 700 ms hold) and checks the new orders through `lists` and `my_households`, and that the switcher still names the same list. It removes what it added.
- `node journeys/voice-default.mjs <dir> [--lang de|en] [--dark] [--scale 2]` (`ld-voice`) on `demo-familie` or `demo-family-en`, on a 320 × 568 phone: adds a second household with a long name and a second list there, shoots the row, the household step and the list step, goes back from the list step and checks through `my_households` and `default_list` that nothing was saved, then picks the long list and checks the household and the list on the server and the row's accessible name. `--scale 2` sets the text size after signing in. It leaves the household at the end.
- The switcher: `page.getByRole('button', { name: / · Familie Brandt$/ })`. With the sheet open the header is under the barrier and has no role: read the switcher before opening or after closing (Escape).
- Text size 130 %: set `document.documentElement.style.fontSize = '130%'` at DOMContentLoaded (`context.addInitScript`).
- Widget tests: `app/test/list/list_declutter_test.dart` (header, clear layer, rows in full behind the bar and the bottom controls by pixel contrast, the end of the list above the add field, total row, pill labels), `app/test/list/summary_block_test.dart` and `app/test/list/subtotals_test.dart` (the summary block's rules, 160 %, English), `app/test/household/switcher_sheet_test.dart` (pages, back, reorder, semantics actions, the invite page below the status bar on iPhone, iPhone SE and 358 px at 130 %, ✕, members, 130/160 %).

## Gotchas

- `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0` builds the flag-off screen (what the e2e specs see): list tabs, More's household and invite rows, the „Einkaufssumme“ bar ([prices](./prices.md)); `VH_FLAGS_OFF=list-declutter` leaves out only this flag. Without the flag the household sheet keeps „Siri, Alexa & KI nutzen: …“ (a dialog) and „Liste: …“ (a menu); both check the tap at once and put the old pick back, with the reason in a snackbar, when the save fails (job 464).
- A first list stored as „Einkaufsliste“ shows as "Shopping list" in English (`listName`): compare what the switcher shows, not the database name.
- `ReorderableDelayedDragStartListener` needs the hold to stay still for the long-press time; a mouse drag without the hold scrolls the sheet instead.
