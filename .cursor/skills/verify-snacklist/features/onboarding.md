# Onboarding: the guided start, from the first screen to the list

Signed out, Snacklist opens on a start screen (`/start`) with „Los geht's“ / "Get started" and „Ich habe schon ein Konto“ / "I already have an account". „Los geht's“ starts the guided start: the usage-data question (only if this browser or phone has not answered it), „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“, the household name (with „Bestehendem Haushalt beitreten“ for someone holding an invite code), the stores, „Was fehlt gerade?“, then the account, as late as possible, and the name step „Wie sollen wir dich nennen?“ (display-name). Everything typed before the account stays on the device and survives a reload or an app kill. Once signed in, the app makes the household from the answers (name, stores, kind, the items) and offers invites, several at once, each marked live when someone joins. A signed-in user without a household still lands on „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ / "Welcome to Snacklist" (`/welcome`); its „Haushalt erstellen“ runs the same steps from „Mit wem“, without the usage question and the account step. An invite link skips all of this: after sign-in, the usage question and the name step the app joins the household with no further tap and opens its list (on the web `/join/<code>` is the static join page, whose „Im Browser beitreten“ opens the app at `/?invite=<code>`; household-invites `join-page`, `link-join`).

## Sub-features

- `start-screen` signed out, `/` opens `/start` (key `start-screen`): the Snacklist icon over the wordmark, the line „Eure Einkaufsliste. Live auf jedem Handy.“ / "Your shopping list. Live on every phone.", the text „Willkommen! In zwei Minuten steht eure gemeinsame Liste, mit allem, was gerade fehlt.“ / "Welcome! In two minutes your shared list is ready, with everything you need right now.", the filled button „Los geht's“ / "Get started" (`start-go`), the text button „Ich habe schon ein Konto“ / "I already have an account" (`start-log-in`, opens `/login`), and the links Impressum and Datenschutz at the foot. No „Einladung einfügen“. With a guided start under way, `/` and `/start` resume at its step instead.
- `steps` the steps live at `/start/<step>`: `usage`, `who`, `name`, `stores`, `need`, `account`, `setup`, `invite`. Each has a title bar whose title is the counter „Schritt N von 7“ / "Step N of 7" (a heading on the web; the setup step's title is „Snacklist“ instead), with the X „Einrichtung verlassen“ / "Leave setup" (`onboarding-exit`; the invite step has none) and a back arrow „Zurück“ to the step before (none on the usage question, „Mit wem“, the setup and the invite step). Android's back does the same since job 206, on the account step since job 236 (before, it went to the start screen `/start`). On a step without the arrow it goes to `/start` signed out (usage, „Mit wem“) and stays on the step signed in („Mit wem“ from „Haushalt erstellen“, the setup, the invite step; before job 236 it showed the signed-out start screen). Widget-tested in `app/test/onboarding/guided_start_test.dart` (`handlePopRoute`); not driven, the web has no Android back. The progress bar is pinned under the title bar, in the same place on every step (outside the step's list), then the step's question as its heading. Since job 454 the seven counted steps are one page: the title bar, the progress bar and the step's docked buttons stay still, and only the step's body changes, the old one sliding 28 px left and fading while the new one comes in from the right in 300 ms (mirrored on Back, Android's back included; the bar moves to its new value). With reduced motion (the browser's prefers-reduced-motion, Android's remove animations, iOS Reduce Motion) it is a 120 ms fade with no movement. Weiter closes the keyboard as the step changes, and the screen reader and the web's focus go to the new step's heading. Docked at the bottom, above the keyboard: Weiter and Überspringen (and JH2's „Bestehendem Haushalt beitreten“ on the name step, the stores hint on the stores step, only with `open-store-app` off, so users have none), the legal links on the account step and „Später“ on the invite step; the usage question's two answers, the account step's sign-in buttons and the invite buttons stay in the body; the seven counted steps are usage, who, name, stores, need, account and invite. A step's list has the key `onboarding-<step>`. „Weiter“ / "Continue" is `onboarding-next`, „Überspringen“ / "Skip" `onboarding-skip`, an outlined button of the same size below it, only on „Mit wem“ and the stores.
- `usage-step` no question heading of its own (the bar says „Schritt 1 von 7“): the consent question with the exact words of privacy-consent (`ConsentQuestion`), „Nicht erlauben“ and „Erlauben“. Shown only when this install has not answered; the answer moves on to „Mit wem“.
- `who` „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“ / "Who do you shop with?", text „Wähl alles, was passt. Danach richten sich Name, Einladung und deine ersten Tipps.“ / "Pick all that apply. We use this for the name, the invite and your first tips." Six chips, any number: „Partner·in“ / "Partner", „Familie“ / "Family", „WG“ / "Flatmates", „Verein oder Team“ / "Club or team", „Nur ich“ / "Just me", „Andere“ / "Other" (keys `onboarding-kind-partner|family|flat|club|solo|other`). „Andere“ adds the field „Mit wem noch?“ / "Who else?" (hint „z. B. Nachbarn“, key `onboarding-kind-other-text`). With two or more picked: „Wir starten mit „<first>“. Die anderen merken wir uns für später.“ / "We'll start with “<first>” and keep the others for later." (`onboarding-kinds-several`). „Weiter“ is off until something is picked; „Überspringen“ goes on without a kind. The first pick becomes the household's kind (`households.kind`); the others are only kept on the device for later (a user can have several households since job 169b; the guided start still makes one).
- `name` „Wie soll euer Haushalt heißen?“ / "What should your household be called?", text „Den Namen sehen alle, die du einlädst. Du kannst ihn später ändern.“ / "Everyone you invite sees this name. You can change it later." Field „Name des Haushalts“ / "Household name" (`household-name`), prefilled from „Mit wem“: „Unsere Familie“, „Unsere WG“, „Unser Verein“, „Meine Liste“ (Nur ich), the „Andere“ words when they are a valid name, otherwise „Unser Haushalt“ / "Our household" (EN: "Our family", "Our flat", "Our club", "My list"). The suggestion follows the answer: back on „Mit wem“ with another kind, the name step suggests that kind's name, unless the user typed a name of their own, which stays (job 461). 1 to 80 characters, else „Gib einen Namen mit 1 bis 80 Zeichen ein.“ / "Enter a name of 1 to 80 characters." The 80 are code points, as the server counts: the field stops there and cuts a character that does not fit whole (an emoji of several code points, such as a family emoji, is several), so a name the field holds is always taken; the counter still counts characters. The same limit holds for the „Andere“ field, the „Neuer Haushalt“ name and the rename dialog (job 461). No „Überspringen“ here; under „Weiter“ the outlined button „Bestehendem Haushalt beitreten“ (`join-existing`).
- `stores` „Wo kauft ihr ein?“ / "Where do you shop?". Users get the region's chains (next bullet, `open-store-app` full since 2026-11-25); with the flag off (`VH_FLAGS_OFF=open-store-app`): text „Wähl die Läden, in denen ihr einkauft. In dieser Reihenfolge stehen sie in der Liste.“ / "Pick the stores you shop at. Your list shows them in this order." Eleven chips, none picked (`onboarding-store-<name>`: EDEKA, REWE, Lidl, Aldi Süd, Aldi Nord, Kaufland, Netto, Penny, dm, Rossmann, Baumarkt; „Baumarkt“ / "Hardware store" (job 245: in the UI language, the server knows both names) is a store type that brings the Baumarkt categories, feature categories `cat-baumarkt`), „Eigenen Laden hinzufügen“ / "Add your own store" (`onboarding-store-own`, an inline field „Name des Ladens“ / "Store name"; a new name joins the chips picked, a typed name of an offered chain in any case or spacing, „edeka“, ticks that chip instead, and a name already picked stays picked once; „Weiter“ adds a name typed but not confirmed with the check, as the check would, „Überspringen“ does not, job 461), no store-name note (since job 313 only in the Impressum, Legal N-292), „Weiter“, „Überspringen“ (no stores) and below it „Ohne Läden starten. Du kannst sie später unter Liste → Anpassen anlegen.“ / "Start without stores. You can add them later under List → Edit." The household gets the stores in the order picked.

Driven with `journeys/onboarding-store-chains.mjs --scenario de|unlisted|at|us` on a plain launch (2026-11-20, job 321 branch at 8f3b96d2, then with `FLAGS_ON=open-store-app`); widget-tested in `app/test/onboarding/stores_linked_test.dart`, the matching in `app/test/list/retailer_registry_test.dart`.
- `need` „Was fehlt gerade?“ / "What do you need right now?", text „Tipp an, was auf die Liste soll, oder schreib es unten hin.“ / "Tap what should go on the list, or type it below." Twelve chips (Milch, Brot, Eier, Butter, Käse, Bananen, Äpfel, Tomaten, Nudeln, Kaffee, Joghurt, Klopapier; EN Milk, Bread, Eggs, Butter, Cheese, Bananas, Apples, Tomatoes, Pasta, Coffee, Yogurt, Toilet paper; keys `onboarding-staple-<name>`), the field „Noch etwas?“ / "Anything else?" (hint „z. B. 2 Gurken“, `onboarding-need-field`) with the plus „Hinzufügen“ / "Add" (`onboarding-need-add`) or Enter. Under „Auf deiner Liste (N)“ / "On your list (N)" each item is a chip with an X „<name> entfernen“ (`onboarding-need-item-<text>`). An item is added once, whatever its case or spacing. „Weiter“ adds what is still in the field, + or not (job 461; the button's count does not include it). The button reads „Weiter“, „Weiter mit 1 Artikel“ or „Weiter mit N Artikeln“ / "Continue with N items". Typed text is split as the list does („2 Gurken“ becomes Gurken with the amount 2) and the server picks the category.
- `account-step` signed out only: the bar says „Schritt 6 von 7“, the heading „Konto erstellen“ / "Create your account", „Fast geschafft. Mit einem Konto speichern wir eure Liste, und du kannst jemanden einladen.“ / "Almost done. - `name-step` once signed in, before the setup: „Wie sollen wir dich nennen?“ at `/your-name` (display-name `dn-step`), required for an account without a household; not counted in „Schritt N von 7“. A Google or Apple name is prefilled but not chosen, so a new Google or Apple user sees the step, prefilled, with „Ja, das bin ich“; only a name saved or confirmed earlier skips it (`asksName` in `your_name.dart`).
- `setup` after the name step, „Wir richten euren Haushalt ein …“ / "Setting up your household…" (`onboarding-setup`) with a spinner: the household is created with the name, stores and kind (a „Verein oder Team“ household starts with „Beitritte bestätigen“ on) and gets the items. Offline or on an error it shows the reason (`onboarding-setup-problem`, for example „Keine Internetverbindung. Versuche es erneut, sobald du online bist.“) and „Erneut versuchen“ / "Try again" (`onboarding-setup-retry`). A retry or an app kill in between makes one household and adds each item once.
- `invite-step` title and first button by kind: „Partner·in einladen“, „Familie einladen“, „Mitbewohner einladen“, „Mitglieder einladen“, otherwise „Jemanden einladen“ (EN "Invite your partner", "Invite your family", "Invite your flatmates", "Invite members", "Invite someone"). Text „Teile den Link mit der Person, mit der du einkaufst. Jede Einladung gilt 7 Tage und für eine Person.“ / "Share the link with the person you shop with. Each invite works for one person and is valid for 7 days." (for a club „Schick jedem Mitglied einen eigenen Link. Alle sehen dieselbe Liste.“), and with items „N Artikel warten schon auf der Liste.“ / "N items are already waiting on the list." (`invite-step-waiting`). Under these texts the grey hint on what the join page shows about you („Wer Link oder Code hat, sieht vor dem Beitritt deinen Namen …“, household-invites `join-page-hint`; driven 2026-11-13 in DE and EN, wording since job 317). The first button (`invite-partner`) makes an invite; after it „Weitere Einladung“ / "Another invite" (`invite-another`) makes one more, each its own card (code, the link's QR code „QR-Code dieser Einladung“, link, „gültig bis …“, „Teilen“ where there is a share sheet, „Link kopieren“, „Code kopieren“; no „Widerrufen“ here). The phone apps open the share sheet at once; the web shows the card.
- `invite-joined` while the step is open, an invite that someone accepts turns into „<Name> ist beigetreten“ / "<Name> joined" (or „Beigetreten“ / "Joined" without a display name) with a check mark, in place of the card's buttons (`invite-joined`, a live region). Realtime on `invites`; an invite accepted before the step subscribed shows too. For a club (join approval on, every „Verein oder Team“ household from the guided start), an accept is a join request: the card stays as it is, and the request shows on the household's page in the switcher sheet (the list's title, then the household) until an owner or admin lets the person in (job 168c).
- `invite-leave` „Später“ / "Later" (`invite-later`), after an invite „Weiter zur Liste“ / "Go to the list", ends the guided start and opens the list with the items on it. „Nur ich“ as the only pick skips the invite step and goes straight to the list.
- `exit` the X opens „Einrichtung verlassen?“ / "Leave setup?" with „Was du bisher eingegeben hast, bleibt auf diesem Gerät gespeichert.“ / "What you've entered so far stays saved on this device." and three buttons: „Fortschritt verwerfen“ / "Discard progress" (red, `onboarding-exit-discard`), signed out „Stattdessen anmelden“ / "Sign in instead" (`onboarding-exit-sign-in`, opens the sign-in, the answers stay) or signed in „Abmelden“ / "Sign out" (`onboarding-exit-sign-out`), and „Weiter einrichten“ / "Continue setup" (`onboarding-exit-stay`). Discarding needs no second confirmation: then the start screen (signed out) or „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ (signed in).
- `resume` the answers are saved on every change (`DeviceCache` key `onboarding.draft`, one per device). A reload or an app kill resumes at the step left; signed out past the questions, at the account step. Typing another step's address goes back to the draft's step. A draft that cannot be read starts over.
- `existing-account` someone who signs in (account step or „Stattdessen anmelden“) to an account that already has a household gets the typed items on that household's list; its name, stores and kind stay as they were, and there is no invite step. Joining by a typed code with a draft under way drops the draft, as an invite link does (`invite-link`; `join_code_screen.dart`, from code, not driven). An account without a household continues the questions where it left them, without the account step.
- `welcome` signed in without a household (after the usage question on builds with analytics): „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ / "Welcome to Snacklist" with „Snacklist teilt eine Einkaufsliste mit allen in deinem Haushalt. Erstelle einen neuen Haushalt oder nimm eine Einladung an.“ and two cards: „Haushalt erstellen“ (hint „Eine neue Liste, zu der du danach Leute einlädst.“ / "Start a new list, then invite the people you shop with.", key `onboarding-create`) opens `/start/who`; „Einladung annehmen“ (hint „Du hast einen Link oder einen Code bekommen.“, `onboarding-join`) opens the join screen. No „Einladung einfügen“. Below: „Konto löschen“ (red); in the app bar a logout icon „Abmelden“.
- `own-while-waiting` on the join-request wait without a household, the text button „Lieber eigene Liste anlegen“ / "Start my own list instead" (`join-request-go-on`) runs the guided start from „Mit wem“. If the request is approved during the questions, they go on and the user ends with both: the joined household and a new one of their own with the typed name, stores and items (the joined list gets none of them). The joined household stays the default for Siri, Alexa & KI.
- `join` the join screen, title „Einladung annehmen“ / "Join with an invite": field „Einladungscode“, „Weiter“ previews „Einladung in den Haushalt <Name>“, then „Haushalt beitreten“ or „Abbrechen“. A link instead joins with no tap. Details in household-invites.
- `invite-link` an invite (`/?invite=<code>` on the web, `/join/<code>` in the apps) survives sign-in, the usage question and the name step (kept 7 days on the device); signed out it opens the sign-in (`/login?invite=<code>`) with the sheet „Du bist in einen Haushalt eingeladen“, not the start screen, and after sign-in it joins with no tap and opens the household's list (household-invites `link-join`). An unfinished guided start on the device is dropped before that join.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open Snacklist signed out: the start screen. „Los geht's“, the usage question if asked, „Mit wem“, name, stores, „Was fehlt gerade?“, the account, the name step, the setup, the invite step, the list.
- With an invite code but no link: „Los geht's“, the usage question, „Überspringen“ on „Mit wem“, then „Bestehendem Haushalt beitreten“ on the name step.
- On the sign-in screen: „Neu bei Snacklist? Los geht's“ / "New to Snacklist? Get started" goes to the start screen, or back into a guided start under way.
- Signed in without a household: „Willkommen bei Snacklist“, „Haushalt erstellen“, then „Mit wem“ to „Was fehlt gerade?“, the setup and the invite step.
- An invite link: the sheet „Du bist in einen Haushalt eingeladen“, the sign-in, the usage question, the name step, then the household's list (joined with no tap).

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Scripted, the step transition (job 454): `node journeys/onboarding-t1.mjs <dir> [--lang en] [--reduced]` films who → name, name → stores (the field focused) and Back on Playwright's paused clock into strips, and checks one „Weiter“ mid-change, the field unfocused by Weiter and the DOM focus on each new step's heading; `--reduced` emulates prefers-reduced-motion. Last driven 26 November 2026, code version d8e81d65 (German, English, German with reduced motion).
- Scripted, the answers no step may drop (job 461): `node journeys/onboarding-dead-ends.mjs <dir> [--lang en]` changes the kind after Zurück (the name suggestion follows), types 74 letters and a family emoji as the name (the field keeps the letters, Weiter takes them), types an own store without its check and an item without +, taps Weiter each time, signs up a fresh user and reads the household: the name, kind `flat`, the store and the item. Hover after Zurück shows its tooltip, which takes the button out of the semantics tree for a moment; the journey moves the mouse away. Driven 2026-11-26 on job 461's branch, German and English: PASS. Widget-tested in `app/test/onboarding/guided_start_test.dart` and `app/test/household/onboarding_test.dart`.
- Scripted: `helpers/drive.sh onboarding [--lang en] [--dark]` (`journeys/onboarding.mjs`, two fresh users) walks every step, the reload resume, two invites and a second browser joining live. Driven 2026-11-14 on main 97b2907e (DE light, EN dark).
- Local stack, the verify-snacklist or e2e web build, a new browser context per run (the draft and the usage answer live in the browser's storage), a fresh email per run (`newEmail(...)`) because the seeded users already have a household. Viewport 390x844 or 480x900.

## Gotchas

- `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0` builds the flag-off screens the e2e specs see: „Ich habe schon ein Konto“ and „Einladung einfügen“ on the start screen, the login screen and „Willkommen“, no „Bestehendem Haushalt beitreten“, no name step, no QR code on invite cards, and a link's join screen that waits for „Haushalt beitreten“.
- Signed out, `/` is the start screen, not the sign-in. The sign-in shows first only after a sign-out, after an account deletion, for an invite link, for a `next` link (an AI assistant's access page, an app link) and when `/login` is opened. Specs that sign in go to `/login` themselves.
- The draft is per device, not per account. A new browser context has none; one context reused across recipes resumes the last one. Discard it with the X or start a new context.
- The step order is the draft's, never the address's: `page.goto('/start/<step>')` lands on the draft's step, and without a draft on `/start` (signed out) or `/welcome` (signed in).
- The usage step and the consent screen are the same question; whichever comes first answers it for the device. A signed-out guided start therefore never shows `/analytics-consent`, and `answerConsent` (which waits for that URL) does not fit it.
- The progress always counts 7 steps. From „Willkommen“ it starts at „Schritt 2 von 7“ and skips 6 (no account step).
- The prefilled household name is not in the DOM until the field is edited (so `toHaveValue` on it fails).
- The invite step on the web shows the card and „Teilen“ only where the browser has Web Share; headless Chromium has none. The phones open the share sheet at once. „Widerrufen“ is only on the invite sheet „Leute einladen“ (the switcher sheet's household page) and on „Einladungen“ (`/more/invites`, which the users' build opens only by link: More has no household rows under list-declutter).
- „<Name> ist beigetreten“ needs the realtime subscription; it shows only while the invite step is open, not on Mehr → „Einladungen“ (used person invites disappear there; who joined with a group link shows under „Wer ist beigetreten“, household-invites `joins`).
- Someone already in a household who opens an invite joins it beside their others, without a warning (job 169b); back or „Abbrechen“ on the join screen forgets a pending invite link; 10 wrong codes in an hour block joining for the hour (household-invites).
- There is no newsletter or email opt-in, no import from other apps and no referral question in the guided start (DECISIONS job 168b item 13).
- Fake-OIDC e2e specs (provider sign-in lands on the join screen) need the fake issuer reachable from the Supabase container and time out on the Hetzner boxes; trust CI for them.
