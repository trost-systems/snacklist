# More tab, help, guides, legal pages

The app has two tabs for users, „Liste“ and „Mehr“ (EN "List", "More"), and a third, „Verlauf“ / "History" (the grocery log, see grocery-log), between them only with the flag `grocery-log`, off for everyone since 2026-11-22 (Peter HP2, job 401; `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=grocery-log"` for a drive): on phones a raised pill floating centred above the bottom edge (rounded under RD1, `redesign-rd1` full; the selected tab on a honey marker; pages scroll on behind it), a left rail from 840 px width. Since job 85 (design "M-P2") „Mehr“ is a list of inset groups (cards with a 2 px contour): each row is an icon in a rounded tile (a hexagon before RD1) and one line of text, with no hint below it (the one exception is „Einkauf starten“). No group has a heading (job 203 removed the last one, „Rechtliches“). The legal rows (Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung, Nutzungsbedingungen, Lizenzen) are a group of their own directly on More, so the imprint is two taps from the list (Mehr, Impressum). Sign-out stands alone, in the same plain style as the other rows (not red); the version line ends the list. Entries either open a page inside the app (on wide screens beside the list, on phones as a full page with a back button) or open a page on the web in a new browser tab (those rows end in an external-link icon instead of a chevron). „Nutzungsdaten“ / "Usage data" is a page of its own with the usage-data switch.

## Sub-features

- `tabs` the tabs (two for users, three with `grocery-log`) and the wide-screen rail.
- `more-list` every More entry in order (below).
- `more-update-card` (key `more-update-card`) a lemon card on top of the list when an update is ready („Update bereit“ / „Neue Version da“ with „Neu starten“ / „Aktualisieren“ and a close button „Später“). No build checks for updates yet, so it never shows (job 85 stub); do not write copy about it.
- `more-wide` from 840 px More shows the entry list (360 px) on the left and the opened page beside it; „Zugänge“ is shown first by default.
- `help-link` „Hilfe & Kontakt“ opens the help page on snacklist.app (new tab).
- `more-usage-data` „Nutzungsdaten“ / "Usage data" (row key `more-usage-data`, only where the build can send analytics) opens the Nutzungsdaten page (`more-usage-data-page`, `/more/usage-data`, title „Nutzungsdaten“ / "Usage data"): only the switch (`analytics-switch`), no policy link.
- `more-legal` a group of direct rows without a heading, below „Hilfe & Kontakt“ and above „Abmelden“: „Impressum“ / "Imprint" (`more-imprint`), „Datenschutzerklärung“ / "Privacy policy" (`more-privacy-policy`), „Nutzungsbedingungen“ / "Terms of Use" (`more-terms`), on the iPhone app only „Lizenzvertrag von Apple (EULA)“ / "Apple’s License Agreement (EULA)" (`more-apple-eula`), „Lizenzen“ / "Licenses" (`more-licenses`). All but „Lizenzen“ open in a new tab; the app stays on More. `/more/legal` (the former Rechtliches page) lands on `/more`.
- `imprint-privacy` the imprint and the privacy policy on snacklist.app (new tab), from the legal rows; the imprint is two taps from the list (Mehr, Impressum). The login screen links both too.
- `terms` „Nutzungsbedingungen“ opens Snacklist's own terms of use on every platform: https://snacklist.app/nutzungsbedingungen (DE) or https://snacklist.app/en/terms (EN). On the iPhone app a second row, „Lizenzvertrag von Apple (EULA)“, opens Apple's standard EULA; Android and the web have no such row.
- `licenses` „Lizenzen“ on More opens Flutter's license page, titled Snacklist with the version.
- `version` last line „Version <version> (<build>)“ from `app/pubspec.yaml` (1.0.0 since 2026-11-24; a local build reads „Version 1.0.0 (1)“).
- `sign-out` „Abmelden“ ends the session and shows the login.
- `analytics-switch` „Nutzungsdaten und Fehlerberichte senden“ switch on the Nutzungsdaten page (only where the build can send analytics); on asks the consent question in a sheet first, off is one tap.
- `internal-tools` in a debug or profile build, or for a user in the server's tester group `internal` (job 337), of a build that can send, „Testfehler senden“ and, after the analytics yes, „Installations-ID kopieren: {id}“ show below the version. A long press on the version does nothing since job 337. Proved by `node journeys/tester-groups.mjs <evidence dir>` (not a `drive.sh` id): a customer's long press unlocks nothing, the group `internal` shows the button after a reload, the flag request after the yes carries `tester_groups`, the shared requests before it do not.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Bottom tab „Mehr“ / "More" on phones (under 840 px); left rail button „Mehr“ from 840 px. Tapping the already-open tab returns to its first page (the list of entries).
- Help page: „Mehr“ → „Hilfe & Kontakt“ / "Help & contact" opens https://snacklist.app/support (German UI) or https://snacklist.app/en/support (English UI) in a new browser tab. The page „Hilfe und Kontakt“ gives the support email address support@snacklist.app and answers FAQs (since 5 Nov first the waitlist and beta questions: the email address, installing the beta on iPhone and on Android, a wrong sign-up email, a missing invite; then inviting someone, join with a code, export data, delete account, Snacklist Lifetime (what happens to it when the last member leaves a household, and refunds through Apple or Google), usage data, Siri, Alexa, AI assistants, TestFlight/Google Play testing).
- Imprint: „Mehr“ → „Impressum“ (in the group below „Hilfe & Kontakt“) opens https://snacklist.app/impressum (DE) or https://snacklist.app/en/imprint (EN). Privacy policy: „Mehr“ → „Datenschutzerklärung“ opens https://snacklist.app/datenschutz (DE) or https://snacklist.app/en/privacy (EN). On the login screen the same two links sit under the form as „Impressum · Datenschutz“.
- Usage data: „Mehr“ → „Nutzungsdaten“ → switch „Nutzungsdaten und Fehlerberichte senden“.
- Terms: „Mehr“ → „Nutzungsbedingungen“ opens https://snacklist.app/nutzungsbedingungen (DE) or https://snacklist.app/en/terms (EN). Apple's EULA (iPhone app only): „Mehr“ → „Lizenzvertrag von Apple (EULA)“.
- Guides on snacklist.app (not linked from anywhere in the app; the site's nav links the index /de/anleitungen, /en/guides and the changelog, its footer also „Chronik“ / "History" (/history, /en/history, the release timeline), the help page links the Alexa and Claude guides and, while native Siri is held, the Siri Shortcut guide; the landing page links the Siri guide): German „Snacklist per Siri: Kurzbefehl einrichten“ at /de/anleitungen/siri-kurzbefehl, „Snacklist per Alexa: Skill einrichten“ at /de/anleitungen/alexa, „Snacklist mit Claude verbinden“ at /de/anleitungen/claude; English "Snacklist with Siri (German only for now)" at /en/guides/siri-shortcut (says Siri is German-only and points to the German guide), "Snacklist with Alexa: set up the skill" at /en/guides/alexa and "Connect Snacklist to Claude" at /en/guides/claude (both full English guides). The widget guides „Snacklist auf dem Home-Bildschirm: das Widget“ / "Snacklist on your Home Screen: the widget" (/de/anleitungen/widget, /en/guides/widget) are drafts: preview builds only, not in production (`site/content/guides/`).
- Deep links: /more, /more/access, /more/account, /more/invites, /more/lifetime, /more/settings, /more/settings/language, /more/notifications (to /more while neither of its flags is on), /more/usage-data, /more/household, /more/tips, /more/feedback and /more/feedback/<id>; /more/legal lands on /more, /more/join (More's join page until job 206) on /join.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Seeded household „Familie Brandt“, signed in, consent declined, on the list (phone 390x844, or 1366x1024 for the wide layout).
- For the external pages stub the site so the test never hits the live site: `page.context().route('https://snacklist.app/**', r => r.fulfill({contentType:'text/html', body:'<title>stub</title>'}))`.
- More is a lazy list taller than the window: wheel to a row before clicking it (`wheelTo` in `e2e/tests/helpers.ts`: `page.mouse.wheel(0, 250)` over the list until the row is above the tab pill; `openMore` does it for you).

- **Open More.** Click the tab. `openTab(page, 'Mehr')` (`getByRole('tab', {name:'Mehr', exact:true})`, on phones and on the rail alike since Flutter 3.50). URL `/more`, heading „Mehr“.
- **Open an entry.** `openMore(page, 'Einstellungen')` (taps Mehr, makes sure the list is shown, clicks `getByRole('button', {name: /^Einstellungen( |$)/})`). Rows are buttons named by their label alone (job 85: no hints; „Einkauf starten“ carries its hint in the name, so match it with `/^Einkauf starten/`), so match exactly, e.g. `getByRole('button', {name: 'Konto', exact: true})`; `openMore` matches the label followed by a space or the end.
- **Help page.** `wheelTo(page, getByRole('button',{name:/^Hilfe & Kontakt/}))`, then `const popup = page.waitForEvent('popup'); await row.click()`. Observable: `(await popup).url()` equals `https://snacklist.app/support`; the app URL is unchanged. Spec: `e2e/tests/help.spec.ts` (also the English URL and 1366 px).
- **Nutzungsdaten.** `openMore(page, 'Nutzungsdaten')`. Observable: URL `/more/usage-data`, heading „Nutzungsdaten“, the switch `getByRole('switch',{name:/^Nutzungsdaten und Fehlerberichte senden/})` (e2e and verify-snacklist builds), no „Datenschutzerklärung“ button. „Zurück“ returns to `/more`.
- **Legal rows, imprint, privacy policy, terms.** On More, `wheelTo` the button `{name:'Impressum', exact:true}`; no heading sits above it (`getByRole('heading',{name:'Rechtliches', exact:true})` has count 0 since job 203). The popup pattern with `getByRole('button',{name:'Impressum', exact:true})`, `{name:'Datenschutzerklärung', exact:true}` and `{name:'Nutzungsbedingungen', exact:true}` gives `https://snacklist.app/impressum`, `https://snacklist.app/datenschutz` and `https://snacklist.app/nutzungsbedingungen` (the Apple EULA row exists on iOS only); the app stays on `/more`. `page.goto('/more/legal')` ends on `/more`. Spec: `e2e/tests/shell-more.spec.ts` ("the Impressum is two taps from the list …"), `e2e/tests/legal.spec.ts` (login footer links).
- **Licenses.** On More click `getByRole('button',{name:'Lizenzen', exact:true})`. Observable: `page.locator('body').ariaSnapshot()` matches `/Snacklist[\s\S]*Flutter[\s\S]*bloc/`; „Zurück“ returns to More.
- **Version.** `page.getByText(/^Version \d+\.\d+\.\d+/)` visible at the end of the list.
- **Sign out.** Click `getByRole('button',{name:/^Abmelden/})`. Observable: `getByRole('textbox',{name:'E-Mail'})` visible, URL contains `/login`.
- **Wide layout.** `page.setViewportSize({width:1366,height:1024})`, click `getByRole('tab',{name:'Mehr',exact:true})`. Observable: the entries and Zugänge's first section heading (`getByText('Zugriffsschlüssel',{exact:true})` on the users' build; „MCP-Server“ with the switch `unified_keys` off) side by side; „Zurück“ buttons absent; clicking the button „Konto“ (exact) shows Konto alone (URL `/more/account`). At 820 px the tab pill returns.
- **Scripted.** `helpers/drive.sh more-help [--lang en]` on a plain launch: More's rows top to bottom against the order above (a Premium row allowed), no Apple EULA row on the web, „Hilfe & Kontakt“, „Impressum“, „Datenschutzerklärung“ and „Nutzungsbedingungen“ each opening its snacklist.app page in a new tab (stubbed, so only the URL counts) while the app stays on `/more`, and the version line from `app/pubspec.yaml`.
- Specs to run: `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/shell-more.spec.ts`, `tests/help.spec.ts`, `tests/legal.spec.ts`.

## Gotchas

- „Archivierte Haushalte“ shows only in builds with HOUSEHOLD_ARCHIVE (the e2e build has it; the verify-snacklist build and a normal user do not see it); archiving itself also needs the server's switch. „Hilfe & Kontakt“ is on for everyone now (the help page is published).
- The „Nutzungsdaten“ row and its page exist only when the build has an analytics key; without one More has no „Nutzungsdaten“ row. Production has one: the production web bundle carries a PostHog project key, and the store builds get it from the `release` environment secret (checked 2026-11-01). The e2e and verify-snacklist builds carry the stand-in key, so the switch shows there too.
- More is reachable only in a household (a user without one lands on the welcome screen).
- A plain launch, like users' builds since 2026-11-22, has two tabs and no „Einkauf starten“. `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0` builds the flag-off More (what the e2e specs see without flags): two tabs, no „Einkauf starten“, the household's name row and „Leute einladen“ after „Konto“, and „Feedback senden“ opening the form instead of „Feedback & Ideen“; `/more/feedback` then lands on `/more`. `VH_FLAGS_OFF=<flag>[,<flag>]` leaves out only those flags.
- The Premium row has no hint any more (job 85): neither „Freigeschaltet“ nor „Siri und KI-Assistenten“.
- On the users' build More reads, top to bottom (seen live 2026-11-25 on 448caf1d in German and English with `journeys/more-help.mjs`; household rows' absence seen live; „Einkauf starten“ gone since 2026-11-22, seen live on job/401-hp2-flags-off): Konto, „Snacklist Premium“ (only when the household or the account itself has Premium), Zugänge, Einstellungen, Nutzungsdaten, Tipps, Feedback & Ideen, Hilfe & Kontakt, Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung, Nutzungsbedingungen, Lizenzen, Abmelden (the iPhone app adds „Lizenzvertrag von Apple (EULA)“ before Lizenzen); no „Rechtliches“ heading, no household or join row.
- The tabs are role `tab` on the phone's bar and on the rail alike (driven 2026-11-13 at 390 and 1366 px). Use `openTab`.
- Pages open by deep link: back goes to the More list, not out of the app.
- Opening snacklist.app pages stays external everywhere; never an in-app browser. Real site pages are not reachable offline; stub them.
- Debug builds and users in the tester group `internal` see „Testfehler senden“ and, while usage data is on, „Installations-ID kopieren: {id}“ / "Copy installation ID: {id}" below the version line, with no long press and no „Interne Installation“ line (removed in job 337); test accounts and the RevenueCat tester household (App Review) are internal for analytics but see neither in a release build, so a plain drive shows none (job 337 review 0). Not for copy.
- Wide More page title: „Mehr“ heading; language page adds a back button next to its title.
- More has no section headings since job 203 (until then „Rechtliches“ / "Legal" stood above the legal rows). „Nutzungsdaten“ (the usage-data switch) and „Datenschutzerklärung“ (the policy) are different rows: the first is a setting, the second the legal text.
