# Categories

Every household starts with 11 categories (fruit and veg, bread, chilled, …, „Sonstiges“ last); a household with a Baumarkt has seven more (`cat-baumarkt`). Users do not see them today: the flag `hide-categories` is full since 2026-11-22, so the list has no category dividers, the add and edit sheets have no category chip, and „Anpassen“ has no „Ohne Laden“ row (`cat-hidden`). Categories still sort the rows underneath, and the classifier still files every item. The screens below are what a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` build shows (and the API and MCP still reach them): there they group the list under small dividers, and under Liste → „Anpassen“ → „Ohne Laden“ (job 78; before, Mehr → „Kategorien verwalten“) the household can add its own, rename any, delete any except „Sonstiges“, and put them in a different order. That order (the "route") sorts the „Ohne Laden“ section and the categories a store has no order for; stores have their own order too (see stores and walk order).

## Sub-features

- `cat-seed` a new household gets these categories, in this order. DE (as stored and shown in German): Obst & Gemüse, Brot & Backwaren, Kühlregal, Fleisch/Wurst/Fisch, Vorrat & Konserven, Süßes & Snacks, Getränke, Tiefkühl, Haushalt, 👶 Drogerie, Sonstiges. EN (shown when the app language is English and the category was not renamed): Fruit & veg, Bread & bakery, Chilled, Meat & fish, Pantry, Sweets & snacks, Drinks, Frozen, Household, 👶 Drugstore, Other. A renamed or self-made category shows exactly what the household typed, in both languages. Each category has an emoji: the one its name starts with, else the seeded one (🥕 🥖 🥛 🥩 🥫 🍫 🧃 🧊 🧽 🧴 🛒, the Baumarkt ones 🎨 🚿 💡 🔨 🔩 🪵 🌱; „👶 Drogerie“ shows 👶, a renamed Drogerie without it 🧴), else 🧺 for a self-made one. Item rows never show it: a row shows the item's own picture (Klopapier 🧻, Zahnbürste 🪥) or the plain placeholder, a rounded square under RD1 (stores-walk-order `sw-rows`); the category rows in „Anpassen“ show it before each name; the divider text drops a leading emoji.
- `cat-baumarkt` the Baumarkt categories (job 151). The first store named „Baumarkt“, „Heimwerkermarkt“, "Hardware store" or "DIY store" (any case or spacing; from onboarding's „Baumarkt“ chip, the store options or „Laden hinzufügen“) gives the household seven more categories, just before „Sonstiges“ in the route and in every store: Farben & Lacke 🎨, Sanitär 🚿, Elektro & Leuchtmittel 💡, Werkzeug 🔨, Schrauben & Befestigung 🔩, Holz & Baustoffe 🪵, Garten & Pflanzen 🌱 (EN: Paint & varnish, Plumbing, Electrical & lighting, Tools, Screws & fixings, Wood & building, Garden & plants). Households without a Baumarkt never get them. A category of the same name the household already has (its own „Werkzeug“) becomes the Baumarkt one, keeping its place; one the household deleted is not created again by a second Baumarkt. Deleting the Baumarkt store keeps all seven: they stay under „Ohne Laden“ until deleted one by one, which only a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` build (or the API) can do. Nothing else is renamed or reordered. Server: `public.hardware_categories()`, `seed_hardware_categories`; pgTAP `supabase/tests/hardware_categories.test.sql`. Journey: see `sw-baumarkt` in stores-walk-order.md.
- `cat-open` (flag `hide-categories` off only) the list header's „Anpassen“ / "Edit" (top right) opens „Läden“; its last row, „Ohne Laden“ / "No store" (muted, on its edge like the store rows since job 148, with „Kategorien für Artikel ohne Laden“ / "Categories for items without a store" below it, outside the row's shape since job 148), opens the screen titled „Ohne Laden“ / "No store" (route `/edit/none`) with the hint „In dieser Reihenfolge stehen die Kategorien unter „Ohne Laden“ und bei Läden ohne eigene Reihenfolge. Tippe auf eine Kategorie, um sie umzubenennen.“ (EN: "Items without a store, and stores without their own order, follow this order. Tap a category to rename it."). This is the only way in: a hold or right click on a divider on the list does nothing (job 171). A store's details (`/edit/<id>`, tap the store on „Läden“) show the same rows in that store's order, with the same rename, add and delete. The rows show no numbers since job 148: the order shows as the order. A screen reader hears each row's place after its name, „2 von 11“ / "2 of 11" (the semantics hint, `aria-description` on the web; the row's name stays the category's name).
- `cat-add` button „Kategorie hinzufügen“ / "Add category" (bottom right, a yellow button on its edge like the rows since job 148; rounded under RD1) opens a dialog of the same title with a field „Name“ / "Name" (at most 60 characters, with a counter) and buttons „Abbrechen“ / „Speichern“ ("Cancel" / "Save"). The new category goes after the last category, just before „Sonstiges“ when that is last, in the route and in every store's order (live: „Baby“ landed between „👶 Drogerie“ and „Sonstiges“).
- `cat-duplicate` names are unique per household ignoring case and spacing (job 90): „baby“, „ BABY“ and „Baby“ are one name. Adding a name the household has, or renaming onto another category's name, keeps the dialog open with „Eine Kategorie „<Name>“ gibt es schon.“ / "A category named “<name>” already exists." under the field (the existing spelling, as shown: in English an unrenamed seed shows its English label, so "drinks" is taken by „Getränke“ too); typing clears it, „Abbrechen“ closes. A category may change its own case („Kühlregal“ → „KÜHLREGAL“). The server enforces the same (unique index on the name key; `create_category` returns the existing category), so a direct API write cannot make a duplicate either. Driven 2026-11-06: „ KINDER“ next to „Kinder“ and „getränke“ onto „Getränke“ were refused in the dialog, and a direct SQL insert of „kinder“ hit the unique index. Only when two devices race past the app's check does it differ: an add quietly gives the existing category, a rename shows the same text as a snackbar (with the typed spelling), and both dialogs have closed already.
- `cat-rename` a tap on a category opens „Kategorie umbenennen“ / "Rename category" with the name filled in; „Speichern“ saves. An empty name or an unchanged name does nothing. „Sonstiges“ can be renamed too.
- `cat-delete` the trash icon (tooltip „<Name> löschen“ / "Delete <name>") opens a dialog „<Name> löschen“ saying „Sie verschwindet aus allen Läden.“ / "It disappears from every store." (job 78) with a dropdown „Artikel verschieben nach“ / "Move items to" (default „Sonstiges“) and „Abbrechen“ / „Löschen“ ("Delete"). All its items, open and ticked, move to the chosen category; the move teaches the classifier nothing, and corrections learned for the deleted category go with it. „Sonstiges“ has no trash icon.
- `cat-reorder` the handle on each row („<Name> verschieben“ / "Move <name>") drags a category to a new place in the route. The list itself does not reorder (job 78), and its dividers do not open this screen (job 171).
- `cat-live` additions, renames and order changes show on a partner's device without a reload.
- `cat-hidden` flag `hide-categories` (job 350; full since 2026-11-22 for beta and launch, N-355; this is what users get): no category shows anywhere a user looks. The list has no category dividers in any store section, also in trip mode, under „Zuletzt verwendet“ and behind the add sheet; a section's rows run on with a rule between each and none under the last. The order is the same as with the flag off: categories still sort the rows underneath (the store's walk order, else the route), only the dividers go. The add sheet (its header without add-feedback-list, the added item's details with it, the compact chip row) and the edit sheet show the store chip and no „Kategorie“ chip; an item still gets its category from the classifier and keeps it on a save. „Läden“ (Anpassen) has no „Ohne Laden“ row and no hint line above the stores (the hint named categories; „Noch keine Läden …“ still shows without stores); a store's screen shows its name field (and its chain card with `open-store-app`) and nothing else: no category rows, no „Kategorie hinzufügen“. `/edit/none` goes back to „Läden“. The tip „Sortiert wie dein Laden“ never shows, on the list or under Mehr → Tipps. The API and MCP still read and write categories; delete-account and leave-household dialogs still say categories are deleted (they are). Tests: `app/test/list/hide_categories_test.dart`, `stores_screen_test.dart`, `store_details_screen_test.dart`, `app/test/tips/`.
- `cat-errors` a refused change shows „Die Änderung konnte nicht gespeichert werden.“ / "The change could not be saved."; a reorder without connection shows „Die Reihenfolge lässt sich nur mit Verbindung ändern.“ / "Reordering needs a connection."

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Users today: nowhere. „Anpassen“ top right opens „Läden“ without an „Ohne Laden“ row, and a store's screen shows only its name (`cat-hidden`).
- On a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` build: tap „Anpassen“ top right, then „Ohne Laden“ (the last row). A store's row opens the same actions in that store's order (per-store category order: Liste → „Anpassen“ → the store).
- Holding or right clicking a divider on the list does nothing (flag-off build; users have no dividers).

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- A build with `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` (export it for `doctor.sh` and `drive.sh` too). On a plain launch only `cat-hidden` is reachable: „Anpassen“ shows „Läden“ with no button matching `/^Ohne Laden/`, `page.goto('<url>/edit/none')` comes back to `/edit`, and the list has no level-2 headings (`node journeys/hide-categories.mjs <dir> --hidden`).
- Signed in on the list. For a deterministic route use a fresh account: `signUpWithHousehold(page, newEmail('categories'))` (no stores; 11 seeded categories). A seeded „Familie Brandt“ may have renamed or extra categories: read the screen first.
- An item without a store to see route order (in a household without stores every item is one: e2e adds Windeln and Bier).

- **Open.** `openStoreDetails(page, null)` (`e2e/tests/catalog.ts`: Liste, „Anpassen“, „Ohne Laden“). Observable: heading „Ohne Laden“; the 11 categories as buttons in route order; `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Sonstiges löschen' })` count 0.
- **Add.** `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Kategorie hinzufügen' }).click()`, `typeInto(page.getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Name' }), 'Baby')`, `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Speichern' }).click()`. Observable: `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Baby', exact: true })` visible, listed just above „Sonstiges“.
- **Duplicate.** Repeat the add with „ BABY“. Observable: `page.getByText('Eine Kategorie „Baby“ gibt es schon.').first()` visible in the dialog; after „Abbrechen“ one button matching `/^baby$/i`. Rename a category to „getränke“: the same message with „Getränke“, the textbox „Name“ still visible.
- **Rename.** `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Baby', exact: true }).click()`, `typeInto(name field, 'Babysachen')`, „Speichern“. Observable: a button named „Babysachen“.
- **Reorder.** `dragHandle(page, 'Getränke verschieben', 'Obst & Gemüse verschieben')` (from `e2e/tests/catalog.ts`). Then `openTab(page, 'Liste')` (the tab goes back to its first page, the list): `groupHeadings(page)` shows the new route order for items without a store.
- **Move an item in.** `openEdit(page, 'Windeln')`, `pickInSheet(page, 'Kategorie', 'Baby')`, `saveEdit(page)`: the heading „Baby“ shows.
- **Delete.** `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Babysachen löschen' }).click()`, `page.getByRole('button', { name: /Artikel verschieben nach/ }).click()`, `page.getByRole('menuitem', { name: '👶 Drogerie' }).click()`, `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Löschen', exact: true }).click()`. Observable: the category button is gone; its items sit under „👶 Drogerie“ after `openTab(page, 'Liste')` and after `page.reload()`.
- **Spec.** `e2e/tests/stores.spec.ts` ("categories: add, move an item there, rename, reorder, delete with reassignment" and the live-sync test). Run `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/stores.spec.ts`.

## Gotchas

- A plain launch has `hide-categories` on, as users do, so none of the recipes above is reachable there: no „Ohne Laden“ row, no category rows on a store's screen, and `openStoreDetails(page, null)` lands back on „Läden“. Build with `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` for them. The e2e specs run flag-off and still cover them.

- Rows are buttons whose name is the category name, except that a leading emoji is left out of the row's name (`{ name: 'Drogerie', exact: true }`; its trash „👶 Drogerie löschen“ and handle „👶 Drogerie verschieben“ keep it; driven 2026-11-04); „Baby“ with `exact: true` avoids hitting „Babysachen“ or the drag handle „Baby verschieben“.
- The new category dialog's title and the button share the text „Kategorie hinzufügen“; wait for the textbox „Name“.
- The delete dropdown opens as a menu: pick with `getByRole('menuitem', …)`.
- Category changes persist and change the divider order in „Ohne Laden“ and in every store; use a fresh account for order assertions.
- Seeded category names are stored in German. An English UI shows English names only for unrenamed seed categories; the Playwright harness runs de-DE.
- Adding a category while the household has stores also places it in every store's order: after the store's last shown category, or just before it when that is „Sonstiges“ (dm, whose hidden categories follow „Sonstiges“, gets it before „Sonstiges“).
- Deleting a category moves ticked items too (they are under „Zuletzt verwendet“), and puts the deleted seeded category's key, and any keys it had absorbed, on the target so the classifier keeps finding a home for the old words (a self-made category passes nothing on; job 109). unverified in the UI (SQL only).
- Names are limited to 60 characters: the field stops there (live: 65 typed, 60 kept).
