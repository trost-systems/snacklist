# Automatic category (the classifier)

Every new item gets a category without the user choosing one: the server looks at the name and files it. Users never see it: with `hide-categories` (full since 2026-11-22) the list has no category dividers and the add and edit sheets no category chip, so the category only orders the rows inside each store, and users cannot correct or pick it in the app. What follows about dividers, the „Wird einsortiert …“ heading and chips is what a build with `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` (or `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0`) shows: there the new row shows under the guessed category at once. Unless the user picked a category, the server classifies the item again when it saves the add, after the user's earlier edits, so a correction made just before counts (job 126); an add without a guess (offline) is classified then too. It uses what the household taught it, then a built-in word list (German: about 1,640 terms with compound words, plurals, umlauts, quantities in front of the name; English since job 470: about 1,150 words, `supabase/classifier/dictionary.en.tsv`, most of them synonyms of a German term so milk and Milch share a key), then synonyms and typos, and finally „Sonstiges“, unless the app sent its own guess for the add (`put_item`'s `p_category_guess`, job 470): a word the server does not know keeps the app's category, marked `categorized_via` `device` and not learned. The list is the same for every language: a name lands in one aisle whatever the app language (so "paprika" is produce, the German Paprika). The same server logic runs for every way of adding: typing in the app, voice dictation, the MCP assistant connection, Siri (voice endpoint) and Alexa. On a build with categories shown a user can override the guess, and the household remembers the correction; on the users' build the household still learns from corrections made through MCP, the API or an older app build.

## Sub-features

- `cl-auto` an item without a category is filed in the matching category (under its divider with `hide-categories` off), e.g. Hafermilch → „Kühlregal“, Apfelsaft → „Getränke“, Zahnbürste → „👶 Drogerie“, Vollkornbrot → „Brot & Backwaren“, Spülmaschinentabs → „Haushalt“, Fischstäbchen → „Tiefkühl“, Gummibärchen → „Süßes & Snacks“, Kichererbsen → „Vorrat & Konserven“, Rinderhack → „Fleisch/Wurst/Fisch“, „Aepfel 2 kg“ → „Obst & Gemüse“ (name „Aepfel“, quantity „2 kg“). The word list is German; English words work only where they are synonyms ("toilet paper" → „Haushalt“).
- `cl-baumarkt` about 270 DIY words (job 151). In a household with a Baumarkt (feature categories, `cat-baumarkt`) they land in its seven categories: Schrauben, Dübel → „Schrauben & Befestigung“, Glühbirne, Kabelbinder → „Elektro & Leuchtmittel“, Wandfarbe, Pinsel, Abdeckband → „Farben & Lacke“, Duschkopf, Silikon → „Sanitär“, Akkuschrauber → „Werkzeug“, Blumenerde, Dünger → „Garten & Pflanzen“, Holzlatten → „Holz & Baustoffe“; the store guess is the Baumarkt. Without a Baumarkt they land in „Haushalt“ (as Glühbirne, Kabelbinder and Blumenerde did before; Schrauben, Dübel and Pinsel were „Sonstiges“ until job 151), and the add sheet's catalogue does not suggest them. Batterien, Grillkohle, Klebeband and Rohrreiniger stay „Haushalt“ in both.
- `cl-fallback` an unknown word lands under „Sonstiges“ / "Other" (the household's fallback category), shown last unless the household reordered its categories.
- `cl-preview` (flag `add-feedback-list` off only: users have no chips while typing, and with `hide-categories` full no category after the add either, no chip and none in the details line, `compact_details.dart`, `add_sheet.dart`; with `hide-categories` off the details line after the add shows a chip with the category's name, accessible name „Kategorie: <name>“) while typing, the add sheet's category chip shows the guess, muted with an "A" mark (accessible name „Kategorie: automatisch, 👶 Drogerie“ for „Zahnbürste“), and the store chip the store guess („Laden: automatisch, dm“); with nothing guessed the chip shows „Kategorie“, named „Kategorie: automatisch“ / "Category: automatic".
- `cl-sorting` (only with `hide-categories` off: the „Wird einsortiert …“ heading is a category divider) an item added without a category (offline, or Enter before the sheet had an answer) shows at the top of its section under a divider „Wird einsortiert …“ / "Sorting…", then moves under its category. Online adds arrive categorized and skip it.
- `cl-learn-edit` (needs the edit sheet's category chip: `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories`) the user moves an item to another category in the edit sheet (chip „Kategorie: …“, pick, „Fertig“); the list itself has no drag since job 78. The household learns it: the next add of that item (and its plural, e.g. „Zahnbürste“ → „Zahnbürsten“) lands there, also right after the correction while it is still on its way to the server (job 126).
- `cl-learn-pick` a category chosen on the add sheet's category chip (before a pick named „Kategorie: automatisch, <guess>“ / "Category: automatic, <guess>", or „Kategorie: automatisch“ without a guess) is learned when it differs from the guess; „Rückgängig“ on that add removes the item and the lesson again. With `add-feedback-list` on and `add-sheet-compact` and `hide-categories` off, the pick is made after the add in the details line instead: that pick is learned like an edit, and „Rückgängig“ on the add keeps it: the next add of the name is corrected in its details (job 322 reverted job 315's take-back, driven 2026-11-20, quick-add `qa-added-details`). Users today have no category pick at all (quick-add `qa-hide-categories`). Any other delete keeps what a move taught (move, delete, add again lands where it was moved).
- `cl-variants` spelling variants, regional and English synonyms and typos land with the word they mean, the name stays as typed: „Karrotten“ → „Obst & Gemüse“ (Möhren), „Paradeiser“ → „Obst & Gemüse“, „Semmeln“ → „Brot & Backwaren“, „Mlich“ → „Kühlregal“, „Schokolde“ → „Süßes & Snacks“, "toilet paper" → „Haushalt“.
- `cl-homographs` words that fold onto another word's key keep their own category, read from the spelling as typed (job 282): "beer" → „Getränke“ (not Beere), "sage" → „Obst & Gemüse“ (Salbei, not Säge), "rolls" → „Brot & Backwaren“ (Brötchen, not Rolle), „Schäufele“ → „Fleisch/Wurst/Fisch“ (not Schaufel); „Beeren“, „Säge“, „Rolle Küchenpapier“ and „Schaufel“ stay where they were. „Schäuferle“ and „Schäuferla“ are Schäufele; "spring rolls" and "egg rolls" are Frühlingsrollen („Tiefkühl“), "cinnamon rolls" bread rolls. „Rohrnudeln“ is „Brot & Backwaren“ since job 282 (it was „Vorrat & Konserven“ through „Nudeln“).
- `cl-tail-guards` seven words a short tail, a stem or a synonym sent to the wrong aisle now land right (job 349): Spiegel, Handspiegel and Kleiderbügel are „Haushalt“ (not Drogerie through `gel`), Bratpfanne and Grillpfanne „Haushalt" (not Fleisch through `braten`), Topf, Töpfe and Kochtopf „Haushalt“ while Topfen stays „Kühlregal“, Medikament „Drogerie“ (not Fleisch through `ente`), "tea light(s)" and "tealight" „Haushalt“ (not Vorrat through `tea`), Kühlpack „Haushalt“ like Kühlakku (not Drogerie through Kühlpad), Zeckenzange „Drogerie“ (not Werkzeug through `zange`). Since the tail guard (job 349 review 0) Element, Pigment, Dokument, Instrument, Vogel, Igel and Kegel are „Sonstiges“, Siegel and Segel „Haushalt“, Bräter and Bügeleisen „Haushalt“. pgTAP: `supabase/tests/classifier_misfires.test.sql`.
- `cl-variant-learn` a correction for one spelling holds for the word's synonyms and spellings in that household only: „Karrotten“ moved to „Getränke“, then „Möhren“ lands in „Getränke“.
- `cl-household-only` learning is per household; another household with the same word still gets the default category.
- `cl-delete-category` deleting a category moves its items and forgets what was learned for it; the move itself teaches nothing.
- `cl-same-everywhere` the app, the MCP assistant connection, Siri and Alexa all add through the same server logic (categories and learned corrections apply the same).
- `cl-english` (job 470) English names land in their aisle for every household: "eggplant" and "aubergine" → „Obst & Gemüse“, "nappies" → „Drogerie“, "peanut butter" → „Vorrat & Konserven“ (not „Kühlregal“ by its head), "orange juice" → „Getränke“, "frozen peas" → „Tiefkühl“, "cat food" → „Haushalt“. `supabase/classifier/expected.en.tsv` (285 entries, written apart from the word list) is checked by `sweep.sh` in the db gate, and by `catalog_language_test.dart` on the device.
- `cl-device-guess` (job 470) an add the server cannot classify keeps the category the app guessed instead of „Sonstiges“ (`categorized_via` `device`); a word the server knows gets the server's category whatever the app sent, and nothing is learned from a guess.
- `cl-store` a new item also gets a store: the one the household remembers for that name, else the store of a store's own brand (Balea → dm), else dm for drugstore items when the household has dm. The add sheet and the server add path (MCP, Siri, Alexa, dictation) guess the same way; "Windeln Größe 4" lands in dm. Adds made in the app also try, after the brand and before dm, the store the household uses most for the item's category, and an item set to „Ohne Laden“ stays without one (stores-walk-order `sw-store-memory`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Type an item in the floating add field at the bottom (the list scrolls on under it) and add it: it appears in the guessed store's section (see `cl-store`, otherwise under „Ohne Laden“), placed by its category, with no category heading.
- Users cannot correct or choose the category in the app today. With `hide-categories` off (`VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories`): hold the item (or right click it) to open „Bearbeiten“, tap the chip „Kategorie: …“, pick a category, tap „Fertig“; or right after adding, tap the category in the item's details (without `add-feedback-list`: the category chip before adding).

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- A fresh account in its own household: `signUpWithHousehold(page, newEmail('learn'), { stores: chainStores })` (dm, Lidl, EDEKA; without `stores` the household has none, since job 64). Learning is per household and persistent, so do not reuse a household for first-guess checks.
- Seeded categories untouched (a renamed category changes the divider text).
- The heading and chip steps below need categories shown: launch with `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` (or `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0`, which the e2e specs use). On a plain launch check the category through the API instead: `api.from('items').select('name, categories(name)')` (as `journeys/quick-add.mjs` does), and `page.getByRole('heading', { level: 2 })` holds no category name.

- **Preview.** (Build with `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0`, quick-add Preconditions.) Type `Zahnbürste` into the add field without Enter: `addSheet(page).getByRole('button', { name: 'Kategorie: automatisch, 👶 Drogerie' })` and `{ name: 'Laden: automatisch, dm' }` show in the sheet (with no stores, as on the demo-familie baseline, the store chip reads „Laden: automatisch“; driven 2026-11-04).
- **Auto-categorize.** `addItem(page, 'Hafermilch')`, then `expectGroup(page, 'Hafermilch', 'Kühlregal')` (nearest level-2 heading above the row, `e2e/tests/catalog.ts`). Observable: also `groupHeadings(page)`. A word not in the list (`Quietscheentchen`) → heading „Sonstiges“.
- **Correct in the edit sheet.** `setCategory(page, 'Hafermilch', 'Getränke')` (`e2e/tests/catalog.ts`). Observable: `expectGroup(page, 'Hafermilch', 'Getränke')` (add `Apfelsaft` first so Getränke has a divider).
- **Correct in the sheet.** `openEdit(page, 'Zahnbürste')`, `pickInSheet(page, 'Kategorie', 'Haushalt')`, `saveEdit(page)`; heading becomes „Haushalt“.
- **Learn.** `tick(page, 'Zahnbürste')` then `addItem(page, 'Zahnbürsten')` → `expectGroup(page, 'Zahnbürsten', 'Haushalt')`. Or delete the first item (`deleteItem`) and add `2 Hafermilch` → „Getränke“.
- **Variants.** `addItem` for `Karrotten`, `Paradeiser`, `Semmeln`, `Mlich`, `Schokolde`, `toilet paper`; headings as in `cl-variants`; the row names stay as typed. Second view: `api.from('items').select('name, categorized_via')` returns exact / synonym / exact / fuzzy / fuzzy / synonym for those names.
- **Homographs.** `cd .cursor/skills/verify-snacklist && node journeys/classifier-homographs.mjs <dir>` on a fresh `demo-familie` (it leaves its 11 items on the list, so a second run on the same seed finds them „schon auf der Liste“; it checks the category in the database on a plain launch, and the heading above each row only on a `VH_FLAGS_OFF=hide-categories` build, since `hide-categories` went full on 2026-11-22; driven 2026-11-18 on the users' build of that day): adds beer, Bier, Beeren, rolls, Rolle Küchenpapier, sage, Salbei, Säge, Schäufele, Schaufel and Rohrnudeln with Enter and checks the heading above each row and its category in the database (demo-familie has no Baumarkt, so Säge and Schaufel are „Haushalt“). pgTAP: `supabase/tests/classifier_homographs.test.sql`, with a Baumarkt.
- **Variant learn.** Move `Karrotten` to „Getränke“ in the sheet, then `addItem(page, 'Möhren')` → „Getränke“. A second account adding `Möhren` gets „Obst & Gemüse“.
- **API view.** `apiAs(email)` then `rpc('add_items', { p_household, p_names: ['Hafermilch', '1 Packung Kaffee'] })` returns name and quantity split (`Kaffee`, `1 Packung`).
- **Spec.** `e2e/tests/classifier.spec.ts` (three tests: learning, dictionary spot checks, spelling variants). Run `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/classifier.spec.ts`.

## Gotchas

- Category dividers show only with `hide-categories` off. The divider shows its category without a leading emoji, but the heading's accessible name keeps the full name ("👶 Drogerie"); Playwright `getByRole('heading', { level: 2 })` reads the full name, as the specs assert.
- Online adds show under the sheet's guessed category at once, so „Wird einsortiert …“ usually never shows; the server's answer can still move the row (a correction just before). Wait for the heading with `expectGroup`, which polls.
- The add sheet's guess goes with an add only as a fallback (`cl-device-guess`, job 470): the server keeps it just for a word it does not know, and learns nothing from it. Only a category picked on the chip is taken as the user's choice. A probe that delays the correction's request by 3 s (`page.route` on the items PATCH) shows the re-add still lands in the corrected category (job 126).
- Typo matching is deliberately shy: never under 5 letters, same first letter; only neighbouring-letter swaps for 5-letter words, one edit for longer words, two edits from 8 letters up; and only when nearby words agree on a category. „Mehk“ or „Kaffe“ stay in „Sonstiges“ on purpose.
- Matching folds case, umlauts and one plural ending. Where that folds two words of different categories together (beer and Beere, sage and Säge), the spelling as typed decides, umlauts included (`public.homograph_pick`), for the category and for the canonical key the duplicate hint matches on, on every path (`public.canonical_key(name)`: adds, renames, catalog backfills): Schäufele next to Schaufel and Beeren next to a renamed "beer" get no hint (`journeys/classifier-homographs-dup-hint.mjs`, review 0). That is the note after Enter in the sheet before AS1 and AM2 (`VH_FLAGS_OFF=add-sheet-compact,add-match-rows`). Users see a duplicate as the add sheet's match rows (quick-add `qa-match-rows`), whose key is the app's `canonicalKeyOf` (`app/lib/list/catalog/suggest_entries.dart`). Since job 473 it reads the spelling the same way, from the homographs the catalog now carries (`catalog_rows()` / `catalog_delta`'s `homographs`, bundled as `catalogHomographs`): „Schäufele“ next to Schaufel and „Beeren“ next to a renamed „beer“ offer no merge, „Bier“ next to „beer“ does (driven 2026-11-26 on the job 473 branch's release web build with `journeys/classifier-homographs-dup-hint.mjs`: PASS; on main since #748, ad1b218b). Before it (driven 2026-11-26 on e6697500) typed „Schäufele“ offered „Zusammenführen, 2× Schaufel“. „Töpfe“ and „Topf“ get different keys on both sides (the server's canonical for a second spelling of one category on a shared form), so neither the hint nor the rows join them. A household correction is still stored by the folded key, so a correction of Schaufel also holds for Schäufele in that household. The add sheet's on-device category guess (`on-device-catalog`, `input-stable-rows`) does not read the spelling yet, although the catalog has the homographs since job 473: it shows the German word's category until the server answers. A short head matches the end of any word, so its tail hit counts only when the typed word really ends in it (`classify_item_detail`): a term that lost its final e to the stem needs its spelling (Pekingente, Frischkäse, Mülltüte, Blumenerde match; Medikament, Element, Dokument, Instrument do not) and gel does not follow a vowel (Duschgel matches; Spiegel, Kegel, Vogel, Igel do not), so gels with a vowel before them are listed (Pflegegel, Energiegel, Aloegel, Massagegel, Wärmegel, Cremegel, Kältegel). Words that need a category of their own are listed (Spiegel, Bügel, Bügeleisen, Siegel, Bräter, Medikament, Zeckenzange, Kühlpack, Pfanne, Spiegelei); the same stem for two words (Topf, Topfen) is a homograph. Names with numbers fold the numbers away: a learned „Xylophon“ also catches „Xylophon-123“. Use distinct words in tests.
- Household learning beats the word list. A test that moves an item changes later adds in the same household; use a new household per test.
- Dictionary-matched compounds prefer the head word: „Ziegenmilch“ → „Kühlregal“, „Milchschokolade“ → „Süßes & Snacks“.
- Shown category names follow the app language only for seeded, unrenamed categories; the harness runs German.
- A brand that stands for a product becomes that product: „Pampers“ is the item Windeln with the brand Pampers, so it lands in „👶 Drogerie“. Own brands of dm, Lidl and Edeka also guess that store.
- Renaming an item in the edit sheet keeps its category; the classifier runs on adds only.
- Typed (online or offline, job 104), a number or count word at the end („Karotten 2“, „Karotten drei“) becomes the quantity only when the name is known to the word list or the household; a count word up front („zwei Gurken“, „two lemons“) always does, except in names such as „Drei Glocken Spaghetti“; see quick-add. Said (dictation, Siri, Alexa), a count after the item is always the quantity unless a size word comes before it („Gurken zwei“, job 89), and a count word up front is the quantity except in the same names as typed („Drei Glocken Nudeln“, „Seven Up“; job 159).
- Offline (add with no network, `cutOff` from the e2e helpers), with `hide-categories` off: the item shows under „Wird einsortiert …“ until the connection is back, then under its category (driven 2026-11-02: Linsen → „Vorrat & Konserven“; no spec).
- `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0` builds the flag-off screen (what the e2e specs see): category chips in the add sheet before the add (`cl-preview`), and no store by category (`cl-store`), so a grocery without a remembered or brand store stays under „Ohne Laden“.
