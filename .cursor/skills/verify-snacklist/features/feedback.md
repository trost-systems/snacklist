# Feedback

Mehr → „Feedback senden“ opens a sheet for a free-text note to Snacklist Support. The user writes the note (up to 2000 characters), optionally picks a kind (Bug, Idee, Sonstiges) and may tick that Snacklist Support may contact them. The note is sent with app version, build, platform, OS/browser family, and language, never list items or a screen name. After sending, the sheet closes with the snackbar „Danke!“. If sending fails the note stays and the button becomes „Erneut senden“.

## Sub-features

- `fb-open` the sheet from More; heading „Feedback senden“, close button „Schließen“.
- `fb-message` multiline field „Deine Nachricht“ (hint „Was ist dir aufgefallen?“), required.
- `fb-kind` three optional chips: „Bug“, „Idee“, „Sonstiges“ under „Art (optional)“; tapping the chosen one again clears it.
- `fb-contact` checkbox „Snacklist Support darf mich dazu kontaktieren“, off by default (job 267: no „Peter“ in the app's feedback strings).
- `fb-attached` fixed line „Wird mitgeschickt: App-Version, Gerät und Sprache. Keine Listeneinträge.“
- `fb-limit` at 1800 characters a counter „{count} / 2000“ shows; above 2000 the field shows „Höchstens 2000 Zeichen“ and Send is disabled.
- `fb-send` „Senden“ (disabled while empty, too long or sending; spinner labelled „Wird gesendet …“), then sheet closes, snackbar „Danke!“.
- `fb-fail` „Nicht gesendet. Prüfe die Verbindung und versuch es noch einmal.“ with the note kept and „Erneut senden“; a retry of an unchanged note stores one row, an edited note counts as a new one (editing clears the error and the button reads „Senden“ again).
- `fb-draft` an unsent note (text, kind, contact box) survives closing the sheet, but not its screenshot; a sent note, or another account signing in, starts blank.

EN labels: "Send feedback", "Close", "Your message", "What did you notice?", "Type (optional)", "Bug", "Idea", "Other", "Snacklist Support may contact me about this", "Included: App version, device and language. No list items.", "{count} / 2000", "Up to {max} characters" ("Up to 2000 characters"), "Send", "Sending…", "Thanks!", "Not sent. Check your connection and try again.", "Retry".

## How to get to it (user POV)

- „Mehr“ / "More" → row „Feedback senden“ / "Send feedback" (second in the group, after „Tipps“ and before „Hilfe & Kontakt“ on the web; in the iPhone and Android apps „Snacklist bewerten“ sits between them; above the legal rows; no hint since job 85). It is the only entry point with the flag `feedback-fb2` off (no shake gesture, none from the list).
- Works from a phone and beside an open More page on wide screens; the note's "screen" is null (since job 259 More's row sends none; the shake entry of job 138, if it ships, sends the live screen).

## With the flag `feedback-fb2` (job 267)

- `fb-shot-menu` with the flag on, switching to the More tab (the tab pill or the wide rail) pictures the screen being left; the form then offers „Bildschirmfoto hinzufügen“ / "Add screenshot". More reached by URL or a reload has no picture and no button, and leaving the More tab drops the picture. It opens the annotator: the frozen screen, centred, and below it one set of tools: ✕ („Schließen“ / "Close", ends it without a picture), „Markiere, was nicht stimmt. Übermale, was niemand sehen soll.“, „Fertig“ / "Done", the segmented „Stift“ / „Rechteck“ / „Text“ ("Pen" / "Rectangle" / "Text", all in tomato) and one undo („Rückgängig“ / "Undo") that takes back the last mark of any tool; ✕, undo and „Fertig“ are 48 px targets. With „Text“, a tap on the picture opens a textbox „Text eingeben“ / "Type here" there, typed into on the web too, and the words become a mark. „Fertig“ attaches the PNG, which holds every mark shown; the form shows switch „Bildschirmfoto“ / "Screenshot" with „Zeigt alles, was auf dem Bildschirm war, auch Einträge und Namen aus deinem Haushalt. Verdecke vorher, was wir nicht sehen sollen.“ / "Shows everything that was on screen, including items and names from your household. Cover anything we shouldn't see first." (Legal N-268, condition C2) and a preview (role img „Vorschau des Bildschirmfotos“ / "Screenshot preview"). While the switch is on, `fb-attached` reads „Wird mitgeschickt: App-Version, Gerät und Sprache.“ (no „Keine Listeneinträge.“); switched off, the full line returns. Sent with the switch on, the PNG goes to `POST /functions/v1/feedback-screenshots/upload/<note id>`, which stores it in the private bucket `feedback-screenshots` as `<user id>/<note id>.png` (clients cannot write the bucket themselves), before the row, whose `screenshot` names it; switched off, nothing is uploaded. More never sends a screen name. A screenshot the server refuses (over the quota, too large, not a PNG) shows „Das Bildschirmfoto wurde nicht angenommen. Schalte es aus, dann geht deine Nachricht ohne Bild.“ / "The screenshot wasn't accepted. Switch it off to send your message without it." and „Erneut senden“.
- `fb-shake` (iOS and Android only, so not drivable here; widget tests `app/test/feedback/fb2_test.dart`): a shake asks in a bottom sheet „Problem mit diesem Bildschirm melden?“ with „Mit Bildschirmfoto“ / „Ohne Bildschirmfoto“, ✕ or a tap beside to dismiss, and „Schütteln ausschalten: Mehr › Einstellungen“. The form after it has switch „Bildschirmname“ / "Screen name" with the live screen's name; with no screenshot the included line keeps „Keine Listeneinträge.“ Einstellungen has „Schütteln für Feedback“ (on by default, phones only).
- Flag on in Playwright: `fakeCollector(context, { flags: { 'feedback-fb2': true } })` and sign up with either consent answer (since #488 flags arrive without the analytics yes; the spec accepts). Spec: "flag on: a screenshot from More is marked up, uploaded and named by the row" in `e2e/tests/feedback.spec.ts` (a pen stroke and a typed text mark, both found in the stored PNG's pixels); read the object with `adminClient().storage.from('feedback-screenshots').download(name)`.
- With the flag off everything above is absent and the form reads as before.

## With the flag `feedback-fb4` (job 268)

- `fb-hub` More's row becomes „Feedback & Ideen“ / "Feedback & ideas" (icon forum_outlined, key `more-feedback`) and opens `/more/feedback` (screen name `feedback`; beside More's list on wide screens). While notes have an answer the user has not opened (a status, version or reply changed since they last opened it: `answered_at` after `seen_at`), the row shows a honey count badge (`more-feedback-badge`) and screen readers hear „Feedback & Ideen, 2 Neuigkeiten“ / "Feedback & ideas, 2 updates" (one per note with news, however many changes it had; an unread note's card is marked „Neue Antwort“ / "New reply" when it has a reply, else „Neuer Status“ / "New status"). The inbox loads when More is shown, when the More tab is selected, when the app comes back to the front, when the session shows another household or user (the hub then shows that household's +1s), after a send from the hub, and after opening an unanswered note. Flag off, `/more/feedback` and `/more/feedback/<id>` redirect to `/more` and the row reads and works as above.
- `fb-hub-write` „An Snacklist Support schreiben“ / "Write to Snacklist Support" (`feedback-write`) opens the same form as `fb-open` (with `feedback-fb2` also its screenshot button); after a send the new note is on top as „Gesendet“.
- `fb-hub-notes` „Deine Notizen“ / "Your notes": cards newest first (`feedback-note-<id>`, at most 50), each with a status chip (`feedback-status`) „Gesendet“ / „Gelesen“ / „Geplant“ / „Behoben in 1.4.1“ / „Behoben“ (EN "Sent" / "Read" / "Planned" / "Fixed in 1.4.1" / "Fixed") and, while unopened, a honey dot with „Neue Antwort“ (a reply) or „Neuer Status“ (EN "New reply" / "New status"); the text is cut after 3 lines. None yet: „Noch keine Notizen. Was du schreibst, steht danach hier, mit Status und Antwort.“ A failed load: „Konnte nicht geladen werden.“ with „Erneut versuchen“ (`feedback-retry-load`).
- `fb-hub-note` a tap opens `/more/feedback/<id>` (screen name `feedback_note`), titled „Deine Notiz“ / "Your note": the status chip, the user's text with its date (and „· gelesen“ / "· read" once the status is past Sent; `feedback-note-message`), Snacklist Support's reply with „Snacklist Support · <date>“ (`feedback-note-reply`), and for a fixed note with a version the link „Was ist neu in 1.4.1“ / "What's new in 1.4.1" (`feedback-whats-new`) to `https://snacklist.app/<de|en>/changelog#v1-4-1`. Opening it calls `feedback_mark_seen` and the badge drops. An id not among the user's notes: „Diese Notiz gibt es nicht.“
- `fb-hub-ideas` „Ideen, die wir abwägen“ / "Ideas being considered" (hidden while none are listed): each idea's title in the UI language, „Wird überlegt“ / „Geplant“ (EN "Being considered" / "Planned"), and a +1 toggle (`feedback-vote-<idea id>`, 48 px, pressed state for screen readers, label „<Titel>: +1, 9 Stimmen“ / "<title>: +1, 9 votes") for the shown household; the count moves at once and goes back with „Deine Stimme ist nicht angekommen. Versuch es noch einmal.“ when the server refuses. Under them: „Ideen schreibt Snacklist Support. Andere sehen nur die Zahl, nie wer abgestimmt hat.“ Only totals reach the app, never who voted.
- No hub, note or form text names a person (widget test `app/test/feedback/fb4_test.dart`, DE and EN). Widget tests and review screenshots: `app/test/feedback/fb4_test.dart`, `fb4_screenshots_test.dart` (`docs/screenshots/268/`).

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Seeded household, signed in, consent declined, on the list.
- To read the stored row: service-role client (`adminClient()` in `e2e/tests/helpers.ts`) on table `feedback`, filtered by the user's id.

- **Open.** `openMore(page, 'Feedback senden')` (it wheels the lazy list to the row; in a journey `openTab(page, L, 'tabMore')` plus `reveal`). Observable: `getByRole('group',{name:'Feedback senden', exact:true})` visible; `getByRole('button',{name:'Senden', exact:true})` disabled.
- **Write.** `typeInto(page.getByRole('textbox',{name:'Deine Nachricht'}), 'Beim Abhaken springt die Liste nach oben.')`. Use `typeInto` (umlauts). Send becomes enabled.
- **Kind.** `getByRole('checkbox',{name:'Idee', exact:true}).click()`; contact: `getByRole('checkbox',{name:'Snacklist Support darf mich dazu kontaktieren'})`.
- **Send.** `getByRole('button',{name:'Senden', exact:true}).click()`. Observable: sheet group gone; snackbar text „Danke!“ (find with `page.locator('[id^="flt-semantic-node-"]').getByText('Danke!',{exact:true}).first()`); one `feedback` row: message, category (`bug`/`idea`/`other`/null), contact_ok, platform `web`, locale `de`, screen null.
- **Limits.** Fill 2001 characters: `getByText('Höchstens 2000 Zeichen')`, `getByText('2001 / 2000')`, Send disabled; at 2000 `getByText('2000 / 2000')` and Send enabled.
- **Offline / retry.** `context.setOffline(true)`, Send: `getByText('Nicht gesendet. Prüfe die Verbindung und versuch es noch einmal.').first()` visible, textbox keeps its value, `getByRole('button',{name:'Erneut senden', exact:true})` enabled; `setOffline(false)`, click it: „Danke!“, exactly one row.
- **Double tap** on Send stores one row.
- Specs: `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/feedback.spec.ts` and `tests/feedback-fb4.spec.ts` (flag off, a reply and the badge, ideas and +1s).
- On the verify-snacklist build: `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=feedback-fb2,feedback-fb4"` (exported for `doctor.sh` too). Driven 2026-11-16 on c2183ca6: List → Mehr by the tab, „Feedback & Ideen“ (no „Feedback senden“), `/more/feedback`, „An Snacklist Support schreiben“, „Bildschirmfoto hinzufügen“, a pen stroke, „Fertig“: the switch „Bildschirmfoto …“ checked and the preview; sent, the card „Gesendet …“ and a row with a `screenshot`. Then `status='fixed', fixed_in='1.4.1', reply` set in the database and an idea inserted into `feedback_ideas`: after a reload the row is named „Feedback & Ideen, 1 Neuigkeit“, the card has „Behoben in 1.4.1“ and „Neue Antwort“, „Ideen, die wir abwägen“ lists the idea, the note opens at `/more/feedback/<id>` with the link „Was ist neu in 1.4.1“; Einstellungen has no „Schütteln für Feedback“ on the web. The plain build: „Feedback senden“, no screenshot button, a row with `screen` and `screenshot` null, `/more/feedback` → `/more`.

## Gotchas

- Kind chips are checkboxes in the accessibility tree, not buttons or radios.
- The message is trimmed; length is counted in characters as trimmed, 1 to 2000.
- A feedback row is stored with or without analytics consent; the analytics event `feedback_submitted` (category bug/idea/other/none, entry_point settings or shake, a screen only after a shake, contact_ok, screenshot true/false; no text) goes out only with consent. Accounts on test domains are marked internal (`.test`, `.invalid`, `.example`, `.localhost`, example.com/net/org), so the baseline's `fg-test.invalid` members store `is_internal: true` (driven 2026-11-04).
- The e2e build and this skill's build both point analytics at a fake collector (posthog.invalid); feedback itself goes to Supabase.
- Rows older than 12 months are deleted by the server (migration); that is not visible in the app.
- With `feedback-fb4` off the sender cannot read notes back in the app (on: `fb-hub-notes`), but Konto → „Meine Daten exportieren“ (EN "Export my data") includes their own notes (section `feedback` of the export).
- Peter reads the notes himself (`docs/FEEDBACK.md`). A daily mail digest of new notes to him is built (job 225) but ships off (`app_switches.feedback_digest`) until Legal's GO; nothing about it shows in the app.
