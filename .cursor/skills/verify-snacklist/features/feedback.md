# Feedback

Mehr → „Feedback & Ideen“ / "Feedback & ideas" opens `/more/feedback`: „An Snacklist Support schreiben“ / "Write to Snacklist Support" opens a sheet for a free-text note, „Deine Notizen“ / "Your notes" lists the user's own notes with their status and Snacklist Support's replies, and „Ideen, die wir abwägen“ / "Ideas being considered" lets the household +1 listed ideas. The note (up to 2000 characters, an optional kind Bug, Idee or Sonstiges, an optional „Snacklist Support darf mich dazu kontaktieren“) is sent with app version, build, platform, OS/browser family and language, never list items or a screen name; a screenshot is added only when the user chooses one and can be marked up and covered first. After sending, the sheet closes with the snackbar „Danke!“ and the note is on top as „Gesendet“. If sending fails the note stays and the button becomes „Erneut senden“. On iPhone and Android a shake also offers to report the current screen.

## Sub-features

- `fb-open` the sheet from „An Snacklist Support schreiben“; heading „Feedback senden“, close button „Schließen“.
- `fb-message` multiline field „Deine Nachricht“ (hint „Was ist dir aufgefallen?“), required.
- `fb-kind` three optional chips: „Bug“, „Idee“, „Sonstiges“ under „Art (optional)“; tapping the chosen one again clears it.
- `fb-contact` checkbox „Snacklist Support darf mich dazu kontaktieren“, off by default (no „Peter“ in the app's feedback strings).
- `fb-attached` fixed line „Wird mitgeschickt: App-Version, Gerät und Sprache. Keine Listeneinträge.“ (with the screenshot switch on: „Wird mitgeschickt: App-Version, Gerät und Sprache.“).
- `fb-limit` at 1800 characters a counter „{count} / 2000“ shows; above 2000 the field shows „Höchstens 2000 Zeichen“ and Send is disabled.
- `fb-send` „Senden“ (disabled while empty, too long or sending; spinner labelled „Wird gesendet …“), then sheet closes, snackbar „Danke!“.
- `fb-fail` „Nicht gesendet. Prüfe die Verbindung und versuch es noch einmal.“ with the note kept and „Erneut senden“; a retry of an unchanged note stores one row, an edited note counts as a new one (editing clears the error and the button reads „Senden“ again).
- `fb-draft` an unsent note (text, kind, contact box) survives closing the sheet, but not its screenshot; a sent note, or another account signing in, starts blank.
- `fb-shot-menu` switching to the More tab (the tab pill or the wide rail) pictures the screen being left; the form then offers „Bildschirmfoto hinzufügen“ / "Add screenshot". More reached by URL or a reload has no picture and no button, and leaving the More tab drops the picture. It opens the annotator: the frozen screen, centred, and below it one set of tools: ✕ („Schließen“ / "Close", ends it without a picture), „Markiere, was nicht stimmt. Verdecke, was niemand sehen soll.“ / "Mark what's wrong. Cover anything nobody should see.", „Fertig“ / "Done", the segmented „Stift“ / „Rechteck“ / „Text“ ("Pen" / "Rectangle" / "Text", all in tomato) / „Verdecken“ ("Cover": a filled, opaque box in the theme's ink colour, square, painted into the PNG's pixels so the upload holds nothing of what lies under it; it spans from where the drag began, in any direction) and one undo („Rückgängig“ / "Undo") that takes back the last mark of any tool; ✕, undo and „Fertig“ are 48 px targets. With „Text“, a tap on the picture opens a textbox „Text eingeben“ / "Type here" there, typed into on the web too, and the words become a mark.
- `fb-shot-attached` „Fertig“ attaches the PNG, which holds every mark shown; the form shows switch „Bildschirmfoto“ / "Screenshot" with „Zeigt alles, was auf dem Bildschirm war, auch Einträge und Namen aus deinem Haushalt. Verdecke vorher, was wir nicht sehen sollen.“ / "Shows everything that was on screen, including items and names from your household. Cover anything we shouldn't see first." and a preview (role img „Vorschau des Bildschirmfotos“ / "Screenshot preview"). Sent with the switch on, the PNG goes to `POST /functions/v1/feedback-screenshots/upload/<note id>`, stored in the private bucket `feedback-screenshots` as `<user id>/<note id>.png` (clients cannot write the bucket themselves), before the row, whose `screenshot` names it; switched off, nothing is uploaded. A screenshot the server refuses (over the quota, too large, not a PNG) shows „Das Bildschirmfoto wurde nicht angenommen. Schalte es aus, dann geht deine Nachricht ohne Bild.“ / "The screenshot wasn't accepted. Switch it off to send your message without it." and „Erneut senden“.
- `fb-shake` (iOS and Android only, so not drivable here; widget tests `app/test/feedback/fb2_test.dart`): a shake asks in a bottom sheet „Problem mit diesem Bildschirm melden?“ with „Mit Bildschirmfoto“ / „Ohne Bildschirmfoto“, ✕ or a tap beside to dismiss, and „Schütteln ausschalten: Mehr › Einstellungen“. The form after it has switch „Bildschirmname“ / "Screen name" with the live screen's name; with no screenshot the included line keeps „Keine Listeneinträge.“ Einstellungen has „Schütteln für Feedback“ (on by default, phones only).
- `fb-hub` More's row „Feedback & Ideen“ / "Feedback & ideas" (icon forum_outlined, key `more-feedback`) opens `/more/feedback` (screen name `feedback`; beside More's list on wide screens). While notes have an answer the user has not opened (a status, version or reply changed since they last opened it: `answered_at` after `seen_at`), the row shows a honey count badge (`more-feedback-badge`) and screen readers hear „Feedback & Ideen, 2 Neuigkeiten“ / "Feedback & ideas, 2 updates" (one per note with news, however many changes it had). The inbox loads when More is shown, when the More tab is selected, when the app comes back to the front, when the session shows another household or user (the hub then shows that household's +1s), after a send from the hub, and after opening an unanswered note.
- `fb-hub-write` „An Snacklist Support schreiben“ / "Write to Snacklist Support" (`feedback-write`) opens the form (`fb-open`, with its screenshot button); after a send the new note is on top as „Gesendet“.
- `fb-hub-notes` „Deine Notizen“ / "Your notes": cards newest first (`feedback-note-<id>`, at most 50), each with a status chip (`feedback-status`) „Gesendet“ / „Gelesen“ / „Geplant“ / „Behoben in 1.4.1“ / „Behoben“ (EN "Sent" / "Read" / "Planned" / "Fixed in 1.4.1" / "Fixed") and, while unopened, a honey dot with „Neue Antwort“ (a reply) or „Neuer Status“ (EN "New reply" / "New status"); the text is cut after 3 lines. None yet: „Noch keine Notizen. Was du schreibst, steht danach hier, mit Status und Antwort.“ A failed load: „Konnte nicht geladen werden.“ with „Erneut versuchen“ (`feedback-retry-load`).
- `fb-hub-note` a tap opens `/more/feedback/<id>` (screen name `feedback_note`), titled „Deine Notiz“ / "Your note": the status chip, the user's text with its date (and „· gelesen“ / "· read" once the status is past Sent; `feedback-note-message`), Snacklist Support's reply with „Snacklist Support · <date>“ (`feedback-note-reply`), and for a fixed note with a version the link „Was ist neu in 1.4.1“ / "What's new in 1.4.1" (`feedback-whats-new`) to `https://snacklist.app/<de|en>/changelog#v1-4-1`. Opening it calls `feedback_mark_seen` and the badge drops. An id not among the user's notes: „Diese Notiz gibt es nicht.“
- `fb-hub-ideas` „Ideen, die wir abwägen“ / "Ideas being considered" (hidden while none are listed): each idea's title in the UI language, „Wird überlegt“ / „Geplant“ (EN "Being considered" / "Planned"), and a +1 toggle (`feedback-vote-<idea id>`, 48 px, pressed state for screen readers, label „<Titel>: +1, 9 Stimmen“ / "<title>: +1, 9 votes") for the shown household; the count moves at once and goes back with „Deine Stimme ist nicht angekommen. Versuch es noch einmal.“ when the server refuses. Under them: „Ideen schreibt Snacklist Support.“ / "Snacklist Support writes the ideas." (Legal N-300, job 317: who sees votes is in the privacy policy §10). Only totals reach the app, never who voted.
- No hub, note or form text names a person (widget test `app/test/feedback/fb4_test.dart`, DE and EN). Review screenshots: `app/test/feedback/fb4_screenshots_test.dart` (`docs/screenshots/268/`).
- Reporting illegal content from the app („Inhalt melden“ in the hub, „Auch: Inhalte melden“ under the row) is built but off for users (flag `report-content-entry`): see [report-content](./report-content.md) `rc-app`.

EN labels: "Send feedback", "Close", "Your message", "What did you notice?", "Type (optional)", "Bug", "Idea", "Other", "Snacklist Support may contact me about this", "Included: App version, device and language. No list items.", "{count} / 2000", "Up to {max} characters" ("Up to 2000 characters"), "Send", "Sending…", "Thanks!", "Not sent. Check your connection and try again.", "Retry".

## How to get to it (user POV)

- „Mehr“ / "More" → row „Feedback & Ideen“ / "Feedback & ideas" (after „Tipps“ and before „Hilfe & Kontakt“ on the web; in the iPhone and Android apps „Snacklist bewerten“ sits between them; above the legal rows) → „An Snacklist Support schreiben“ / "Write to Snacklist Support" for the form, a note card for its page.
- On iPhone and Android: shake the phone on any screen (unless „Schütteln für Feedback“ is off under Mehr › Einstellungen).
- Works from a phone and beside an open More page on wide screens; a note sent from More has no screen name.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Seeded household, signed in, consent declined, on the list; a plain `helpers/launch.sh` build.
- To read the stored row: service-role client (`adminClient()` in `e2e/tests/helpers.ts`) on table `feedback`, filtered by the user's id.

- **Open.** `openTab(page, L, 'tabMore')`, `reveal` and click `getByRole('button',{name:/^Feedback & Ideen/})`. Observable: URL `/more/feedback`, „Deine Notizen“. Click `getByRole('button',{name:'An Snacklist Support schreiben'})`: `getByRole('group',{name:'Feedback senden', exact:true})` visible; `getByRole('button',{name:'Senden', exact:true})` disabled.
- **Write.** `typeInto(page.getByRole('textbox',{name:'Deine Nachricht'}), 'Beim Abhaken springt die Liste nach oben.')`. Use `typeInto` (umlauts). Send becomes enabled.
- **Kind.** `getByRole('checkbox',{name:'Idee', exact:true}).click()`; contact: `getByRole('checkbox',{name:'Snacklist Support darf mich dazu kontaktieren'})`.
- **Send.** `getByRole('button',{name:'Senden', exact:true}).click()`. Observable: sheet group gone; snackbar text „Danke!“ (find with `page.locator('[id^="flt-semantic-node-"]').getByText('Danke!',{exact:true}).first()`); a card „Gesendet …“ on top; one `feedback` row: message, category (`bug`/`idea`/`other`/null), contact_ok, platform `web`, locale `de`, screen null.
- **Limits.** Fill 2001 characters: `getByText('Höchstens 2000 Zeichen')`, `getByText('2001 / 2000')`, Send disabled; at 2000 `getByText('2000 / 2000')` and Send enabled.
- **Offline / retry.** `context.setOffline(true)`, Send: `getByText('Nicht gesendet. Prüfe die Verbindung und versuch es noch einmal.').first()` visible, textbox keeps its value, `getByRole('button',{name:'Erneut senden', exact:true})` enabled; `setOffline(false)`, click it: „Danke!“, exactly one row.
- **Double tap** on Send stores one row.
- **Screenshot.** From the list, switch to More by the tab, open the form: „Bildschirmfoto hinzufügen“, draw a pen stroke and a „Verdecken“ box, „Fertig“. Observable: switch „Bildschirmfoto …“ checked, preview img „Vorschau des Bildschirmfotos“; after sending, the row's `screenshot` names the object; read it with `adminClient().storage.from('feedback-screenshots').download(name)` (the covered area is one flat colour).
- **Reply and badge.** Set `status='fixed', fixed_in='1.4.1', reply` on the row as postgres and insert an idea into `feedback_ideas`, reload. Observable: the row is named „Feedback & Ideen, 1 Neuigkeit“, the card has „Behoben in 1.4.1“ and „Neue Antwort“, „Ideen, die wir abwägen“ lists the idea; the note at `/more/feedback/<id>` has „Was ist neu in 1.4.1“, and the badge is gone after it.
- Specs: `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/feedback.spec.ts` and `tests/feedback-fb4.spec.ts` (they turn the flags on with `fakeCollector(context, { flags: { 'feedback-fb2': true } })` and the like; "flag on: a screenshot from More is marked up, uploaded and named by the row" checks a pen stroke and a typed text mark in the stored PNG's pixels).
- Driven 2026-11-16 on c2183ca6 with both flags on (now the users' build): „Feedback & Ideen“, `/more/feedback`, a screenshot with a pen stroke sent and named by the row, the reply, badge, idea and note page as above; Einstellungen has no „Schütteln für Feedback“ on the web.

## Gotchas

- `VH_FLAGS_FULL=0` builds the flag-off screen (what the e2e specs see without flags): More's row is „Feedback senden“ and opens the form directly, with no screenshot button; `/more/feedback` and `/more/feedback/<id>` redirect to `/more`. `VH_FLAGS_OFF=feedback-fb4` leaves out only the hub.
- Kind chips are checkboxes in the accessibility tree, not buttons or radios.
- The message is trimmed; length is counted in characters as trimmed, 1 to 2000.
- A feedback row is stored with or without analytics consent; the analytics event `feedback_submitted` (category bug/idea/other/none, entry_point settings or shake, a screen only after a shake, contact_ok, screenshot true/false; no text) goes out only with consent. Accounts on test domains are marked internal (`.test`, `.invalid`, `.example`, `.localhost`, example.com/net/org), so the baseline's `fg-test.invalid` members store `is_internal: true` (driven 2026-11-04).
- The e2e build and this skill's build both point analytics at a fake collector (posthog.invalid); feedback itself goes to Supabase.
- Rows older than 12 months are deleted by the server (migration); that is not visible in the app.
- Konto → „Meine Daten exportieren“ (EN "Export my data") includes the user's own notes with status and reply (section `feedback`) and the household's +1s (`feedback_idea_votes`).
- Peter reads the notes himself (`docs/FEEDBACK.md`). A daily mail digest of new notes to him is built but ships off (`app_switches.feedback_digest`) until Legal's GO; nothing about it shows in the app.
