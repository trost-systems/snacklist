## Recommendation

**DM1, actions under the row.** The actions sit on the item they change, so the relation to the list is shown, not
explained. Every action has a word, merge, tick off and 2 more each take one tap, a typed amount comes first, and a
weight steps by its pack. 7 list rows stay in view (Today 8), and at 200 % with the keyboard up the matched row and
its chips stay in view (DM3–DM5 show no list row then).

**Runner-up: DM3, one sentence.** The plainest words, and all three choices in view at 200 %, but it covers more of
the list (6 · 2 · 0) and links to the row only by the outline. Its tick-off words could move onto DM1's chip (DM-Q5).
