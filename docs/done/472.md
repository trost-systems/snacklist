## Decisions

Moved from `docs/DECISIONS.md` (job 472e), where its heading was "Job 472: shopping reminders SR1, Benachrichtigungen & Erinnerungen (2026-11-26)". The numbers are the ones other docs cite as DECISIONS 472.n.

Peter picked SR1 with the SR-D defaults and the menu name (t1344u, NR2 / SR-L2 at 11:55 and confirmed 12:05). Legal gave the wording GO with M1-M4 and the merge conditions C1-C4 (`/workspace/legal/reviews/2026-11-26-snacklist-shopping-reminders-sr1.md`). Draft PR only: the flag `push-shopping-reminders` is off, the hold `shopping-reminders` unreleased, no `supabase/functions` change.

1. **The old table becomes a view, not dropped outright.** C1 wants one store. Old app builds (and this build's flag-off chips) read `shopping_day_reminders.weekdays`, and the frozen `outsiders_integration_test.ts` inserts into it. So the table is renamed, its rows go into `shopping_reminders` through the view's INSTEAD OF INSERT trigger (the same mapping pgTAP tests), and the table is dropped. The view holds no data; `weekdays` lists the shopping days of all the person's reminders.
2. **RPC arguments the outsiders sweep knows.** The sweep only has values for integer, boolean, text and the like, and it flags a function whose every call fails on its arguments. So the offset is `p_day_before boolean` (an integer offset would only ever be tried as 1) and the time is `p_minute integer`, any whole minute 0-1439; the app offers 5-minute steps (SR-Q10). `invalid_offset` refuses a null offset.
3. **`last_sent_on` is the occurrence's fire date.** A reminder at 23:50 that goes out at 00:10 in the catch-up window is marked with the previous date, the occurrence it belongs to. Due and saved-late share one helper (`shopping_reminder_last_fire`), so they cannot disagree, and instants (`at time zone`) keep DST right: a time in the spring gap fires once at the shifted instant.
4. **Deleting the last reminder: server first, then forget push.** Brief: an optimistic save that fails reverts with a snackbar. If the device forgot push first (the chips' N-436 order) and the delete then failed, the row would come back while the device no longer gets it. So SR1 deletes on the server, and only once that worked, and the server then holds no reminder of the person (the page's list only if that read fails), with the household-add alert off too, runs `PushRegistration.forget()`. A failed delete changes nothing anywhere: the row is back, push stays. The chips keep their order and pending clear (N-439) unchanged.
5. **With reminders but push off on this device, the list still shows them.** They are the person's (SR-Q4), sent to their other devices. The zone refresh runs only with push on here, so a tablet in another zone cannot move the phone's reminders. The next Save asks for permission again.
6. **Row layout.** The shopping day is the row's title, „Am Tag davor, 19:00“ / „Am Einkaufstag, 09:15“ under it, then „Nächste: Fr., 27. Nov., 19:00“ (SR-Q11, a real date). The prototype's one line „Samstag · am Vortag · 20:00“ wrapped its time onto a line of its own at 390 px, and „Vortag“ is not Legal's M1 wording.
7. **Sheet.** No checkmarks on the chips: a checked chip grows and reflows the 24 hour chips. Cancel and Save sit below the scrolling fields, so they stay in reach at 200 %. They sit side by side only when both labels fit half the width at the reader's text size; otherwise one above the other at full width (review 0: „Abbrech|en“ broke at 200 %). The sheet's goldens now assert word-only breaks too. A duplicate is refused in the sheet before it reaches the server; the server's own refusal (another device) reverts the row with the same words.
8. **Snackbars.** Only for what the row does not show: today skipped (SR-Q7), duplicate, the cap, blocked permission (`shoppingDayBlocked`), failure (`catalogSaveFailed`). A plain save shows its next firing on the row.
9. **Wrapping between words.** At 200 % „Benachrichtigungen“, „Datenschutzerklärung“, „Nutzungsbedingungen“ and „EINKAUFSERINNERUNGEN“ broke inside the word. `wordFitScaler` shrinks a text just enough that its widest word fits (never below 100 %), solving for the scale from two measurements because the theme's letter spacing does not scale. More's rows, the page title (an app bar that grows with its title; Material's own cuts with an ellipsis), the notification page's switches and `HlSectionLabel` use it. The label is never shortened and no legal string changed. The cost, measured at 200 %: the German row „Benachrichtigungen & Erinnerungen“ renders at 1.57× (siblings 2×), „Datenschutzerklärung“ at 1.46× and „Nutzungsbedingungen“ at 1.42×; English stays at 2×. That shrink is Peter's call (brief: wraps badly means NEEDS_LEAD), so it is filed as NEEDS_LEAD Job 472 with options; the code stays until he decides.
10. **Policy blocks.** One `::: until` / `::: once shopping-reminders` pair per changed paragraph; the two inside `::: once push-notifications` get a four-colon outer fence. `updated:`, `Stand:` and `Last updated:` stay; the later hold-release PR bumps them.
11. **Local golden failures that are not this job's.** `prices_golden_test.dart` (4) and `screens_golden_test.dart` "list wide" / "add sheet wide" (4) fail on this box on plain `origin/main` (0579afbb) too; CI renders them green.
12. **Review round (independent reviewer).** Fixed: "last" is decided from the server after the delete, not at the tap (a failed earlier delete brings its row back); a chips clear waiting offline is sent before SR1 reads or writes and a saved reminder supersedes it, so it cannot wipe SR1 reminders at the next start; `update_shopping_reminder` keeps `last_sent_on` only on the same shopping day and offset (pgTAP pins it, and fails on the old rule); the skip-today snackbar follows the server's answer; the sheet checks duplicates against the list at Save; the wrapping app bar subtracts the side safe area; a failed first read retries after 10 s. Not changed: two rows briefly sharing a slot cannot clash keys (each key sits on its row's own ListTile), and the server refuses the duplicate.
