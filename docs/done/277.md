# Job 277: the shopping total counts a per-kg item without a weight (2026-11-16)

## What was broken

With `prices-and-trip-total` on and `list-declutter` off, the „Einkaufssumme“ bar left out an
item priced per kg whose quantity has no weight. Adding 3 Gurken at 2,99 €/kg did not change the
counts. The declutter block already counted such an item as „ohne Preis“ (#471).

## Fix

`tripTotal` (now in `app/lib/list/subtotals.dart`) classifies each item with the block's
`Sum.of`. A per-kg item without a weight is „offen“ in the bar and „ohne Preis“ in the block.

## Proof

- `app/test/list/subtotals_test.dart`: one list with a per-kg item without a weight, a per-kg
  item with a weight and a per-piece item. The bar's and the block's counts and cents agree. It
  failed before the fix (`open` 0, expected 1).
- `app/test/list/summary_block_test.dart`: the same list with `list-declutter` on (block
  „2 mit Preis · 1 ohne Preis“, „mind. ≈ 4,18 €“) and off (bar „Einkaufssumme 4,18 €, 2 mit
  Preis, 1 offen“). The off case read „0 offen“ without the fix.
- Release web build with `FLAGS_ON=prices-and-trip-total`. Typing „3 Gurken“ and then giving it
  2,99 €/kg moved the bar from „1 mit Preis, 14 offen“ to „15 offen“. The same journey on
  c2183ca6 stayed at „14 offen“. Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/277/`.
