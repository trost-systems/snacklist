# Job 480a: harden the Play listing lane before Dec 1

Follow-up to job 480 review 2's nice-to-haves.

- **Date floor.** `PlayListing.blockers` refuses a `held_release_from` earlier than 2026-12-01T00:00:00+01:00 (`HELD_FROM_FLOOR`). An upload with such a value opens no edit; preflight logs the reason.
- **Not sent for review.** The `android listing` lane's client has supply's rescue off (`listing_client` in the Fastfile). When Play asks for `changesNotSentForReview=true`, `PlayListing.commit` commits with it (`SupplyCommit#commit_not_sent_for_review!`, the flag is reset after that commit), the lane reads the listing back and fails with `!!! NOT SENT FOR REVIEW !!!`, naming Play Console's "Send changes for review".
- **Runbook.** PAYWALL_RELEASE 6.5 and RELEASE.md's "After the integration-lock hold flip" checklist: second `mode=upload` without `with_held_release`, Google's approval, publish, `mode=check`.
- **Stale order.** `docs/done/480.md`'s first "Dec 1 order" is struck through and points to the RELEASE.md checklist.
