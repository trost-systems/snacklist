## Left out
- BayWa Bau & Gartenmärkte: no consumer app. "My BayWa" (`com.baywa.baustoffe.baustoffwelt.app`) is the building-trade
  portal, not the chain's DIY app. Not added.
- OBI SK and IT, Globus Baumarkt CZ, Hagebau CH/NL/etc.: the app is in the storefront, but no evidence from the
  chain's own pages that the chain trades there (obi.sk and obi-italia.it answer 404). Bauhaus CH, NL, CZ, SE, NO,
  DK, FI, HU, SK, LU, SI, BE and others: the BAUHAUS app is not in those storefronts. "BAUHAUS CZ" (546370987,
  IMPER CZ) is no AG app; "Bauhaus Türkiye" (1487387594) is a separate app and TR is no registry country.
- Hornbach HU, SI, BE: the app is in the storefront, but no Hornbach stores (no domain, not verified).
- OBI PRO, oneOBI, SMART HOME by hornbach, hagebau Wallner, hugo, Piardino: other apps, not the chain's customer app.
- Store counts (about 50 or more) are from Wikipedia where it states them (Sonderpreis 358, Bauhaus about 290,
  toom group about 330 incl. B1); HELLWEG's and B1's own counts are not verified.

## Matching
Case-insensitive through name and aliases: "Hornbach", "OBI", "Toom", "Bauhaus", "Hellweg", "Hagebau",
"hagebaumarkt", "Globus Baumarkt", "Globus Bau". The registry has no grocery Globus entry, so plain "Globus" has no
exact match and keeps none; as a typing suggestion it lists Globus Baumarkt (prefix, as for any chain). "Globus Markt"
and "Globus Hypermarkt" match nothing. No collision with existing names or aliases. Tests:
`retailer_registry_test.dart`, `supabase/tests/retailers.test.sql`, `scripts/retailers_test.ts`.
