# Job 457: hill climb of the add sheet and the app's dictation

A bounded climb (4 steps or 4 hours) on the add flow users have today, with the flags
`input-stable-rows`, `on-device-catalog`, `add-feedback-list`, `add-sheet-compact` and
`add-match-rows` all full. No file under `supabase/functions/` changed, and no ARB, site,
email, store or legal copy changed.

## The measure

`tool/hillclimb/hc-add-item-voice/measure.sh` prints one JSON line. Lower is better. The
frozen weights, the probe and the noise band are in
[docs/hillclimb/hc-add-item-voice.md](../hillclimb/hc-add-item-voice.md):

```
score = key_ms_p50 + enter_ms_p50 + 50 * (rank_keys_new + rank_keys_bought)
      + 1000 * (gate_failures + adds_missed)
```

The client voice parse accuracy part was dropped. The app has no sentence parser, because
`ListCubit.addSpoken` sends dictated text to the frozen `voice` function.

## Steps

The step table, with each hypothesis, score and verdict, is in

## Kept

- **No second session while the microphone gets ready.** Before the fix, a tap while the
  recognizer was getting ready (the permission prompts, the offline-language check, up to 5 s
  on Android) started a second `prepare` and `listen`. Now that tap does nothing.
- **A sentence's late answer leaves the next sentence alone.** A tap while the server splits
  a sentence starts the next one, as on main. Before the fix, the first sentence's answer then
  emptied the field under the new live words and moved the focus mid-listen. A sentence's answer
  now clears the field only while the field still holds its text. It focuses the field only while
  no sentence is listening or getting ready. Both sentences' items are added.
- Tests in `app/test/list/dictation_test.dart`. The first test fails on main (2 prepares). The
  second fails on main (the field is emptied under „Eier“). It also fails on review 0's head,
  which ignored the tap and lost „Eier“, and when the focus guard is removed (the field gets focus
  mid-listen). The third test, where the next sentence hears nothing and the first answer still
  focuses the field, fails on review 1's head, which used a per-tap counter. `FakeRepository.spokenGate` holds the split.

In short, Enter on a ticked match drops a typed amount or brand (documented as by design),
offline dictation leaves one sentence that Enter adds as one item, a bare „2“ becomes an item,
and dictating over typed text replaces it.

Copy for job 456 or the lead (not edited here). `dictationSplitFailed` says „Der Text steht

users have has no visible „Hinzufügen“ / "Add" button, because that name is only the sheet's
