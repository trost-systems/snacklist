## What was produced

`docs/design/anonymous-start/AN1.md` specifies step one of anonymous start:

- Anonymous start in the iOS and Android apps, from the guided start's account step
  („Erst mal ohne Konto“). The web stays account-first because of Safari's 7-day storage cap.
- An upgrade that keeps the same user id: `updateUser(email)`, `linkIdentity` on the web,
  `linkIdentityWithIdToken` in the apps. A transfer for an email or identity that is already
  taken. The name step comes right after the link.
- Invites, joining, helper links, API keys, MCP and Alexa consent, Siri, Premium and a second
  device need an account. The server refuses each of them for anonymous users. Section 6 is the
  table of every guarded RPC and its overloads.
- Web helpers („Helferlink“): a per-list grant on an anonymous browser session. It is not a
  membership, and helpers get no PostHog and no flag query. A helper can export and delete their
  own data.
- Data loss, abuse caps, kill switch, purge (90 / 30 / 7 days), display name, export, stores, and
  a build sequence of 7 jobs.
- Proposed DE and EN copy (section 7). No ARB change.
- Legal (section 11): the new processing P1-P9, redlines R0-R12 for the privacy policy and the
  deletion page, and 10 questions for Legal.

Decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Job 375".
