# Job 497: the add sheet's top row follows the sheet's corners (2026-11-28, Europe/Berlin)

Peter (28 Nov 2026, 14:08, t1419u): "in the screenshot you can see that the top-left corner of the
highlighted item in the sheet is cut off. The top item's top-left and top-right corners should align with
the sheets radius to avoid this visual bug."

## What was wrong

The compact add sheet (`add-sheet-compact`, full) clips its content to its rounded top
(`_Surface`'s `ClipRRect`, radius `sheetRadius`: 28 px, 24 px under the soft look). Its first row sits 12 px
below that edge, under the grab handle, so it lies inside the curve. That row was a square box: the clip
cut its fill on a slant at both top corners, and with AE2 (`add-ae2-list-peeks`) also the merge part's
Enter outline (a gap at the top-left) and the tick key's mint face (top-right). The focus ring of a
focused top row was cut the same way.

## The fix

`app/lib/list/add_sheet/add_sheet.dart`: the row right under the sheet's top (`topRow`: the compact sheet
with no note above its rows) gets top corners of `sheetRadius` (`_rowCorners`). This applies to whichever
row comes first: AE2's split row (merge part top-start, tick key top-end), a match row (`_MatchTile`) or the
first suggestion (`_SuggestionRow`). Its `Material` takes them with an anti-aliased clip, so the face and ink
follow the curve. The Enter outline (`foregroundDecoration`) and the focus ring (`_rowOutline`) take them too.
Every other row keeps no radius and no clip, as before.

The radius is the sheet's own radius, not a smaller inner one. The row sits 12 px down from the sheet's top
and 0 px in from its sides, so an inner radius cannot match both insets. A copy of the sheet's curve moved
12 px down lies wholly inside the sheet's clip, so nothing is cut, and the row's corner keeps the sheet's
shape.

## Proof

- `app/test/list/add_sheet_top_row_test.dart` (47 tests) reads the rendered pixels in each top corner of the
  first row. Within an arc of `sheetRadius` from the row's top it expects the face colour. Outside the arc
  it expects no face colour. Just inside the arc it expects the edge colour (the Enter outline, 2 px, or
  the focus ring, 3 px). Cases: the AE2 split row (merge part and tick key), the AE2 first suggestion,
  and with the flag off the match row and the first suggestion. Each runs in light and dark, at 100 % and
  200 %, in German and English, and once in the soft look. The focus ring has two more cases, light and dark.
  - On main (commit `78d8950a4`, test only): **47 of 47 fail**, every one with "face outside the arc"
    (for example 359 faults in the AE2 merge part's top-left at 100 %).
  - With the fix: 47 of 47 pass.
  - With the radius mutated to 0.5x: 47 fail ("face outside the arc", "no edge along the arc"). At
    1.6x: 47 fail ("no face inside the arc"). So the test pins the radius, not just "rounded".
- Goldens: `app/test/list/goldens/top-row-{ae2,compact}-{light,dark}-{100,200}-{de,en}.png` (16).
- `add_match_rows_test.dart`'s "square rows" pin (job 340) now says: the first row takes the sheet's top
  corners, every other row stays square.
- `test/list`, `test/design` and `test/hillclimb`: 2213 tests pass, including `screens_golden_test`,
  unchanged.
