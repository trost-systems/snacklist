# Job 326: store rating moments ST1 and ST2 (flag `rating-triggers-v2`)

**What changed, in plain words.** The system's "rate this app" dialog used to be asked for about 3 seconds after the
user emptied the list. With trip mode on, the question „Kaufst du gerade ein?“ opened on that same tick and always
won: the ask was dropped. Behind the new flag `rating-triggers-v2` (off), the app now asks at better moments:

- **ST1.** After the user ends a trip with Beenden and leaves its summary („Fertig“ or back), once they have been idle
  on the list for 3 seconds. This needs at least 3 items bought, everything planned bought, a save that went through
  on the first try, and no „Einkauf löschen“. It never asks on the summary itself, after the 4-hour auto end, or
  after „Weiter einkaufen“.
- **ST2.** After the list is emptied and „Kaufst du gerade ein?“ is answered „Nein, nur abgehakt“ (or closed), the
  3 seconds start again on the list. „Ja“ waits for the trip's end.
- **SA1.** A list emptied while a trip runs asks nothing; the trip's end does.
- **SA2.** „Zurücknehmen“, or a put-back from „Zuletzt verwendet“ onto the still empty list within 10 minutes, takes
  that trip off the count and stops asks for the rest of the session.
- **SA3.** A failed or refused trip save, a household switch, and a join or new household block asks for 24 hours
  and the next session. A sent bug note blocks them for 14 days.
- **SA5.** At least 180 days between asks and at most 2 per 365 days, instead of 120 and 3. The other gates stay.

Flag off, everything works as before. The „Snacklist bewerten“ row in Mehr is unchanged.

**Where.** `app/lib/review/` (`ReviewRules` table v1/v2, new gates and counters, the trigger's parking of the
moment while the trip question is up, `trip_signals.dart`), `app/lib/trip/trip_cubit.dart` (`activity`: answers,
summary left with bought count and completeness, failed saves), `app/lib/list/list_cubit.dart` (`TripReopened`),
`app/lib/shell/list_scope.dart` (`TripCubitSignals`) and `app/lib/shell/app.dart` (joins, switches, bug notes).
Decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Job 326". Feature map: `rate-snacklist.md` (`rate-prompt-trips`).

**How to verify.** The dialog is native and asks only in public store builds, so the proof is tests:
- `app/test/trip/list_trip_widget_test.dart`, group "the rating prompt with rating-triggers-v2": the real list, ask
  sheet, strip and summary page. The emptying tick's question keeps the moment and „Nein“ asks 3 s later; a trip asks
  nothing at the emptying and asks once its summary is left; a planned item left over asks nothing. With the flag
  off the same steps give today's behaviour (no ask after „Nein“; an ask mid-trip), checked by flipping the flag.
- `app/test/review/` (gates for v1 and v2, counters, the trigger's trip moments), `app/test/trip/trip_cubit_test.dart`
  (when a left summary is complete), `app/test/list/list_cubit_test.dart` (the reopened trip, also not on a refilled
  list), `app/test/shell/review_setbacks_test.dart` (join, switch and bug-note rules).

**Results.** `scripts/verify.sh --only gitleaks,lint,flutter` passed (lint and gitleaks on the first run, flutter
after a lint fix in the new test). db and e2e run in CI (app-only change, the prompt never shows on the web).

**Not done or blocked.**
- No ARB, legal or privacy file changed; no analytics change (SA7 is out of scope).
- Turning the flag on changes nothing anyone can see until a public store build exists: TestFlight never shows the
  dialog, and Android asks only with `STORE_RELEASE`. Creating the PostHog flag `rating-triggers-v2` is the lead's
  step.
- Out of scope as briefed: ST3, ST4, SA4, SA7.
