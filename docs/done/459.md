# Job 459: hc-category-sorting

Hill-climb of the enabled list's store sections and walk order (`hide-categories` full). Started
2026-11-26 11:53 CET, stopped 12:55 CET after 3 kept steps (of at most 4). Usage gate 0.85 to 0.87.

## What changed

- **Fixed:** an item moved to another store in its edit sheet showed at its old place's key in
  the new store and jumped a moment later, when the server answered. It now goes last among its
  category's items in that store's walk at once (`ItemChanges.apply`, `app/lib/list/model.dart`).
- **Fixed:** two stores on the same sort order (two members adding a store at the same moment)
  showed in whatever order the rows arrived, so two phones or two reloads could differ. They now
  sort by name (`sectionsOf`, `app/lib/list/list_view/sections.dart`).
- **Faster:** regrouping the list after an add or an edit reuses each store's walking order while
  the catalog is unchanged (`walkingOrder`, an `Expando` per `Catalog`): −35 % in same-load A/B
  pairs on 1,000 items and 12 stores (311 → 202 µs; the review's 4 pairs: −28 % by median).
- The frozen measure: `tool/hillclimb/hc-category-sorting/measure.sh` over
  `app/test/list/sections_bench_test.dart` (`SECTIONS_BENCH=1`); method, baseline and steps in
  `docs/hillclimb/hc-category-sorting.md`.
