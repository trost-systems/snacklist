# Job 383: Premium copy per user (Legal N-348, N-349, N-350)

Legal pre-approved three Premium strings for a per-user plan (§ 5 UWG: no promise to unlock the
household next to a plan that is per user). This job changes them in `app/l10n/app_de.arb` and
`app_en.arb`, with tests and goldens. It does not merge (the lead lands it).

## What changed

- **N-348.** `premiumHeroTitle`: „Snacklist Premium für dich“ / "Snacklist Premium for you".
- **N-349.** `premiumWeb`, second sentence: „… für dein Konto freigeschaltet.“ / "… for your
  account."
- **N-350.** `premiumHousehold` shows above the Buy button, before a new purchase, so it got the
  new text: „Premium gilt dann für dein Konto, in jedem Haushalt, in dem du Mitglied bist.“ /
  "Premium then applies to your account, in every household you are a member of." The household
  name placeholder is gone, and with it `_Plans`'s `household` argument.
- Widget tests, the e2e locators in `purchases.spec.ts`, nine Premium goldens and the feature
  map (`premium.md`, `purchases.md`, FEATURES-FOR-WRITERS) follow the new strings.

## How to verify

- `cd app && flutter test test/purchases test/design/premium_golden_test.dart`
- Goldens: `app/test/design/goldens/premium-offer-*.png`, `premium-compact-de-light.png`.
