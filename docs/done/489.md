## The options

| Label | Slug | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | `today` | The list is swapped for the last two adds under the scrim. Enter on a duplicate adds a second row |
| AE1 | `ae1-explain` | Words only. A card says the list (n items) is behind the sheet and closes it on tap. The sheet says where items go, the adds get a heading and their store, and duplicate rows say what each does |
| AE2 | `ae2-list-peeks` | No scrim and no swap. The real list stays above the sheet, which shows 2 suggestion rows. Adds appear in place as „New“. A duplicate's row scrolls into view and Enter adds 1 |
| AE3 | `ae3-inline-row` | No sheet. The field becomes a row in the list, after the last add or under a matching row. Suggestions open under it |
| AE4 | `ae4-landing-preview` | Today's sheet, plus a card of the target store. Before typing it shows the stores. While typing it shows a dashed ghost in the landing spot. After an add it shows the item there |
| AE5 | `ae5-fly-to-row` | The real list stays dimmed behind the scrim. Each add flies to its row, which lifts. A duplicate's row lifts and counts up. With reduced motion there is no flight |

AE2 to AE5 make Merge the Enter default for an item already on the list. Today and AE1 keep production's „As typed“.
AE1 does not change behaviour, so it labels that row „Puts a second Bananas on the list“.

## Recommendation

**AE2, the list stays in view.** The list never disappears, so there is nothing to explain. With the keyboard up,
5 rows stay visible (Today 0). Each add appears in its real place, all 3 adds are in view while adding, and seeing
all 3 takes 4 taps instead of 5. A duplicate's row scrolls into view and Enter makes it 7× instead of a second
row (from the Merge default, AE-Q2, which Today could take too). The sheet, rows, chips and gestures stay as they are, and nothing gets taller: 60 px less while typing.

**Runner-up: AE3, adding inside the list.** It shows the relation most literally and keeps the most list at 200 %
(7 rows, AE2 3), but at 310 % its field next to „Done“ shows about one letter. It is the biggest rebuild, its field moves as you add, and its suggestions take 321 px of list.
