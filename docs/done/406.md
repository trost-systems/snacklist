# Job 406: Get Snacklist grid and larger hero store badges (2026-11-22)

**Built.** `site/public/landing.css` only; no copy, link, media, legal or privacy file changed.

- **Get Snacklist (desktop with a mouse).** The two store cards are a two-column grid whose cards are
  subgrids spanning three shared rows (badge, QR, caption), so each row starts at the same y in both
  columns. Columns are centred inside; captions are capped at 24ch and may wrap, rows stay shared.
- **Badges.** One `--badge-h` per block (40 px in Get Snacklist, 56 px in the hero, 48 px under 600 px).
  Apple's SVG is drawn `--badge-h` tall. Google's PNG carries its own margin and differs per locale
  (alpha bounds: en 168 of 250 px visible, de 192 of 250 and no side margin), so it is drawn
  250/`--play-vis` times `--badge-h`. Both sit centred in a box 1.5 `--badge-h` tall, which gives the
  quarter-height clear space above and below. The de PNG gets a quarter-height side padding.
- **Stacking.** Below 640 px the Get Snacklist grid is one column (two columns need about 640 px with
  the German captions), each card centred. Review 0 measured the 480 px breakpoint overflowing.
- **Scope.** The grid rules sit under `.cta`, so the hero (no QR codes) keeps its flex row.

**Verified.** Built with the stage set to launch (not committed) and measured in Chromium: visible badge
heights 56/56 (hero), 40/40 (Get Snacklist) in EN and DE at 540 and 1280 px; no horizontal overflow from
360 to 1300 px in 10 px steps, EN and DE; site tests 251 pass. Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/406/`.
