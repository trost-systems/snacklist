## Today (read from the code; the details are in APPROACHES.md)

- The list sorts by store, then by category in the store's walk order, then by the order of adding. People set all of it by hand. Nothing learns, and nothing on the list drags (job 78).
- A store the household adds itself, like IKEA, gets the supermarket route order. IKEA items land in Household or Other. In English, eight of the round's 29 names land in a category other than the expected one ("Tea lights" in Pantry).
- A wrong place is fixed with the category chip in the edit sheet. The item goes to the end of its new category, and the move applies in every store.
- The pieces for learning already exist and go unused: every tick is stored with its time (`item_events`), trips are marked (trip mode is on for everyone), and `place_item` exists on the server.

## Round IO

| Option | Rows, iPhone 100 % | Fix Eggs | Whole IKEA list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Today | 9 (German 8) | 4 taps, ends after Passata, and in Pantry in every store | not possible item by item |

| IO2 Learns your walk | 11 | no taps, one trip | one trip |

| **IO4 Learns, plus "Next to"** | 11 | 4 taps, right after Flour | one trip |
| IO5 Categories on or off | 10 (switch on: as Today) | 6 taps (Move down ×4) | 31 taps (German 27) |

**Pick: IO4. Runner-up: IO2.** IO4 hides the categories and learns each store's order from the household's trip ticks. When the order is wrong, "Next to Flour" in the edit sheet puts the item right after another, at any distance, in 4 taps, without drag and with a screen reader. IO2 is the same without the chip: cheaper, but wrong until the next trip. IO5's per-user switch is IO-Q3, so users who like categories keep them. IO-Q1 to IO-Q11 are in `docs/design/order/io/RECOMMENDATION.md` (German in `.de.md`). The flag default is a new `learned-order`.

Agents (IO-Q9): `list_items` and `GET /v1/items` return the walk order by store, with `position`. MCP `place_item(item, before|after)` and `set_store_order(store, items[])` are new, with API twins. `move_item`, A1 `update_item` and A9 `set_store_layout` stay as they are, and A9 becomes the default order for new items.
