## What was built (flag on)

- **More.** The feedback row („Feedback & Ideen“ with FB4, „Feedback senden“ without) gets the
  subtitle „Auch: Inhalte melden“ / "Also: report content" (RC-Q1, Legal N-259).
- **Entry.** „Inhalt melden“ / "Report content", an outlined full-width button with Legal's
  one-line hint, under „An Snacklist Support schreiben“ in the hub, or on top of the feedback
  sheet opened from More (not from a shake). No item-menu entry (RC-Q2).
- **Form** (`app/lib/report/`), a full page: Legal's B.4 intro, the six kinds, „Wo ist der
  Inhalt?“ with a picker (search, items, lists, household name, „Woanders“ with N-260's help),
  the reason (20 to 5,000, counter), name and e-mail prefilled and editable (hidden for CSAM
  behind „Namen und E-Mail-Adresse angeben“), the screenshot row (only with `feedback-fb2` and a
  picture of the screen before More; never automatic; the annotator opens on its cover tool „Verdecken“, job 311), the
  good-faith box, the privacy §10 link, „Meldung senden“. Errors under each field on Send; send
  failures keep the input (429 with N-242's text, 503, offline).
- **Confirmation:** „Meldung gesendet“, the number with „Nummer kopieren“, Legal's thanks text,
  where the confirmation mail goes (or that none can), „Meldungen stehen nicht unter Deine
  Notizen …“, „Fertig“ back where it started. Status reaches the reporter by e-mail only (RC-Q3).
- **Server:** `POST /functions/v1/report/app` in #514's `report` function, the user's own session
  token, CORS; migration `20261118210000_content_reports_app.sql` (`source = 'app'`,
  `target_kind`/`target_id`, `content_report_screenshots`, `content_report_app_senders`,
  `content_report_submit_app`, a daily prune). 5 per user and 200 app reports an hour; every
  report with an address gets the confirmation (N-241). `docs/MODERATION.md` "Reports from the
  app", `docs/PRIVACY_DATA_MAP.md`.
