# Job 399: the „… ist erledigt“ card (SC1)

**What.** With the flag `open-store-app`, ticking off the last open item of a store matched to a
chain with an app shows a card at the top of the list: „Lidl ist erledigt“ / "Lidl is done", with
„App öffnen“ / "Open app" always below the title and an X. No icon. Only members who ticked in that
store within 30 minutes before its last tick see it. „App öffnen“ opens the app as the header key
does and keeps the card. The X hides it until the store is finished again. The card goes on its own
after 60 minutes or when an item is added there. One card, for the store finished last.

**Where.** `app/lib/list/store_done.dart` (rule and constants), `app/lib/list/list_view/store_done_card.dart`
(card), `ListCubit.storeDone` and `dismissStoreDone`, `ListPrefs.storeDoneDismissed`, `Item.boughtBy`
(read from the existing `items.bought_by`), `StoreAppSource` in `store_app.dart` (`store_app_opened.source`).

**New ARB keys.** `storeDoneTitle` („{store} ist erledigt“ / "{store} is done") and `storeDoneOpen`
(„App öffnen“ / "Open app"). Reused: `close` (the X), `storeAppOpen` (the button's screen-reader name).
