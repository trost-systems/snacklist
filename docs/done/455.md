# Job 455: competitor pain points, mapped to the enabled feature set

Research only. The report is [docs/research/competitor-pain-points.md](../research/competitor-pain-points.md), also at
`/workspace/snacklist/research/competitor-pain-points.md` and in the job directory.

## What it found

Evidence: 5,536 App Store reviews from Apple's review feed (Bring!, AnyList, OurGroceries, Out of Milk, Listonic; DE,
AT, CH, US, CA, GB, AU), German press reader comments (iphone-ticker, mobiflip, appgefahren), German Apple Community
threads, forums and official help pages, all fetched 26 Nov 2026. Reddit answers this runner with 403 and Google Play
reviews load by JavaScript, so neither is used.

Ranked recommendations for the enabled features:

1. Say what a mistaken tap just ticked (needs Peter: he removed the tick's undo snackbar on 29 Oct).
2. Teach where ticked items go, in the „Erste Schritte“ hint and the tips (simple fix, copy only).
3. Let users fix an item's place within a store, since hidden categories left no way to (needs Peter).
4. A VoiceOver/TalkBack pass on a real phone (needs Peter: a device).
5. Show who added an item in the edit sheet (needs design pick).
6. English catalog words in the English app (needs Peter: scope).
