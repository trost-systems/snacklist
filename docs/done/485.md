- Today's chips, text button and rows change no pixel by 3:1 when focused, so they fail focus appearance in both
  themes; its field and buttons pass with the outer ring.
- The phone replica reads the field-to-chips gap as 16 → 11 (24 px tap-target spacing); the real web build at
  desktop density measured 8 → 3. The same 5 px reach either way.
- FH2 passes the area rule by only 2 to 4 %: its 2 px ring is outside, so its perimeter is just longer than the
  control's.
- Largest text: 200 % (Android's largest) and 310 % (iOS AX5), EN and DE, both looks. The own-store field stays
  48 px tall in production (`store_name_field.dart:135-137`), so at 310 % its text is cut in every option, and at
  200 % an inside indicator can touch a glyph by 0 to 3 px; the column and the checks leave the field out
  (FH-Q5). FH4 gives the text button 8 px more room below its label (no change at 100 %). At 200 and 310 % the step
  scrolls inside the phone, so the focused field is below the fold there; the sheet beside it shows it.
- Stability (review 0): the first version named "the worst control" in each WCAG cell, and two controls 2
  antialiased pixels apart swapped places between runs, so a correct page failed. A reworded page sentence also put
  the phones on a half pixel, which moved gaps by 1 px. Now each WCAG cell takes the smallest ratio and the smallest
  contrast on their own, the table accepts ±0.02× and ±0.2:1 there (pass or FAIL must match), and every control
  is on whole pixels. After the fix the verify passed 5 fresh runs in a row on the same build (504 focus states
  and 24 sheets each, about 3 minutes), and it still exits 1 when FH2's light ratio is written as 1.10× instead of
  1.03× (outside the ±0.02× tolerance).
- With FH1's chip gap changed to 8 → 7 in the table it fails: `table row FH1: page says ["FH1","8 → 7",…],
  measured ["FH1","8 → 8",…]`, exit 1. Earlier runs failed real misses before the options were fixed (FH2's ring
  crossing the button edge at 0.6×, FH3's light cobalt on honey in dark at 0.2×, FH5's row glow painting 760 px onto
  the next row).

## Recommendation

**FH1 `inset-ring`**, runner-up **FH4 `accent-bar`**.

FH1 keeps every gap exactly as it is unfocused, because nothing is drawn past the control. It is one style for
every control where today mixes an outer ring with fills that fail 3:1. It keeps today's 3 px cobalt and the
field's cobalt contour, follows hexagons and soft shapes alike, and passes WCAG 2.4.13 in both themes and looks
(at least 1.33×). FH4 also keeps every gap and passes, and reads like a tab or a Material field. It came
second because its bar changes thickness with the control (4 to 8 px), needs room under the text button's label
at large text, and on a chip it is easy to read as "selected". FH3 competes with the honey "picked" state, FH2
costs 4 px of air everywhere, and FH5's glow still reaches into the gaps.
