# Job 472b: large text wraps at full size, never shrunk (PR #745)

**Status.** Pushed to PR #745's branch as plain commits on top of `bd537f17` (the build, its done summary and the review-0 fix), without a rebase, so the legal files keep their hashes. The PR is still a draft and is not merged. Merging waits on Legal's GO.

**What changed for users.** At large text sizes (140 %, 200 %) More's rows, the Einstellungen rows, section labels and the reminder sheet render at the full requested size. A word too long for its line („Benachrichtigungen“, „Datenschutzerklärung“, „Nutzungsbedingungen“, „Schütteln“) breaks at a syllable and shows a hyphen. The hyphen and line break are written into the drawn text, because the web engine breaks at a soft hyphen without drawing a „-“ (review 0). Before, the row text shrank to fit (1.57×, 1.46× and 1.42× at 200 %). The reminder sheet's „Am Tag davor“ / „Am Einkaufstag“ choice and the appearance setting (System / Hell / Dunkel) show as stacked radio rows when their labels don't fit side by side at the chosen size. Before, they shrank. At 100 % nothing changes: the 100 % goldens are byte-identical.

**Where.**
- `app/lib/design/hyphenate.dart` holds the syllable rule and an exception table for the app's own compounds (Datenschutz-erklärung, Er-innerungen). The strings themselves are untouched.
- `app/lib/design/word_wrap.dart` defines `hyphenateToFit` and `HlWordWrapText`, and the wrapping app bar uses them. `wordFitScaler` is gone.
- `app/lib/design/section_label.dart` uses hyphenation and no longer cuts at two lines.
- `app/lib/design/hex_segmented.dart` gained `stackWhenTight`, and `hex_radio.dart`'s `HexRadioTile` gained `enabled`.
- `app/lib/settings/shopping_reminders.dart` and `settings_screen.dart` opt in, and the settings rows wrap.
- Decisions: below.

## Decisions

Moved from `docs/DECISIONS.md` (job 472e), where its heading was "Job 472b: large text wraps at full size, never shrunk (2026-11-26)". The numbers are the ones other docs cite as DECISIONS 472b.n.

Peter's rule (t1350u, 26 Nov): menu item titles WRAP at 2× text, no shrinking. This replaces 472.9's shrink and answers NEEDS_LEAD Job 472 (option b). Rendering and tests only: no ARB, generated l10n, policy, flag or migration byte changed.

1. **Explicit hyphen and line break at render time, only in words that do not fit.** `hyphenateToFit` (in `design/word_wrap.dart`) measures each word at the requested scale. A word wider than its line starts a new line and is cut at the last syllable (`design/hyphenate.dart`) whose part plus „-“ fits, followed by a real „-“ and `\n`, as often as needed. Words that fit are untouched and wrap between words. The first version left U+00AD soft hyphens to the engine: flutter_tester (the goldens) draws a „-“ at such a break, but review 0 measured that the release web build breaks there without drawing one („Datenschutzer“ / „klärung“). Breaking explicitly renders the same on every engine. `HlWordWrapText`, the wrapping app bar and `HlSectionLabel` use it; `wordFitScaler` is deleted. Screen readers get the plain label (`semanticsLabel`).
2. **A rule of thumb plus the app's own compounds.** No hyphenation package is in the app and adding one (pattern files, licence review) for a handful of long labels does not earn its place. The rule: vowel runs are nuclei, `sch`/`ch`/`ck`/`ph`/`th`/`sh`/`qu` count as one consonant, one consonant goes right, of several the last one or an onset pair (`bl` … `tr`, `schl` … `schw`) goes right, at least two letters either side, never next to a non-letter. Compound seams it misses („Schut-zer“, „Erin-ne“) are fixed for the app's own long words by a small exception table (Datenschutzerklärung, (Einkaufs)erinnerung(en)), split as the Duden does. Tested in `test/design/hyphenate_test.dart`.
3. **`HexSegmented` stacks instead of shrinking, opt-in.** With `stackWhenTight: true` a control whose widest label does not fit its share of the track at the full text size becomes one `HexRadioTile` per option, labels wrapping at full size, same `RadioGroup`, keys and semantics. SR1's „Am Tag davor / Am Einkaufstag“ and the settings theme control opt in (both already stack at 140 % on a 390 px phone; before, they shrank). The feedback annotator's tool bar and the price editor's basis toggle keep the old shrink: in the annotator four tool labels do not fit their quarter even at 100 %, and stacking would put four rows over the screenshot being marked up. `HexRadioTile` gained `enabled`.
4. **Settings rows wrap like More's.** Einstellungen's language and „Schütteln für Feedback“ rows and the language list now use `HlWordWrapText`; at 3.1× „Schütteln“ broke as „Schütte|ln“ without a hyphen. The trip-detection row comes from its own module and was not touched.
5. **`HlSectionLabel` lost `maxLines: 2` with its ellipsis.** A hyphenated „EINKAUFSERINNERUN-GEN“ needs two lines at 200 %, and a third would have been cut; the rule is never cut.
6. **Tests.** `expectFullScale` checks every non-icon text in More's rows, the SR1 section and sheet and Einstellungen renders at exactly the requested scale with no shrinking transform above it; `expectWordBreaks` now also allows the break after a hyphenated word's own „-“ and rejects any soft hyphen left in the text. On the old code the first fails at 1.57× (row) and 1.87× („EINKAUFSERINNERUNGEN“). Goldens at 390×844: More rows and page 100/140/200 % DE/EN light/dark; SR1 list and sheet 100/140/200 % DE/EN light plus DE dark. More tests that look labels up under the test font (whose square glyphs make „Datenschutzerklärung“ wider than a row even at 100 %) use `findLabel`, which also matches a hyphenated label by its screen-reader label.
7. **Keyboard in the stacked control.** The stacked options are `HexRadioTile`s, as in the language list and the store-chain picker: each row takes Tab focus and Space/Enter picks; the arrow keys do not move the pick, because the tile, not the radio, holds focus. That matches every other `HexRadioTile` list in the app rather than the track's arrow-key picking.
8. **The app bar title keeps Material's 1.34× cap** (`_titleMaxScale`). It is a page title, not a row, and Material caps it so a two-line title leaves room for the page; it now wraps and hyphenates at that size instead of shrinking further. A conscious exception to "no shrinking" for the lead to confirm with Peter.
