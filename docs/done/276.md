# Job 276: adds land in the list behind the sheet, the add's details, RI1 + RI2

Merged as: the next commit on main after this PR (see the PR). Flag: `add-feedback-list`
(PostHog, rollout off; `releases/flags.json`).

## What was built

All of it behind the new PostHog flag `add-feedback-list`. With the flag off nothing changes.

- **The list behind the open add sheet** drops its store and category headings. It shows the open
  items in walk order, then „Gerade hinzugefügt“ / "Just added" with this opening's adds. It
  scrolls so the last add stands just above the sheet, also after a partner's change. Its header
  scrolls away to leave room.
- **Closing glides back** into the stores and categories in 300 ms. Rows move from where they were
  painted to their places, headings fade in, and the list returns to its scroll position from
  before the opening. With reduced motion it switches at once. The glide moves paint only
  (`app/lib/list/list_view/glide.dart`): no row is laid out again per frame.
- **The added item's details** take the rows' room until the next letter: Menge with − and +, Marke
  and Eigenschaften (the edit sheet's `ItemDetailFields`, moved out of the edit sheet and reused).
  The sheet's chips set the added item's store and category meanwhile. A stepper or chip pick
  saves at once. Typing saves 500 ms after the last key, or at once with the next letter in the add
  field, another add, or closing (also while the sheet slides away). Saves go through
  `ListCubit.edit`, the edit sheet's path.
- **One sheet height while open** (from the first letter or add): rows, notes and details take
  the room of three rows and scroll inside it, so the sheet doesn't jump between details and
  suggestions.
- **RI2**: a 2 px looping line on the sheet's bottom edge, right above the field, from 150 ms into
  a request for suggestions until the answer. It stands still with reduced motion. Screen readers
  hear „Vorschläge laden“.
- **RI1**: without `input-stable-rows`, rows that still answer an earlier text are dimmed to 55 %
  while the answer for the new text loads. They still take a tap, and screen readers hear
  „älterer Vorschlag, lädt noch“. Rows made on the device are never dimmed, so with 272's flag on
  RI2 carries the feedback.

Decisions and reasons: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Job 276" and "Job 276 in-job review".

## How to verify

- Widget tests: `cd app && flutter test test/list/add_feedback_list_test.dart` (14 tests). They
  cover the add landing under „Gerade hinzugefügt“ above the sheet with the headings gone, the
  close glide (the painted offset moves halfway and ends at zero, then the grouped view), reduced
  motion, flag off, a partner's add keeping the end pinned, the details at an unchanged sheet
  height, and saving on a stepper tap, after the pause, on the next letter, on close and while the
  sheet slides away. They also cover RI1 dimming, RI2 after 150 ms of a request, full strength
  for device rows, and no line for a quick answer.
- Web: launch with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=add-feedback-list,input-stable-rows"`, then
  `node journeys/add-feedback-list.mjs <dir>` and `node journeys/add-feedback-glide.mjs <dir>`
  (verify-snacklist).
