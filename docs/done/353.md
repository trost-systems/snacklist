# Job 353: Snacklist without hexagons, round RD (2026-11-21)

This is a draft PR that must not be merged. It holds the hexagon inventory and one prototype round of ten
designs. Nothing in the app changed (Changelog: none).

## What is here

- `docs/design/redesign/HEX-INVENTORY.md`: every hexagon in the app, the icon, the store frames, the site and the
  emails, with file:line and how hard it is to replace. Every hex in Flutter goes through `HexBorder.pathFor`: 135
  direct call sites in 46 files, about 227 more through the theme.
- `docs/design/redesign/rd/`: the round page, `RECOMMENDATION.md` and `.de.md`, `measured.json`.
- Ten options plus Today, six frames each (list, tick, menu open, add sheet, settings, empty list), light and dark,
  100/200/310 % text, English and German. Mock data only (the fictional Brandt family).

## Pick

**RD2 Pill dock**: pills instead of hexagons; no tab bar; a round menu button beside the add field opens one sheet
with lists, History, Stores, Edit and Settings. 8 whole rows against Today's 7; History costs 2 taps. **Runner-up
RD4 One screen** (10 rows, menu at the top). Ship RD1's tokens-only swap first (RD-Q1). RD-Q1 to RD-Q10 in
`RECOMMENDATION.md`.
