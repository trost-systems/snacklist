# Job 327: paywall copy for CS1, the locks open it, manage or cancel from Mehr (2026-11-20)

Peter t1086u approved items 1, 2 and 5. PR: https://github.com/trost-systems/snacklist/pull/579

## What was built

- **Paywall copy (flag `paywall-cs1`, off).** The Premium page says Premium belongs to the buyer and works in every
  household they are in, and that the list, prices and shopping totals stay free. Benefits in pitch order: AI
  assistants (MCP), the API (while `public-api` is on), planned-vs-bought after a trip (while `trip-mode` is on), all
  trips in Verlauf (while `grocery-log` is on). Under the plans: „Premium gehört dann dir …“. The hexes show the
  benefits instead of three members. Nothing says prices, totals or barcodes are Premium; nothing says „unbegrenzt“.
  Flag off, the page is unchanged. Code: `app/lib/purchases/premium_screen.dart`.
- **The locks open the paywall (same flag).** Verlauf's older-trips row and the trip summary's „Was war geplant, was
  kam dazu?“ card always show their button and open the page over the screen (`showPaywall`,
  `app/lib/shell/router.dart`). ✕ returns to where the user was. Premium arriving while it is open (purchase, restore,
  another device) closes it by itself, and both locks are open then. The fresh summary used to lose its lines after a
  purchase (the server's save answer withholds them without Premium); it now keeps the device's lines
  (`app/lib/trip/trip_cubit.dart`), and a summary opened from Verlauf refetches (`summary_page.dart`).
- **Manage or cancel (no flag).** It existed only on the Premium page. Mehr now has „Abo verwalten oder kündigen“ under
  „Snacklist Premium“ whenever a yearly plan runs for the user (`Entitlements.yearlyRuns`). iOS opens StoreKit's
  subscriptions sheet, Android Play's page for `com.snacklist.app.yearly`, the web a dialog with both stores' pages.
  RevenueCat's `managementURL` is not used: it would start the SDK outside Buy and Restore (H-67).
