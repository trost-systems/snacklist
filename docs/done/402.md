# Job 402: #637 reworked for HP2, Legal N-366 and Peter's preview review (2026-11-22)

PR #637 (branch `job/369-align-site-store-videos`), rebased on main after job 401 (#648) switched
`grocery-log`, `prices-and-trip-total`, `trip-mode` and `total-priced-only` off for everyone. Not
merged: it waits for Legal's hash re-GO (NEEDS_LEAD "Job 402").

**Why the old captures showed prices.** `marketing/CAPTURE_FLAGS` only added flags. The
verify-snacklist build and `marketing/videos/remake.sh` also put every `rollout: "full"` flag of
`releases/flags.json` into `FLAGS_ON`, and three of the four HP2 flags were `full`.
`marketing/capture-flags.mjs` is now the one rule for every capture: full flags, minus
`marketing/CAPTURE_FLAGS_OFF` (the four HP2 flags), plus `CAPTURE_FLAGS`. Every store, hero and video
capture fails when the flag list compiled into its build disagrees, and the store and hero captures
also fail on a Verlauf tab or a price chip on screen (`marketing/launch-look.mjs`). Trip mode is no
longer assumed: the video shots answer the trip question only if it appears.

**Store listings** (`app/fastlane/metadata/`). Prices, totals, trip mode, History, "amounts paid"
and "log a trip" are gone from every field. The keywords `verlauf,preise` and `history,prices` are
gone too. The Alexa bullet now reads „Mit dem Snacklist-Skill für Alexa fügst du …“ / "With the
Snacklist skill for Alexa you can …". Each long description ends with N-366's trademark notice
(App Store: Siri and Amazon; Play: Amazon only). The free line is „Siri, Alexa und die Liste
selbst brauchen kein Premium.“ (Play has no Siri). `check.mjs` refuses HP2's words, "Amazon Alexa",
"Hey Alexa" and a missing notice. It is now part of the lint gate.

**Premium.** At launch Premium gates nothing a listing may name. History and the trip comparison
are off (HP2). AI assistants and the API need Premium only while `integration_lock` is on, and the
`integration-lock` hold is unreleased. Premium has no export (the account export is the GDPR copy),
and the app has no themes or user photos. So the Premium paragraph names the plans only: yearly or
Lifetime, with the same features. `docs/store/LISTING.md` holds the paste-ready lock-on block,
which leads with MCP and the API (P3a without export). `check.mjs` fails when the paragraph and the
hold disagree.

**Alexa is free in the launch build.** The newest `public.integration_gate`
(`supabase/migrations/20261113034530_siri_free.sql:17`) answers open for `'siri'` and `'alexa'`
before it reads the lock. The alexa function never calls the Premium gate
(`supabase/functions/alexa/`, the comment at `speech_en.ts:2`).

**Store screenshots, previews, videos, hero.** Re-made from a build with the HP2 flags off and
RD1, hide-categories and add-sheet-compact on. This covers every screenshot set and both feature
graphics and contact sheets (de-DE, en-US), the six App Store previews, and all 16 site videos with
their posters and captions (add-tick, household-sync, voice-add, mcp-chat). The hero's phone is now
a capture of the real web app (`site/scenes/capture-hero.sh`), not an HTML list with checkboxes.
Frames were checked by eye: no price chip, total, Verlauf tab, trip question or strip, no
checkboxes, and no Alexa logo, badge or Echo. Store videos show no Alexa.

**Site.**
- Every site button is a rounded rectangle with RD1's button radius (`--button-radius`, from
  `--hl-shape-soft-md`). Its 4 px edge is the only shadow. Chips, ticks and play keys keep their
  shapes.
- The launch stage (`SITE_STAGE=launch` for previews; the committed stage stays `waitlist`) has
  App Store and Google Play badges in the hero and the closing block. Under the badges is the
  line „Lieber im Browser? Snacklist im Browser öffnen“ to app.snacklist.app, and „Web-App“ stays
  in the nav. Launch pages have no waitlist, beta or November wording.
- An Alexa card sits next to Siri's in „Sag's einfach“. It is text only and links the skill page.
  `ALEXA_SKILL_URLS` in `site/config/stores.mjs` holds the skill pages, and `alexaPublished` in
  `site/config.json` is the toggle (default on).
- The build adds Amazon's trademark sentence to every page that names Alexa.
  `site/test/alexa.test.mjs` fails a page without it, "Hey Alexa", or an „Alexa, …“ sentence that
  the skill models do not take. The support pages and guides now use the N-366 naming and link the
  Skill Store.
