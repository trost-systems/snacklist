Peter picked AM2 „Outcome rows“ on 2026-11-20 (t1081u; prototype round job 323, #571). PR stacked on job 322's #573
(→ #570 → #569, Legal GO pending); it lands after them. It adds two UI strings, so it waits for Legal GO too.
Decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Job 324".

**What users get with the flag `add-match-rows` (off; on for Peter and the testers once the lead creates it; needs
`add-sheet-compact` on as well).**
- Typing something already on the shown list (the same name, a plural, another spelling or a synonym, whatever brand
  and details) shows marked rows in the sheet before the add: merge on blue with the merged amount („2× Kefir“), tick
  off on mint (name struck through), and for an item ticked lately a grey back-on-the-list row with its amount, store
  and details. At most three, then the `+` row right above the field.
- One tap each; a line says what happened until the next letter. No Undo.
- Enter (or the `+` row) with an open match adds a separate item, also an exact duplicate (Peter's AM-Q2). With only a
  ticked match, Enter puts it back.
- „Milch“ never offers „Hafermilch“; nothing behind the sheet scrolls or is highlighted; a tick from the sheet asks no
  trip question.
- The rows follow the list while you type: another member's delete, tick or add changes them without a key, and a row
  whose item went does nothing (review 0). Two items that would read alike get one merge row.
- Flag off: unchanged („… steht schon auf der Liste“).
