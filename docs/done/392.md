# Job 392: per-account Premium leftovers (Legal N-362 to N-365)

Report: Legal review of #639 (22 Nov 2026), items N-362 to N-365. Built on #639.

## What changed

- **N-362, account deletion.** The red Premium card in „Konto löschen?“ now shows only when the
  deleting account has Snacklist Premium itself. That means its own `user_entitlements` rows,
  written from RevenueCat's events and grants, judged by the existing rule (`unlocksOf`,
  `premiumAt`; the server's `user_premium()`). Read on open by `PremiumServer.myPremium()`. A
  failed read shows the fate error. The card has one text, `accountDeletePremium`, Legal's words
  in DE and EN, with no household and no archive sentence. `accountDeletePremiumLastMember` is
  gone. While the account's yearly plan renews, „Abo verwalten“ / "Manage subscription" opens
  StoreKit's sheet on iOS, else the App Store or Google Play subscriptions page. The refund link
  and the tick box stay, and Delete still waits for the tick. A household's Premium alone no
  longer shows the card, and the fate no longer calls `leaves_unlocked`.
- **N-363, support FAQ.** `site/content/legal/support.md` and `support-en.md` l.63 to 65 use
  Legal's heading and body word for word. The refund FAQ heading is now „Kann ich Snacklist
  Premium zurückgeben?“ / "Can I get a refund for Snacklist Premium?", and the two quotes of it
  (l.59, l.65) follow.
- **N-364.** `datenschutz-kaeufe.md` and `privacy-purchases.md` are deleted. Nothing linked them.
  `docs/PRIVACY_DATA_MAP.md` and the legal feature file no longer point at them.
- **N-365.** `lifetimeWeb` and `lifetimeUnlockedBody` are deleted from both ARBs.

  with „Abo verwalten“.
