# Job 322: the compact add sheet (AS1) and Undo as a plain delete again

Peter picked AS1 „Chip row“ on 2026-11-20 (prototype round: job 319, #568). PR stacked on job 321's #570, which sits on
job 313's #569 (Legal GO pending); it lands after both. Decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Job 322".

**What users get with the flag `add-sheet-compact` (off; on for Peter and the testers once the lead creates it).**
- After an add: no ✕, no „✓ Hinzugefügt“ note, no „Rückgängig“, no gap above the field. One row of chips under a slim
  grab handle: − Menge +, the store, the category and the details chip (brand and details shown on it; its field edits
  the details only). 132 px from the sheet's top to the keyboard in English (was 312).
- An open picker is one line that swipes sideways with the item's own option in view; one picker or the details field
  at a time. Every control stays above the keyboard up to 310 % text (390 × 844) and 200 % (360 × 640), also with
  long names (a chip takes at most half the row, its name cut with „…“; an option at most the line).
- The list behind the sheet is empty until the first add, then shows only the last two adds, without a heading.
- Closing: a tap outside the sheet (also on the rows behind it), the handle, a swipe down, Android back, Escape, Enter
  in the empty field.
- Duplicates are unchanged: „… steht schon auf der Liste“, „Zusammenführen“ and the merge's „Rückgängig“.

**For everyone.** „Rückgängig“ in the flag-off sheet deletes the item like any delete and keeps what its details taught
(job 315 reverted, Peter t1071u).
