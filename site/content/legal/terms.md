## 2. Snacklist and Snacklist Premium

Snacklist is free and has no ads. Everything you need for shopping is free, for example the shared list, prices and totals, shopping trips in the history (those of the last 4 weeks), Siri and Alexa.

Snacklist Premium is paid and also unlocks:

- the Snacklist API and connections with AI assistants that support the Model Context Protocol (MCP),
- insights into your shopping trips, for example what was planned and what was added,
- the full history instead of only the last 4 weeks,
- features that cost us money each time they are used, once they exist, for example reading receipts in the cloud, product images from the web or your own photos, with the limits in section 8.

What exactly is included is shown on the "Snacklist Premium" page in the app before you buy. Downloading your data (More › Account › Export my data) always stays free. AI assistants, Siri and Alexa are other providers' services with their own terms. You may need your own account there.

## 3. For you, in each of your households

Snacklist Premium belongs to the person who buys it and is tied to their Snacklist account. It is for your personal use and for the households you belong to. It applies in every household you are a member of, including households you join or create later. You can be a member of at most 20 households, with or without Premium. If you leave a household or it is deleted, you keep Premium. The other members of your households do not get Premium through your purchase. Premium can't be moved to another Snacklist account. Apple Family Sharing is not supported.

On a new device you restore a purchase with "Restore purchase" on the "Snacklist Premium" page. To do so, sign in with the same Snacklist account and use the same Apple or Google account as when you bought it.

## 5. Yearly plan

The yearly plan costs the price shown to you in the store before you buy. Your Apple or Google account is charged when you confirm the purchase and at every renewal. The plan renews automatically for one year at a time unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current year.

Only the person who took out the plan can cancel it, and only with Apple or Google. The app shows the way under More › Snacklist Premium › Manage or cancel subscription, and so does our [help page](/en/support). After you cancel, Premium stays until the end of the paid year. Deleting the app or your Snacklist account, or leaving a household, does not cancel the plan. Further cancellation rights follow Apple's or Google's terms and the law.

A new price applies to a running plan no earlier than the next renewal, and only in the way Apple or Google announce it in advance under their rules and, where those rules require it, ask for your consent. You can cancel at any time before the renewal.

## 6. When the yearly plan ends

If the yearly plan ends because it was not renewed or was refunded, Premium ends. We don't delete anything because of that: your households, lists, prices, shopping trips and the full history stay stored. The history in the app shows only the shopping trips of the last 4 weeks again; you see older trips and the insights again as soon as you have Premium again. Connections with AI assistants and API keys stay stored but only work again with Premium. You can keep using everything that is free (section 2), including downloading your data.

## 7. One-time purchase ("Lifetime")

The stores call the one-time purchase "Lifetime". It is paid once and does not renew.

"Lifetime" means: Premium applies to you for as long as your Snacklist account exists and as long as we run Snacklist. It does not mean that Premium lasts beyond the end of Snacklist or that features with running costs are included without limit (section 8). If you delete your Snacklist account, the one-time purchase ends too. Section 12 explains what happens if we shut Snacklist down.

We do not terminate the one-time purchase with notice. Both sides keep the right to terminate for good cause.

If you still have a yearly plan, it keeps running and being billed alongside the one-time purchase until you cancel it.

## 8. Limits on features with running costs

These limits apply to the Snacklist API: per API key up to 60 calls a minute, briefly up to 20 at once; per household up to 1,000 calls per calendar day (UTC), all keys together; and at most 10 active API keys per person. If you reach a limit, the API answers further calls only in the next period; everything else keeps working.

Features that cost us money each time they are used and that don't exist yet, for example reading receipts in the cloud, product images from the web or storing your own photos, we offer only once their limits are stated as numbers in these terms and on the "Snacklist Premium" page before you buy. Snacklist Premium, the one-time purchase included, does not include such features without limit.
