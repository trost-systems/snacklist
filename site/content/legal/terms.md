## 7. Usage limits for API and MCP

These limits keep Snacklist fast for everyone. They are also shown on the purchase screen before you buy.

- **Per API key:** You can send up to 20 requests in quick succession. After that, 60 requests per minute refill.
- **Per day:** 1,000 requests per user account. All API keys and all MCP connections of your account count together. The day follows UTC and starts at 00:00 UTC (in Germany at 01:00 or 02:00).
- **Per create request (POST):** at most 50 items.

The same daily limit applies to MCP. Every call your AI assistant makes to Snacklist counts as one request.

When it is reached, we reject further requests until the new day starts. Your data and your Premium are not affected.

Premium is meant for you personally. Please don't create extra accounts just to get around these limits. We don't lower the limits without your agreement.
