# AI assistants and your Snacklist plan

You can let an AI assistant read your shopping list and change it, for example to add items or tick them off. This page explains what it means when an assistant tells you that it needs a different Snacklist plan.

## What the assistant says

If your plan does not include this access, the assistant tells you: "This needs a different Snacklist plan". It has not read or changed anything. Your connection stays in place, so you do not need to sign in again after a change.

## What you can do

Nothing breaks in the meantime. Your list is unchanged, and you can keep using it in the app, with Siri and with Alexa. If you have questions about your plan, contact us (below).

## Siri, Alexa and the app

Siri, Alexa and the app itself work the same on every plan. You can add and tick off items by voice or in the app at any time.
